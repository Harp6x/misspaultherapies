## The Anger Iceberg

What people see: Anger (the tip)
What's underneath: Hurt, fear, shame, frustration, helplessness, grief, betrayal, exhaustion, feeling unheard

Anger is a secondary emotion — it protects you from more vulnerable feelings.

## The Anger Cycle

```
Trigger → Interpretation → Physical arousal → Impulse → Behaviour → Consequences
```

You can intervene at every stage:
- **Trigger:** Identify and prepare for your triggers
- **Interpretation:** Challenge the thought ("They did that on purpose" → "Maybe they didn't realise")
- **Physical arousal:** Use body-based calming (breathing, cold water, movement)
- **Impulse:** Delay response (count to 10, leave the room)
- **Behaviour:** Choose a response aligned with your values

## Levels of Anger

| Level | Feeling | Body Signs | Appropriate Response |
|-------|---------|------------|---------------------|
| 1-3 | Annoyed, irritated | Mild tension | Address it calmly or let it go |
| 4-6 | Frustrated, angry | Clenched jaw, raised voice | Take a break, use techniques |
| 7-8 | Furious | Racing heart, shaking | Remove yourself immediately |
| 9-10 | Rage | Loss of control, seeing red | STOP. Walk away. Do not engage. |

## Core Techniques

### 1. STOP Protocol
**S** — Stop what you're doing
**T** — Take 3 deep breaths
**O** — Observe what you're feeling in your body
**P** — Proceed mindfully (choose your response)

### 2. The 20-Minute Rule
When you're above a 7, your brain is in fight-or-flight. Rational thinking is offline. Walk away for at least 20 minutes. Not to avoid — to calm down enough to communicate effectively.

### 4. Assertive Communication
- **Aggressive:** "You're such an idiot!"
- **Passive:** Saying nothing, seething inside
- **Assertive:** "I feel frustrated when X happens. I'd like Y instead."

## When Anger Is a Problem

- Physical aggression (hitting, throwing, breaking things)
- Verbal aggression (yelling, threats, name-calling)
- Road rage
- Others are afraid of you
- Anger lasts for hours/days
- You regret what you say/do when angry
- Relationships are damaged by your anger
- Substances used to manage anger

**If any of these apply → anger management therapy is strongly recommended.**
