## The Problem

Average Indian spends 7+ hours daily on screens. Social media is designed to be addictive — variable rewards, infinite scroll, social comparison. The impact: anxiety, depression, insomnia, attention fragmentation, loneliness, and reduced real-world connection.

## Signs You Need a Digital Detox
- First thing you do is check your phone (before even getting out of bed)
- Phantom vibrations (feeling your phone buzz when it didn't)
- Anxiety when separated from your phone
- Comparing your life to others' curated feeds
- Doom scrolling (can't stop consuming negative content)
- Spending 3+ hours daily on non-work screen time
- Sleep disrupted by late-night scrolling
- Real conversations feel less satisfying than online ones

## The 7-Day Digital Detox Plan

### Day 1-2: Awareness
- Track your screen time (use built-in tracking)
- Note how you feel before and after each session
- Identify your trigger: Boredom? Anxiety? FOMO? Habit?

### Day 3-4: Boundaries
- No phone in bedroom (buy an alarm clock)
- No phone during meals
- 1-hour daily "phone-free" block
- Turn off non-essential notifications

### Day 5-6: Replacement
- Replace scrolling with: reading, walking, calling a friend, journaling
- Replace news consumption with: one 10-minute briefing per day
- Replace social media with: real-world social plans

### Day 7: Reflection
- What did you notice about your mood, sleep, attention?
- What habits will you maintain going forward?
- What was hardest? What was surprisingly easy?

## Long-Term Digital Wellness

- **Morning:** No phone for first 30 minutes after waking
- **During work:** Phone in another room during focus blocks
- **Evening:** No screens 1 hour before bed
- **Social media:** Set daily time limits (30-60 minutes)
- **Weekly:** One screen-free evening or morning
