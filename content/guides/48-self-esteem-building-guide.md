## What Is Self-Esteem?

Self-esteem is your overall sense of personal value and worth. It's not confidence (which is skill-specific) — it's the fundamental belief that you matter and deserve good things.

## Low Self-Esteem Patterns
- Harsh inner critic that's never satisfied
- Difficulty accepting compliments
- Comparing yourself unfavourably to others
- Apologising for existing
- Staying in situations below your worth (jobs, relationships)
- Not speaking up for fear of being "wrong"
- Achievement-dependent worth ("I'm only valuable when I succeed")

## Where Low Self-Esteem Comes From
- Critical or neglectful parenting
- Bullying (childhood or adult)
- Abusive relationships
- Academic/career "failures"
- Cultural messages (especially about appearance, gender, caste)
- Unprocessed trauma

## Rebuilding Self-Esteem

### 1. The Inner Critic Audit
For one week, notice every self-critical thought. Write them down. Ask:
- "Whose voice is this?" (parent? teacher? bully?)
- "Would I say this to a friend?"
- "Is this fact or opinion?"

### 2. Evidence Collection
Create a "worthiness file" with evidence that contradicts your inner critic:
- Things you've accomplished
- Kind things people have said about you
- Challenges you've overcome
- Skills you have
- People who love you

### 3. Values-Based Worth
Shift from achievement-based worth to values-based worth:
- "I am worthy because of WHO I am, not what I DO"
- Identify your core values (kindness, creativity, integrity, courage)
- Live according to those values daily

### 4. Boundary Practice
Every time you set a boundary, you tell yourself: "My needs matter."

### 5. Body-Based Practices
- Power posing (2 minutes, expansive posture)
- Exercise (increases self-efficacy)
- Self-care rituals (treating your body with kindness)

### 6. Stop Comparing
- Limit social media
- "Comparison is the thief of joy"
- Your journey is YOUR journey

## Daily Self-Esteem Practice

| Morning | "One thing I appreciate about myself today:" |
|---------|---------------------------------------------|
| Midday | "One boundary I held or need I expressed:" |
| Evening | "One thing I did well today:" |
