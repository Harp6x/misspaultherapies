## Why Couples Fight

Conflict is inevitable in relationships. Research by Dr. John Gottman shows that 69% of relationship problems are perpetual — they never fully resolve. The goal isn't to eliminate conflict but to manage it constructively.

## The Four Horsemen of Relationship Apocalypse (Gottman)

| Horseman | What It Looks Like | Antidote |
|----------|-------------------|----------|
| **Criticism** | "You never help around the house" | Gentle startup: "I feel overwhelmed. Can we divide chores?" |
| **Contempt** | Eye-rolling, sarcasm, mockery | Build culture of appreciation: "I noticed you..." |
| **Defensiveness** | "It's not my fault!" | Take responsibility: "You're right, I could have..." |
| **Stonewalling** | Shutting down, walking away mid-argument | Self-soothe: "I need 20 minutes to calm down. I'll come back." |

## The Soft Startup Formula

Instead of: "You NEVER listen to me!"
Try: **"I feel [emotion] about [specific situation]. I need [specific request]."**

Example: "I feel unheard when I'm talking and you're on your phone. I need you to put it down when we're having a conversation."

## The Repair Checklist

When conflict escalates, use repairs:
- "Can we take a break and come back to this?"
- "I'm sorry. That came out wrong."
- "I can see your point."
- "What do you need from me right now?"
- "We're on the same team."
- "I love you even when we disagree."

## Fair Fighting Rules

1. One issue at a time (no kitchen-sinking)
2. No name-calling or insults
3. Use "I" statements, not "You" accusations
4. Take breaks if either person is flooded (20+ minutes)
5. No involving parents or third parties during the fight
6. Don't threaten divorce/breakup during arguments
7. Resolve within 24 hours (don't let resentment build)

## Indian Relationship Context

- In-law dynamics creating couple conflict
- Gender role expectations
- Financial disagreements (joint family contributions)
- Intimacy discussions considered taboo
- "Adjusting" expected more from one partner (usually the wife)
- Comparison with siblings' marriages

## When to Seek Couples Therapy

- Same fights recurring without resolution
- Emotional distance or disconnection
- Contempt is present (the strongest predictor of divorce)
- Communication has broken down
- Infidelity or breach of trust
- Major life transition creating strain
