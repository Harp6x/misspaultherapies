## What OCD Really Is

OCD involves two components:
- **Obsessions:** Unwanted, intrusive thoughts, images, or urges that cause distress
- **Compulsions:** Repetitive behaviours or mental acts performed to reduce that distress

The cycle: Obsession → Anxiety → Compulsion → Temporary relief → Obsession returns stronger

## Common OCD Subtypes

### Contamination OCD
- Fear of germs, dirt, chemicals, illness
- Compulsions: Excessive washing, cleaning, avoiding "contaminated" objects

### Harm OCD
- Intrusive thoughts about harming yourself or others (despite having NO desire to)
- Compulsions: Checking, reassurance-seeking, avoiding sharp objects or being alone with people

### Symmetry/Ordering OCD
- Need for things to be "just right," symmetrical, or in a specific order
- Compulsions: Arranging, counting, repeating actions until it "feels right"

### Scrupulosity (Religious/Moral OCD)
- Obsessive fear of sinning, being immoral, or offending God
- Compulsions: Excessive prayer, confession, ritualistic behaviour
- **Especially relevant in Indian context** where religious rituals may mask OCD

### Relationship OCD (ROCD)
- Constant doubt about your relationship ("Do I really love them?")
- Compulsions: Testing feelings, comparing to other couples, seeking reassurance

### Pure O (Primarily Obsessional)
- Intrusive thoughts with mental compulsions (no visible rituals)
- Themes: Harm, sexual, religious, identity-related
- Often the most shame-inducing and least understood

## How OCD Is Different From...

| Condition | Key Difference |
|-----------|---------------|
| Normal worry | OCD thoughts are ego-dystonic (against your values) |
| Anxiety | OCD has specific obsession-compulsion cycles |
| Perfectionism | OCD involves distress and loss of control |
| Superstition | OCD sufferers know their fears are irrational but can't stop |

## The Gold Standard Treatment: ERP

**Exposure and Response Prevention** involves:
1. Deliberately exposing yourself to the obsessive trigger
2. Resisting the urge to perform the compulsion
3. Learning that anxiety decreases naturally without the ritual

Example: If you fear contamination from doorknobs:
- Touch the doorknob (exposure)
- Don't wash your hands for 30 minutes (response prevention)
- Notice: anxiety rises, peaks, then naturally falls

## What Doesn't Help
- Reassurance from others (temporarily helps, long-term worsens)
- Avoidance (life shrinks, OCD grows)
- Thought suppression ("Don't think about it" makes it worse)
- Giving in to compulsions (reinforces the cycle)

## OCD in the Indian Context
- Religious rituals may mask compulsions (excessive pooja, fasting, purification)
- "Superstitious" behaviours may actually be OCD
- Family members often accommodate (participate in rituals to reduce sufferer's distress)
- Contamination OCD surged post-COVID
- High shame around sexual or harm-themed intrusive thoughts

## Recovery Is Possible

With proper ERP therapy, 60–80% of people with OCD experience significant improvement. OCD is highly treatable — but it requires the RIGHT treatment.
