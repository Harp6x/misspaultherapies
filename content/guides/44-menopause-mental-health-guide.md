## Beyond Hot Flashes

Menopause is a major life transition affecting every system in your body — including your brain. Oestrogen plays a crucial role in mood regulation, sleep, and cognition. When it drops, psychological symptoms can be significant.

## Mental Health Symptoms of Menopause
- Mood swings and irritability
- Anxiety (new onset or worsening)
- Depression (especially during perimenopause)
- Brain fog (difficulty concentrating, memory lapses)
- Sleep disruption
- Loss of confidence
- Decreased libido and body image distress
- Rage episodes (peri-menopausal rage is real)
- Panic attacks (new onset)
- Feeling of losing yourself

## Why It's Missed
- Symptoms attributed to "just stress" or "ageing"
- GPs may not connect mood symptoms to hormonal changes
- Average age of menopause in India: 46-48 (earlier than Western data)
- Cultural silence: "Women just deal with it"
- Mental health symptoms start in perimenopause (can be 10 years before menopause)

## What Helps

### Medical
- Discuss HRT (Hormone Replacement Therapy) with your gynaecologist
- Screen for thyroid issues (symptoms overlap)
- Review medications that may worsen mood

### Psychological
- CBT for menopausal symptoms (evidence-based)
- Mindfulness and relaxation for hot flashes and anxiety
- Self-compassion practice
- Therapy for grief about ageing, fertility ending, identity shift

### Lifestyle
- Regular exercise (strength training + cardio)
- Sleep hygiene (crucial during menopause)
- Balanced nutrition (calcium, vitamin D, omega-3)
- Social connection
- Limiting alcohol and caffeine (worsen hot flashes and mood)

### Identity Work
- Menopause is an ending AND a beginning
- Many women report post-menopause as their most authentic, empowered phase
- Freedom from periods, pregnancy worry, and people-pleasing
- Time to invest in yourself

## For Partners
- Educate yourself
- Don't dismiss symptoms ("You're just hormonal")
- Be patient with mood changes
- Support her healthcare decisions
- Maintain physical and emotional intimacy
