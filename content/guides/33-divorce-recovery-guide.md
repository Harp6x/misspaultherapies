## The Grief of Divorce

Divorce is the death of a future you planned. Even if the decision was right, grief is expected and necessary.

## Stages of Divorce Recovery

### 1. Shock & Denial (Weeks 1-4)
- Disbelief, numbness, autopilot mode
- What helps: Basic survival — eat, sleep, show up. Tell trusted people.

### 2. Anger & Bargaining (Months 1-6)
- Rage at your ex, at yourself, at the situation
- "What if I had done X differently?"
- What helps: Channel anger into physical activity. Journal. Therapy.

### 3. Depression & Grief (Months 3-12)
- Deep sadness, loneliness, questioning self-worth
- Hardest phase — especially on anniversaries, festivals, quiet evenings
- What helps: Allow the grief. Don't rush. Connect with supportive people.

### 4. Acceptance & Rebuilding (Months 6-18+)
- Making peace with what happened
- Rediscovering who you are outside the marriage
- Building new routines, relationships, and goals
- What helps: Intentional identity work. New experiences. Therapy.

## Divorce in India — The Extra Layer

- Social stigma (especially for women)
- "Divorcee" label in matrimonial culture
- Family pressure to reconcile regardless of toxicity
- Legal complexity (especially property, custody)
- Financial vulnerability if economically dependent
- Children used as pawns or messengers

## Practical Recovery Steps

### Immediately
- Secure finances (separate accounts, understand assets)
- Get legal counsel
- Build a support team (friend, therapist, lawyer)
- Protect your children from adult conflict

### Short-Term
- Establish YOUR routine (not the one you shared)
- Process emotions (don't numb with alcohol, rebounds, or overwork)
- Limit social media exposure to your ex
- Set boundaries with well-meaning but unhelpful relatives

### Long-Term
- Rediscover your identity (hobbies, interests, goals)
- Address patterns that contributed to the relationship breakdown
- When ready: heal before dating again
- Co-parenting requires civility, not friendship

## When to Seek Help
- Persistent depression beyond 6 months
- Substance use to cope
- Inability to function at work or home
- Thoughts of self-harm
- Difficulty co-parenting
