## Before You Say "I Do"

Whether your marriage is love or arranged, these conversations are essential. Most couples discuss the wedding but not the marriage.

## The 10 Essential Pre-Marriage Conversations

### 1. Expectations
- What does a "good marriage" look like to you?
- What did you learn from your parents' marriage (to keep or change)?
- How will decision-making work?

### 2. Finances
- How will money be managed? Joint or separate accounts?
- Debt disclosure (both partners)
- Financial support to parents — how much, how long?
- Spending habits and financial goals

### 3. Family Dynamics
- Where will you live? (With in-laws or independently?)
- How will in-law relationships be managed?
- Boundaries with extended family
- Festival and holiday expectations

### 4. Children
- Do you want children? How many? When?
- Parenting style and values
- What if one partner changes their mind?
- Division of childcare responsibilities

### 5. Career & Ambitions
- Both partners' career goals
- Relocation willingness
- What if one partner earns significantly more?
- Support during career transitions

### 6. Intimacy & Affection
- Physical and emotional intimacy expectations
- Love languages (how each person feels loved)
- Discussing sexual health openly
- What happens during dry spells

### 7. Conflict Resolution
- How will you handle arguments?
- What's off-limits during fights? (name-calling, threats, stonewalling)
- When will you seek outside help?
- Cool-down agreements

### 8. Household Management
- Division of chores (equitable, not "helping")
- Cooking, cleaning, laundry — who does what?
- Hiring help if possible and agreeable

### 9. Individual Identity
- Maintaining personal friendships
- Solo time and hobbies
- Supporting each other's growth even when it's uncomfortable

### 10. Deal-Breakers
- What is absolutely non-negotiable for you?
- Infidelity, substance abuse, violence, disrespect — define boundaries
- When would you consider ending the marriage?

## Pre-Marital Therapy Is Not a Red Flag

It's the smartest investment you can make. A few sessions before marriage can prevent years of conflict after.
