## The Mental Health Impact of Being LGBTQ+ in India

LGBTQ+ individuals face higher rates of depression, anxiety, suicidal ideation, and substance use — not because of their identity, but because of societal stigma, discrimination, and minority stress.

## Common Challenges
- Coming out (or the burden of staying closeted)
- Family rejection or conditional acceptance
- Conversion therapy pressure (harmful and unethical)
- Workplace discrimination
- Internalized homophobia/transphobia
- Lack of legal protections (despite Section 377 decriminalisation)
- Marriage and partnership recognition issues
- Healthcare access and provider sensitivity

## Minority Stress Model

LGBTQ+ individuals experience unique stressors:
1. **External:** Discrimination, harassment, violence, microaggressions
2. **Internal:** Internalised shame, expectation of rejection, concealment stress
3. **Cumulative:** Daily navigating of a world not designed for you

## What Affirming Therapy Looks Like
- Your identity is NOT the problem to be fixed
- Therapist uses correct pronouns and names
- No attempt to change sexual orientation or gender identity
- Focus on the impact of stigma, not on "fixing" you
- Support for coming out process (at YOUR pace)
- Family work when needed
- Trauma processing related to identity-based harm

## For Families
- Your child's identity is not your failure
- Educate yourself before reacting
- Family rejection is the #1 risk factor for LGBTQ+ youth suicide
- Acceptance literally saves lives
- You can hold traditional values AND love your child unconditionally
- Family therapy can bridge the gap
