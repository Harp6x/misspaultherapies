## The State of Workplace Mental Health in India

- 80% of Indian workers report workplace stress
- "Hustle culture" glorifies overwork
- Boundaries between work and personal life are blurred (especially WFH)
- Mental health stigma prevents disclosure at work
- Limited organisational support in most Indian companies

## Common Workplace Mental Health Issues

### Burnout
Not just tiredness — emotional exhaustion, cynicism, and reduced effectiveness. Three dimensions: exhaustion, depersonalisation, reduced accomplishment.

### Imposter Syndrome
Feeling like a fraud despite evidence of competence. Especially common for women, first-generation professionals, and those in new roles.

### Workplace Anxiety
Performance anxiety, fear of conflict, email dread, meeting anxiety, deadline panic.

### Toxic Work Culture
Micromanagement, bullying, discrimination, unrealistic expectations, blame culture.

## Strategies for Individuals

### Boundary Setting
- Define work hours and communicate them
- "I'll respond to that first thing tomorrow"
- Turn off notifications after work
- Lunch breaks are non-negotiable (eat away from your desk)
- Use your leave days

### Stress Management at Work
- 5-minute breathing break every 2 hours
- Walk after lunch (even around the office)
- The "worry window" — schedule 15 minutes for worrying, not all day
- Prioritise ruthlessly: What MUST happen today?

### Communication
- "I can take this on, but I'll need to deprioritise X. Which is more important?"
- "I'm struggling with the workload. Can we discuss priorities?"
- "I need a mental health day" (you don't need to specify the reason)

### Career Decisions
- A job is not worth your mental health
- Start building an exit plan if the environment is toxic
- Therapy helps you make career decisions from clarity, not panic

## Know Your Rights
- POSH Act (Prevention of Sexual Harassment)
- Mental health as legitimate sick leave reason
- Reasonable accommodations for diagnosed conditions
- Right to a safe and healthy work environment
