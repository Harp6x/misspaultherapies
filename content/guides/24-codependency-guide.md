## What Is Codependency?

Codependency is an excessive reliance on another person for your sense of identity, self-worth, and emotional regulation. It often involves caretaking at the expense of your own needs.

## Signs of Codependency
- Your mood depends on the other person's mood
- You neglect your own needs to care for others
- You feel responsible for other people's feelings and problems
- You have difficulty saying no or setting boundaries
- You stay in unhealthy relationships out of obligation or fear
- Your self-esteem depends on being needed
- You enable destructive behaviour (making excuses, covering up, rescuing)
- You feel guilty when you do something for yourself
- You lose yourself in relationships

## The Codependency Cycle

```
Other person has a problem → You take responsibility → You sacrifice your needs
→ Resentment builds → You feel unappreciated → You sacrifice more (hoping for recognition)
→ Exhaustion/burnout → The cycle continues
```

## Recovery Steps

### 1. Recognise the Pattern
- Where did you learn that love = sacrifice?
- Whose emotions were you responsible for as a child?
- Do you know what YOU want (independent of others)?

### 2. Develop Self-Awareness
- Practice checking in with yourself: "What am I feeling right now?"
- Identify your needs before asking about others'
- Notice when you're caretaking out of fear vs. genuine desire

### 3. Set Boundaries
- "I care about you AND I need to take care of myself"
- "I can't fix this for you, but I'll support you while you work on it"
- "I'm not available for that right now"

### 4. Allow Others to Struggle
- Rescuing prevents growth
- You cannot control other people's choices
- Helping vs. enabling: helping empowers; enabling maintains the problem

### 5. Build Your Own Identity
- What are YOUR interests, values, goals?
- Spend time alone (learn that it's okay)
- Develop friendships outside your primary relationship
- Invest in your own growth

## Codependency in Indian Culture
- "Sacrificing for family" is culturally celebrated
- Women especially expected to be self-sacrificing (wife, mother, daughter-in-law)
- Enmeshed family systems normalise lack of boundaries
- "Adjust" is the most common advice for relationship problems
- Codependency with parents can last well into adulthood
