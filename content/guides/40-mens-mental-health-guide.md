# Men's Mental Health — Breaking the Silence
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

---

## The Silent Crisis

Men die by suicide at 3-4x the rate of women. Yet men are far less likely to seek mental health support. The reason isn't that men don't struggle — it's that they've been taught not to show it.

## Why Men Don't Seek Help
- "Men don't cry" / "Man up" messaging from childhood
- Emotional expression seen as weakness
- Provider role pressure ("I can't afford to fall apart")
- Stigma amplified in Indian culture (men as stoic pillars)
- Difficulty identifying emotions (alexithymia)
- Lack of close friendships with emotional depth
- Alcohol as the socially acceptable "coping" mechanism

## How Depression Looks Different in Men
- Irritability and anger (rather than sadness)
- Risk-taking behaviour
- Workaholism (staying busy to avoid feelings)
- Physical complaints (back pain, headaches, digestive issues)
- Substance use increase
- Social withdrawal disguised as "being busy"
- Aggression or controlling behaviour

## Common Issues Men Face
- Performance pressure (career, earnings, provider role)
- Relationship difficulties (inability to communicate emotions)
- Fatherhood transition
- Sexual health concerns (ED, performance anxiety)
- Identity crisis (retirement, job loss, ageing)
- Loneliness (especially after 40)
- Unprocessed childhood trauma

## What Helps

### 1. Redefine Strength
True strength isn't silence — it's the courage to be vulnerable. Asking for help takes more bravery than suffering alone.

### 2. Find Your Language
You don't have to use therapy-speak. "I'm stressed," "I'm not coping," "Something's off" — all valid entry points.

### 3. Physical First
Exercise is not a substitute for therapy but it's a powerful complement. Men often access emotions through the body first.

### 4. Build One Deep Connection
You don't need to bare your soul to everyone. Find ONE person you can be honest with.

### 5. Therapy
Male-friendly therapy focuses on:
- Problem-solving and practical tools
- Goal-oriented approaches
- Skills you can use immediately
- Confidential, non-judgmental space

## For Partners of Men Who Are Struggling
- Don't push ("Talk to me!") — create space and wait
- Normalise help-seeking ("I saw a therapist and it helped me")
- Suggest action-oriented framing ("Would you try 3 sessions and see?")
- Don't take their withdrawal personally
