## The Science of Habit Formation

Habits follow a loop: **Cue → Routine → Reward.** Change any part of the loop to build new habits or break old ones. Research shows habits take 21–66 days to form (not 21 days as commonly believed).

## The 5 Non-Negotiable Mental Health Habits

### 1. Sleep (7-9 hours)
- Consistent sleep/wake time (even weekends)
- No screens 30 min before bed
- Cool, dark, quiet room
- Sleep is the #1 foundation for mental health

### 2. Movement (30 min most days)
- Doesn't need to be a gym — walking counts
- Exercise is as effective as antidepressants for mild depression
- Find something you enjoy (sustainability > intensity)

### 3. Nutrition
- Regular meals (don't skip)
- Minimise sugar and processed food (inflammation affects mood)
- Stay hydrated
- Limit caffeine after 2 PM and alcohol

### 4. Connection (Daily)
- One meaningful interaction per day (even a text)
- Quality > quantity
- Be present when with people (phones away)

### 5. Stillness (10 min)
- Meditation, deep breathing, journaling, or simply sitting
- Your nervous system needs decompression time
- Not optional — essential

## Building Habits That Stick

### Start Tiny
Not "meditate 30 minutes daily" but "meditate 2 minutes after morning coffee"

### Stack Habits
Attach new habit to existing one: "After I brush my teeth, I will write 3 gratitudes"

### Track Visually
Use a calendar. Mark each day you complete the habit. Don't break the chain.

### Expect Setbacks
Missing one day doesn't erase your progress. Never miss twice in a row.

### Reward Yourself
Small, immediate rewards reinforce habit loops.
