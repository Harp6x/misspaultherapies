## Why Sleep Matters for Mental Health

Sleep is not a luxury — it is a **biological necessity**. During sleep, your brain:
- Consolidates memories and learning
- Processes emotional experiences
- Clears metabolic waste products
- Restores neurotransmitter balance

**Poor sleep** is both a symptom and a cause of mental health issues. Improving sleep often improves anxiety, depression, and emotional regulation significantly.

## Part 2: The Sleep Environment

### Temperature
- Ideal bedroom temperature: **18–20°C (65–68°F)**
- Your body needs to cool down to initiate sleep
- Use breathable cotton sheets; avoid heavy synthetic blankets in warm weather

### Light
- Complete darkness is ideal — use blackout curtains
- If not possible, use a sleep mask
- Remove or cover LED lights from devices
- Use warm, dim lighting in the hour before bed (avoid white/blue light)

### Sound
- If you need background noise, use white noise or nature sounds
- Avoid falling asleep to TV or podcasts with narrative content
- Earplugs can help in noisy environments

### Your Bed
- Use your bed ONLY for sleep
- If you work from home, never work from bed
- This trains your brain: bed = sleep

## Part 3: The Pre-Sleep Routine

Start this routine 45–60 minutes before your target sleep time:

### The Wind-Down Sequence

| Time Before Bed | Action |
|----------------|--------|
| 60 min | Stop all work. Dim the lights. |
| 45 min | Screens off. Put phone on Do Not Disturb in another room. |
| 30 min | Warm shower or bath (the cooling effect after promotes sleepiness). |
| 20 min | Gentle stretching, reading (paper book), or journaling. |
| 10 min | 4-7-8 breathing or body scan meditation. |
| 0 min | Lights off. Eyes closed. |

### The Worry Dump

If your mind races at bedtime:
1. Keep a notepad beside your bed
2. Spend 5 minutes writing down everything on your mind
3. Write tomorrow's to-do list
4. Close the notebook and tell yourself: "These will be here tomorrow. Tonight, I rest."

## Part 4: When You Can't Sleep

### The 20-Minute Rule
- If you haven't fallen asleep within 20 minutes, **get out of bed**
- Go to another room
- Do something boring and calm (fold laundry, read something dull)
- Return to bed only when you feel sleepy
- This prevents your brain from associating bed with frustration

### Racing Thoughts at 3am
- Don't check the time (it creates "time anxiety")
- Try the **cognitive shuffle**: pick a random letter and think of unrelated words starting with that letter (Apple, Antelope, Airplane...). This occupies the analytical mind and bores it to sleep.
- Body scan: slowly mentally scan from your toes to your head, noticing each body part relaxing

### What NOT to Do
- ❌ Don't check your phone
- ❌ Don't calculate how many hours of sleep you'll get
- ❌ Don't try harder to sleep (effort is the enemy of sleep)
- ❌ Don't drink alcohol to fall asleep (it disrupts sleep quality)

## Part 5: Common Sleep Disruptors

### Caffeine
- Half-life is 5–6 hours (a 3pm coffee is still in your system at 9pm)
- Cut off time: **2pm** at the latest
- Remember: tea, chocolate, and some medications contain caffeine too

### Alcohol
- May help you fall asleep but dramatically reduces sleep quality
- Suppresses REM sleep (the restorative phase)
- Causes middle-of-the-night waking

### Screens
- Blue light suppresses melatonin production
- Content stimulates the brain (news, social media, messaging)
- Use Night Shift mode if you must use devices, but screen-free is best

### Naps
- If you nap, limit to **20 minutes** before **2pm**
- Long or late naps steal from nighttime sleep
- If you're struggling with nighttime sleep, eliminate naps entirely

## Part 6: Your 2-Week Sleep Reset Plan

### Week 1: Foundation
- [ ] Set a consistent wake time (same every day, including weekends)
- [ ] Create a screen-free zone 30 minutes before bed
- [ ] Move caffeine cut-off to 2pm
- [ ] Do the Sleep Hygiene Audit and fix your environment

### Week 2: Optimise
- [ ] Implement the full Wind-Down Sequence
- [ ] Practice the Worry Dump every night
- [ ] Use the 20-minute rule if you can't sleep
- [ ] Track your sleep with a simple journal (time to bed, time asleep, wake time, quality 1–10)
