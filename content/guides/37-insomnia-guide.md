## Types of Insomnia
- **Onset insomnia:** Difficulty falling asleep (lying awake 30+ minutes)
- **Maintenance insomnia:** Waking up during the night and can't get back to sleep
- **Early morning insomnia:** Waking too early and can't return to sleep
- **Acute insomnia:** Short-term (stress, travel, life events)
- **Chronic insomnia:** 3+ nights per week for 3+ months

## The Insomnia Cycle
```
Poor sleep → Worry about sleep → Try harder to sleep → More awake
→ Frustration → Daytime fatigue → Napping/caffeine → Worse sleep
```

## CBT for Insomnia (CBT-I) — The Gold Standard

### 1. Sleep Restriction
Paradoxically, spending LESS time in bed improves sleep quality:
- Calculate your average sleep time (e.g., 5 hours)
- Set bedtime to 5 hours before wake time (e.g., 1 AM–6 AM)
- Gradually extend by 15 minutes as sleep efficiency improves

### 2. Stimulus Control
- Bed = sleep and intimacy ONLY (no phone, TV, work, worry)
- If not asleep in 20 minutes, get up and do something boring
- Return to bed only when sleepy
- Same wake time every day (including weekends)

### 3. Sleep Hygiene
- No caffeine after 2 PM
- No alcohol within 3 hours of bedtime
- Cool, dark, quiet bedroom
- No screens 30 minutes before bed
- Regular exercise (but not within 2 hours of bedtime)
- Consistent pre-sleep routine

### 4. Cognitive Restructuring
Challenge sleep-related thoughts:
- "I need 8 hours or I can't function" → "I can manage on less; one bad night won't ruin me"
- "I'll never sleep again" → "Insomnia is temporary and treatable"
- "I have to fall asleep RIGHT NOW" → "Sleep will come. Resting is also valuable"

### 5. Relaxation Training
- Progressive Muscle Relaxation before bed
- Body scan meditation
- 4-7-8 breathing (inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8)
