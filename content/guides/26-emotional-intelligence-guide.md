## The Five Components of EQ (Goleman)

### 1. Self-Awareness
Recognising your emotions as they happen. "I am feeling angry right now."

**Practice:** Three times daily, pause and ask: "What am I feeling right now?" Name it specifically (not just "bad" — frustrated? disappointed? anxious?).

### 2. Self-Regulation
Managing emotions so they don't manage you. Feeling angry is okay. Acting on anger impulsively is the problem.

**Practice:** When triggered, use the 6-second rule — the neurochemical lifespan of an emotion is 6 seconds. Breathe through it before responding.

### 3. Motivation
Internal drive beyond external rewards. Pursuing goals because they matter to you.

**Practice:** Connect daily tasks to your deeper values. "I'm doing this because..." 

### 4. Empathy
Understanding others' emotions without necessarily agreeing. "I can see why you feel that way."

**Practice:** In conversations, listen to understand (not to respond). Reflect back: "It sounds like you're feeling..."

### 5. Social Skills
Navigating relationships effectively. Communication, conflict resolution, collaboration.

**Practice:** One difficult conversation per week using "I feel..." language.

## Emotions Wheel (Expanded)

Instead of "I feel bad," try:
- **Angry:** Frustrated, irritated, resentful, furious, betrayed
- **Sad:** Disappointed, lonely, grieving, helpless, empty
- **Anxious:** Worried, nervous, overwhelmed, panicky, restless
- **Ashamed:** Embarrassed, guilty, humiliated, inadequate
- **Happy:** Content, grateful, proud, excited, peaceful, relieved
- **Fearful:** Scared, insecure, vulnerable, threatened

## EQ in Indian Professional Context
- Managing emotions in hierarchical workplaces
- Navigating family dynamics with empathy and boundaries
- Cultural code-switching (traditional vs modern contexts)
- Handling criticism without taking it personally
- Leading teams with emotional awareness

## Daily EQ Practice

| Time | Practice | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Morning | Emotion check-in | 1 minute |
| During interactions | Active listening + empathy | Ongoing |
| After conflict | Reflect: "What was I really feeling?" | 5 minutes |
| Evening | Review: "How did I handle emotions today?" | 3 minutes |
