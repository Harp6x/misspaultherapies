## What Is Narcissistic Abuse?

A pattern of manipulation, control, and emotional exploitation by someone with narcissistic traits. It can occur in romantic, family, friendship, or workplace relationships.

## Key Tactics
- **Love-bombing:** Intense affection early on to hook you
- **Gaslighting:** Making you doubt your reality ("That never happened")
- **Devaluation:** Gradual criticism, contempt, and put-downs
- **Isolation:** Cutting you off from support systems
- **Triangulation:** Using third parties to create jealousy or insecurity
- **Silent treatment:** Punishment through withdrawal
- **Projection:** Accusing you of what THEY are doing
- **Hoovering:** Pulling you back in after you try to leave

## Signs You're in a Narcissistic Relationship
- You constantly question your own perception
- You apologise for things that aren't your fault
- You feel like you're "walking on eggshells"
- Your self-esteem has plummeted since the relationship started
- You feel responsible for their emotions
- You've lost connection with friends and family
- The relationship cycles between wonderful and terrible

## Recovery

### 1. Education
Understanding narcissistic patterns is the first step. Name what happened.

### 2. No/Low Contact
If possible, eliminate or drastically reduce contact. This is essential for healing.

### 3. Rebuild Self-Trust
Your instincts were right all along. Relearn to trust yourself.

### 4. Process the Grief
You're grieving the person you THOUGHT they were, the relationship you WANTED, and the time you lost.

### 5. Therapy
Trauma-informed therapy for:
- Processing the abuse
- Rebuilding self-esteem
- Understanding why you were vulnerable to this dynamic
- Breaking the pattern for future relationships

## Indian Context
- Narcissistic dynamics in joint families (controlling in-laws, entitled family members)
- "Adjust karo" enabling narcissistic abuse in marriages
- Patriarchal structures giving narcissistic men unchecked power
- Cultural respect for elders making it harder to recognise narcissistic parents
