# Mindfulness for Beginners — A Practical Guide
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

---

## What Mindfulness Is (And Isn't)

**Is:** Paying attention to the present moment, on purpose, without judgement
**Isn't:** Clearing your mind, stopping thoughts, achieving bliss, or being calm all the time

Your mind WILL wander. That's normal. The practice IS noticing it wandered and gently coming back.

## Why Mindfulness Works

Research shows mindfulness:
- Reduces anxiety and depression symptoms
- Improves attention and focus
- Lowers cortisol (stress hormone)
- Strengthens emotional regulation
- Physically changes brain structure (increases grey matter in prefrontal cortex)

## 7 Core Practices

### 1. Mindful Breathing (5 minutes)
- Sit comfortably, eyes closed or soft gaze
- Focus on the sensation of breathing (nostrils, chest, belly)
- When mind wanders → notice → gently return to breath
- No judgement about wandering (that IS the practice)

### 2. Body Scan (10 minutes)
- Lie down or sit. Close eyes.
- Slowly move attention from toes to head
- Notice sensations: warmth, tension, tingling, nothing
- Don't try to change anything — just observe

### 3. Mindful Eating
- One meal per week: no phone, no TV
- Notice colours, textures, smells before eating
- Chew slowly. Taste each bite.
- Notice when you're full

### 4. Walking Meditation
- Walk slowly and deliberately
- Feel each foot touching the ground
- Notice the movement of your legs, the air on your skin
- When mind wanders, return to the sensation of walking

### 5. Loving-Kindness (Metta)
- Sit quietly. Bring to mind someone you love.
- Silently say: "May you be happy. May you be healthy. May you be safe."
- Extend to yourself, then a neutral person, then a difficult person, then all beings.

### 6. RAIN for Difficult Emotions
- **R**ecognise: "I'm feeling anxious"
- **A**llow: Let the feeling be there without fighting it
- **I**nvestigate: Where do I feel it in my body? What does it need?
- **N**urture: Offer yourself compassion

### 7. Mindful Moments Throughout the Day
- Mindful first sip of chai/coffee
- Mindful hand-washing (feel the water)
- Three conscious breaths before responding to a stressful message
- Gratitude moment before sleep

## Starting a Practice

| Week | Practice | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Mindful breathing | 3 minutes daily |
| 2 | Mindful breathing | 5 minutes daily |
| 3 | Add body scan | 5 min breathing + 5 min body scan |
| 4 | Add one mindful meal per week | Continue daily practice |

**The best meditation is the one you actually do. Start small. Be consistent.**
