# Dealing with Change & Uncertainty
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

---

## Why Change Is Hard

Your brain is wired for predictability. Change activates the same threat centres as physical danger. Even positive changes (marriage, promotion, new city) create stress.

## The Change Curve (Adapted from Kübler-Ross)

1. **Shock:** "This can't be happening"
2. **Denial:** "Maybe things will go back to normal"
3. **Frustration/Anger:** "Why is this happening to me?"
4. **Depression:** "I can't handle this"
5. **Experimentation:** "Maybe I can try..."
6. **Decision:** "I'm going to make this work"
7. **Integration:** "This is my new normal"

## Strategies for Each Phase

### During Shock & Denial
- Allow time to process
- Don't make major decisions
- Lean on your support system

### During Frustration & Depression
- Validate your feelings ("This is hard AND I'll get through it")
- Maintain routines (anchor points in chaos)
- Focus on what you CAN control

### During Experimentation & Integration
- Try new things with curiosity, not pressure
- Celebrate small adaptations
- Build new routines that serve you

## Tolerating Uncertainty

The skill of sitting with "I don't know" without spiraling:
- "I can handle not knowing for today"
- "Uncertainty is uncomfortable, not dangerous"
- "I've survived uncertainty before"
- Focus on the next 24 hours, not the next 5 years
