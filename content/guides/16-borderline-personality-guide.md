# Understanding Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD)
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

---

## What BPD Is

BPD is characterised by intense emotions, unstable relationships, fear of abandonment, impulsive behaviours, and a fragile sense of self. It's not a character flaw — it's a pattern that develops from a combination of genetic vulnerability and invalidating environments.

## Core Features

### Emotional Dysregulation
- Emotions are felt 10x more intensely and last longer
- Mood can shift rapidly within hours
- Emotional pain feels unbearable
- Small rejections feel catastrophic

### Unstable Relationships
- Idealisation ("You're perfect") → Devaluation ("You're terrible")
- Fear of abandonment drives clinginess or preemptive rejection
- Intense, chaotic relationship patterns
- Difficulty maintaining consistent friendships

### Identity Disturbance
- "Who am I?" — shifting values, goals, preferences
- Adopting others' identities or interests
- Feeling empty or hollow
- Not knowing what you want from life

### Impulsive Behaviours
- Reckless spending, substance use, binge eating
- Self-harm or suicidal behaviours (NOT for attention — for pain relief)
- Impulsive relationship decisions

## DBT: The Gold Standard Treatment

**Dialectical Behaviour Therapy** was designed specifically for BPD:

### Four Skill Modules

1. **Mindfulness:** Observing emotions without reacting
2. **Distress Tolerance:** Surviving crisis without making things worse (TIPP, ice diving, self-soothing)
3. **Emotion Regulation:** Understanding and managing intense feelings
4. **Interpersonal Effectiveness:** Asking for what you need, saying no, maintaining self-respect

### The TIPP Skill (Crisis Survival)
- **T**emperature: Cold water on face, ice on neck
- **I**ntense exercise: 20 minutes of hard activity
- **P**aced breathing: Slow exhale
- **P**rogressive relaxation: Tense and release muscles

## For Loved Ones

- Educate yourself about BPD — it's not manipulation
- Set boundaries with compassion
- Validate their emotions even when you disagree with their actions
- Don't take splitting (idealisation/devaluation) personally
- Get your own therapy or support

## Recovery Is Possible

With proper treatment, many people with BPD achieve significant improvement. The diagnosis doesn't define you — it explains patterns that can be changed.
