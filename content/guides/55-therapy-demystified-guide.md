## Common Myths About Therapy

| Myth | Reality |
|------|---------|
| "Therapy is for crazy people" | Therapy is for anyone who wants to understand themselves better |
| "Just talking won't help" | Therapy literally changes brain structure and chemistry |
| "I should be able to handle it myself" | Seeking help IS handling it |
| "It takes years" | Many issues improve in 8-16 sessions |
| "The therapist will judge me" | We've heard it all. We don't judge. |
| "It's too expensive" | Many therapists offer sliding scale; it's an investment in yourself |

## Types of Therapy

### CBT (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy)
**Best for:** Anxiety, depression, OCD, phobias, insomnia
**Focus:** Changing unhelpful thought patterns and behaviours
**Style:** Structured, homework-based, goal-oriented

### DBT (Dialectical Behaviour Therapy)
**Best for:** BPD, emotional dysregulation, self-harm, chronic suicidality
**Focus:** Distress tolerance, emotional regulation, mindfulness, interpersonal skills

### EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitisation & Reprocessing)
**Best for:** PTSD, trauma, phobias
**Focus:** Processing traumatic memories through bilateral stimulation

### Psychodynamic Therapy
**Best for:** Recurring patterns, relationship issues, identity exploration
**Focus:** Understanding how past experiences shape present behaviour

### ACT (Acceptance & Commitment Therapy)
**Best for:** Chronic pain, anxiety, depression, life transitions
**Focus:** Accepting difficult feelings while committing to values-based action

### EFT (Emotionally Focused Therapy)
**Best for:** Couples, attachment issues
**Focus:** Understanding and changing emotional patterns in relationships

## What to Expect in Your First Session

1. **Paperwork:** Consent forms, intake questionnaire
2. **Getting to know you:** The therapist will ask about your background, current concerns, and goals
3. **It's okay to be nervous:** Everyone is in their first session
4. **You lead:** You share only what you're comfortable with
5. **The therapist won't "fix" you in session 1:** It's a foundation-building conversation
6. **You can ask questions:** About their approach, experience, and how therapy will work

## How to Get the Most From Therapy

- **Be honest** (even about the hard stuff)
- **Do the homework** (if your therapist assigns it)
- **Bring topics** (what's been on your mind since last session?)
- **Give feedback** ("That exercise didn't feel helpful" is valuable)
- **Be patient** (it often gets harder before it gets easier)
- **Attend consistently** (weekly sessions build momentum)

## How to Choose the Right Therapist

- **Qualifications:** M.Phil Clinical Psychology (RCI licensed) or equivalent
- **Specialisation:** Relevant to your concerns
- **Approach:** Ask about their therapeutic orientation
- **Fit:** Do you feel safe and heard? (The relationship matters most)
- **Logistics:** Online/in-person, fees, scheduling, cancellation policy

## When to Consider Changing Therapists

- You don't feel safe or heard after 3-4 sessions
- The therapist imposes their values on you
- No progress after 10+ sessions with no clear explanation
- Boundary violations (personal questions, dual relationships)
- You feel judged rather than understood

## Online Therapy: Equally Effective

Research confirms online therapy is as effective as in-person for most conditions. Benefits: no travel, more flexible scheduling, access to specialists anywhere in India.
