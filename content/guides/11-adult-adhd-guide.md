## What ADHD Looks Like in Adults

ADHD isn't just a childhood condition — it persists into adulthood in 60% of cases. In India, it's massively underdiagnosed, especially in women and high-achievers who compensate through anxiety.

### Three Presentations
1. **Inattentive (formerly ADD):** Daydreaming, forgetfulness, disorganisation, difficulty sustaining focus
2. **Hyperactive-Impulsive:** Restlessness, impulsivity, talking excessively, difficulty waiting
3. **Combined:** Both inattentive and hyperactive traits

### Why It's Missed in India
- Confused with laziness or lack of willpower
- Academic success through compensatory anxiety masks the condition
- Women present differently (inattentive, quiet, "daydreamy")
- Cultural emphasis on discipline over neurodiversity
- Limited ADHD awareness among Indian mental health professionals

## Executive Function: The Real Problem

ADHD is fundamentally an executive function disorder. Executive functions include:
- **Working memory** (holding information in mind)
- **Time management** (perceiving and managing time)
- **Emotional regulation** (managing frustration, excitement)
- **Task initiation** (starting tasks, especially boring ones)
- **Organisation** (keeping track of things, planning)
- **Inhibition** (impulse control, waiting)

## Practical Strategies

### For Focus
- Use body doubling (work alongside someone)
- Pomodoro technique (25 on, 5 off)
- External accountability (deadlines, check-ins)
- Interest-based nervous system: make boring tasks interesting (gamify, pair with music)
- One tab, one task at a time

### For Time Management
- Visual timers (see time passing)
- Buffer time between everything (build in 15-min cushions)
- Set alarms for transitions
- "If it takes less than 2 minutes, do it now"

### For Emotional Regulation
- Name the emotion before acting on it
- Use TIPP skills (Temperature, Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Progressive relaxation)
- Wait 24 hours before major decisions
- Exercise daily (non-negotiable for ADHD)

### For Organisation
- Everything has a "home" (keys, wallet, phone)
- Use ONE system (not 5 apps, 3 notebooks, and loose papers)
- Sunday evening planning session (15 minutes)
- Visual reminders (sticky notes, whiteboards)

## ADHD Strengths

ADHD isn't all deficit:
- Creativity and out-of-the-box thinking
- Hyperfocus on interesting tasks
- Energy and enthusiasm
- Resilience from navigating a world not designed for you
- Empathy and emotional depth

## Treatment Options

1. **Medication** (stimulant or non-stimulant) — consult a psychiatrist
2. **CBT adapted for ADHD** — skills-based therapy for executive function
3. **Coaching** — practical accountability and structure
4. **Environmental design** — setting up your life to work WITH your brain
5. **Exercise** — as effective as low-dose medication for some
