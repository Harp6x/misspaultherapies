## What Is Emotional Regulation?

The ability to manage your emotional responses — not suppressing them, but experiencing them without being overwhelmed or acting impulsively.

## The Window of Tolerance

- **Hyperarousal zone:** Anxiety, panic, rage, restlessness (above the window)
- **Window of tolerance:** Calm, present, able to think and feel (the goal)
- **Hypoarousal zone:** Numbness, dissociation, shutdown, depression (below the window)

## Core Skills

### 1. STOP Skill
**S**top | **T**ake a breath | **O**bserve | **P**roceed mindfully

### 2. TIPP Skills (Crisis Level)
- **T**emperature: Ice on face, cold water on wrists
- **I**ntense exercise: Sprint, jumping jacks (2 minutes)
- **P**aced breathing: Exhale longer than inhale
- **P**rogressive relaxation: Tense and release muscles

### 3. Name It to Tame It
Labelling emotions reduces their intensity by 50%. "I notice I am feeling angry."

### 4. Opposite Action
When emotion urges you to do something unhelpful, do the opposite:
- Anger → Speak gently, walk away
- Fear → Approach what you fear (gradually)
- Sadness → Activate (move, connect, accomplish)
- Shame → Share with someone safe

### 5. Ride the Wave
Emotions are like waves — they rise, peak, and fall. Average duration: 90 seconds. If you don't feed the emotion with thoughts, it will pass.

## Daily Emotional Hygiene
- Morning emotion check-in (1 minute)
- Midday body scan (notice physical signals of emotion)
- Evening processing (journal or talk about your day)
- Regular exercise, sleep, and nutrition (the foundation of regulation)
