# Self-Compassion Assessment
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

---

**Scale:** 1 = Almost never | 2 = Rarely | 3 = Sometimes | 4 = Often | 5 = Almost always

### Self-Kindness
| 1 | I'm kind to myself when things go wrong | |
| 2 | I give myself the caring I need | |

### Self-Judgement (reverse scored)
| 3 | I'm disapproving and judgmental about my flaws | |
| 4 | I'm intolerant of aspects of my personality I don't like | |

### Common Humanity
| 5 | I try to see my failures as part of the human condition | |
| 6 | I remind myself that others go through similar things | |

### Isolation (reverse scored)
| 7 | When I fail, I feel alone in my failure | |
| 8 | When I'm down, I feel most people are happier than me | |

### Mindfulness
| 9 | When something upsets me, I try to keep my emotions in balance | |
| 10 | I take a balanced view of the situation | |

### Over-Identification (reverse scored)
| 11 | When I fail, I become consumed by feelings of inadequacy | |
| 12 | When something painful happens, I blow it out of proportion | |

**Total (after reversing items 3,4,7,8,11,12): ____ / 60**

| Score | Level |
|-------|-------|
| 48–60 | High self-compassion |
| 36–47 | Moderate self-compassion |
| 24–35 | Low self-compassion — practice recommended |
| 12–23 | Very low — self-compassion focused therapy recommended |
