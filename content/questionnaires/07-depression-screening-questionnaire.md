## Scoring

| Score | Severity | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 0–4 | Minimal | Monitor |
| 5–9 | Mild depression | Watchful waiting; self-help |
| 10–14 | Moderate depression | Therapy recommended |
| 15–19 | Moderately severe | Therapy + consider medication |
| 20–27 | Severe depression | Urgent professional support |

> ⚠️ **If you scored 1+ on Question 9:** Please reach out to a mental health professional or call Vandrevala Foundation: 1860-2662-345
