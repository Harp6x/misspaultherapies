Your primary attachment style is the section with the highest score. Most people are a blend.

## What This Means

- **Secure:** Healthy relationship foundation — continue nurturing
- **Anxious:** You may benefit from therapy focusing on self-soothing and trust
- **Avoidant:** Explore emotional intimacy gradually in a safe therapeutic space
- **Disorganised:** Often linked to early trauma — trauma-informed therapy recommended

**Attachment styles can change with awareness and effort.**
