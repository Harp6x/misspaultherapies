**Total Score: ____ / 80**

## Scoring

| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| 0–20 | Below clinical threshold |
| 21–32 | Some PTSD symptoms — monitor |
| 33–50 | Probable PTSD — professional assessment recommended |
| 51–80 | Significant PTSD — professional support urgently recommended |

**A provisional PTSD diagnosis requires: 1+ intrusion, 1+ avoidance, 2+ negative cognition/mood, 2+ hyperarousal symptoms rated ≥2.**
