| Score | Severity | Recommendation |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| 0–4 | Minimal anxiety | Self-care strategies |
| 5–9 | Mild anxiety | Monitor; self-help techniques |
| 10–14 | Moderate anxiety | Consider professional support |
| 15–21 | Severe anxiety | Professional support recommended |
