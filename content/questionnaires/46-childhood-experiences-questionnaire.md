## What Your Score Means

| Score | Risk Level |
|-------|-----------|
| 0 | Low ACE score |
| 1–3 | Moderate — awareness of impact recommended |
| 4–6 | High — increased risk of health/mental health issues. Therapy strongly recommended |
| 7–10 | Very high — trauma-informed therapy essential |

## Important Notes

- A high ACE score does NOT determine your future — it describes your past
- Resilience factors (one supportive adult, community, education) can buffer ACE impact
- It's never too late to heal from childhood experiences
- Many people with high ACE scores live meaningful, healthy lives with the right support
