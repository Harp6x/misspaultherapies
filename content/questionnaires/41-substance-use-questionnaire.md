## Part 1: Core Questions (Answer Yes/No)

| # | Question | Yes | No |
|---|----------|-----|----|
| 1 | Have you felt you should **Cut down** on your drinking or drug use? | | |
| 2 | Have people **Annoyed** you by criticising your substance use? | | |
| 3 | Have you felt **Guilty** about your drinking or drug use? | | |
| 4 | Have you needed a drink or drug first thing in the morning (**Eye-opener**)? | | |

**2+ Yes answers = clinically significant — professional assessment recommended**
