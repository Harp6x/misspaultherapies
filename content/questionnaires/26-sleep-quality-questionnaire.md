## Interpretation

- Sleep latency >30 min, <6 hours sleep, or frequent waking = poor sleep quality
- Racing thoughts (Q13) scored 2+ = anxiety-related insomnia — CBT-I recommended
- Screen use (Q14) scored 2+ = digital hygiene intervention needed
- Medication use (Q16) 2+ = discuss alternatives with healthcare provider
