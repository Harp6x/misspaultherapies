## Scoring

| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| 0–16 | Unlikely ADHD |
| 17–32 | Possible ADHD — further evaluation recommended |
| 33–48 | Probable ADHD — professional assessment recommended |
| 49–64 | Highly likely ADHD — seek comprehensive evaluation |

**Note:** ADHD in adults looks different from children. If you scored high AND these patterns have been present since childhood, professional assessment is strongly recommended.
