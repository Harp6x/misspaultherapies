## Scoring

| Score | Level | Action |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 15–30 | Low burnout risk | Maintain healthy habits |
| 31–45 | Moderate burnout risk | Increase self-care, set boundaries |
| 46–60 | High burnout | Seek support, consider changes |
| 61–75 | Severe burnout | Professional help recommended urgently |

## Subscore Analysis

- **Exhaustion (Q1-5) high:** Physical and emotional depletion — rest and recovery needed
- **Cynicism (Q6-10) high:** Disconnection from work — values realignment needed
- **Reduced efficacy (Q11-15) high:** Confidence eroded — support and skill-building needed
