## Scoring Guide

- **Obsessions/compulsions rated "Often" or "Always" + Impact score 8+:** OCD likely — seek professional assessment
- **Time spent 1+ hour daily:** Clinically significant
- **ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention)** is the gold standard treatment
