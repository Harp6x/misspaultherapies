### Scoring Guide

**Per dimension:**
- **21–25:** Strong — this is a personal strength
- **16–20:** Good — room for growth
- **11–15:** Developing — focus attention here
- **5–10:** Needs support — consider working on this in therapy

**Overall:**
- **120–150:** Strong emotional wellness
- **90–119:** Generally healthy with some growth areas
- **60–89:** Several areas need attention
- **Below 60:** Professional support recommended
