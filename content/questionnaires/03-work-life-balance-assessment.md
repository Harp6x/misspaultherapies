**"Yes" count: ___ / 10**

- **8–10:** Strong work boundaries
- **5–7:** Some boundaries but gaps exist
- **0–4:** Work is dominating your life — this needs urgent attention

## Part 6: The Recalibration Plan

### This Week
- [ ] Set one work boundary (e.g., no emails after 8pm)
- [ ] Schedule one activity that's purely for enjoyment
- [ ] Have one phone-free meal with someone I care about
- [ ] Go to bed 30 minutes earlier than usual
- [ ] Say no to one non-essential commitment

### This Month
- [ ] Audit and adjust my weekly schedule
- [ ] Have a conversation with my manager about workload (if applicable)
- [ ] Plan one day that's entirely for me
- [ ] Reconnect with a friend I've been neglecting
- [ ] Start one new hobby or revisit an old one

### Ongoing
- [ ] Weekly check-in: "Am I living according to my values this week?"
- [ ] Monthly review of the Balance Wheel
- [ ] Protect rest time as fiercely as work time
