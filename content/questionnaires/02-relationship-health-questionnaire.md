## How to Use

Both partners should complete this independently, then compare answers together. Rate each statement 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree) based on how things have been over the past month.

### Per Section
- **21–25:** This is a relationship strength
- **16–20:** Healthy, with room to deepen
- **11–15:** This area needs intentional work
- **5–10:** This is a significant concern — consider couples therapy

### Overall
- **120–150:** Strong, healthy relationship
- **90–119:** Generally good with some areas to nurture
- **60–89:** Several areas of concern — proactive work recommended
- **Below 60:** Professional support strongly recommended

## Discussion Guide

### For Both Partners Together

1. **Where do we align?** (Sections where both scored similarly)
2. **Where do we differ?** (Sections with the biggest gap between scores)
3. **What surprised me about my partner's answers?**
4. **What's one thing we do really well as a couple?**
5. **What's one area we both want to improve?**
6. **What's one action each of us will take this week?**

### Important Rules for This Conversation
- No blame or defensiveness
- Listen to understand, not to respond
- Accept your partner's experience as valid (even if you see it differently)
- Focus on solutions, not who's at fault

## Red Flags Checklist

If ANY of these are present, professional help is important:

- [ ] There is physical aggression of any kind
- [ ] One partner controls the other's finances, friendships, or movements
- [ ] There is name-calling, humiliation, or contempt
- [ ] One partner is afraid of the other
- [ ] Infidelity has not been addressed
- [ ] There are threats (leaving, harming, involving family)
- [ ] One partner feels trapped

**These are not "communication problems" — they are safety concerns. Please seek professional help.**
