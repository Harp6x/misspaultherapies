## The Science Behind Gratitude

Gratitude isn't just "being thankful." Research from UC Berkeley and Harvard shows:
- Gratitude practice **physically changes brain structure** — strengthening neural pathways for positive thinking
- People who journal gratitude for 3 weeks show **measurable increases** in happiness and decreases in depression
- Gratitude improves sleep, immune function, and relationship satisfaction
- It activates the same brain regions as social bonding and reward

**Commitment:** 5 minutes per evening, for 30 days.

---

## How to Use This Journal

Each day has a different prompt. Write at least 3 lines per prompt. Be SPECIFIC — "I'm grateful for good food" is less powerful than "I'm grateful for the dal mum made tonight because it reminded me of childhood."

## Quick Gratitude Techniques for Busy Days

If you can't journal, try these:

- **Gratitude alarm:** Set a phone alarm at 3pm. When it rings, think of 3 things.
- **Gratitude walk:** On your commute, notice 5 things you're grateful for.
- **Gratitude text:** Send one appreciation message per day.
- **Gratitude jar:** Write one thing on a slip of paper daily. Read them all on a hard day.
- **Before sleep:** Last thought before closing your eyes — one good thing from today.
