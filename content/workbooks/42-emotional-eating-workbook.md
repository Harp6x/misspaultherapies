## Week 2: Hunger vs Emotion
### Physical Hunger vs Emotional Hunger
| Physical | Emotional |
|----------|----------|
| Gradual onset | Sudden onset |
| Any food satisfies | Specific cravings |
| Stops when full | Continues past fullness |
| No guilt after | Guilt/shame after |

### Before eating, ask: "Am I physically hungry or emotionally hungry?"
