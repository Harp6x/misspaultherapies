## Understanding Stress

Stress is your body's response to demands. Some stress is helpful (it motivates you). But chronic stress damages your body, mind, and relationships.

### The Stress Response

```
Perceived Threat → Amygdala Activates → Cortisol & Adrenaline Released → Fight/Flight/Freeze
```

The problem: your brain can't distinguish between a tiger and an angry email. Both trigger the same response.

## Week 2: Stress Reduction Toolkit

### Tier 1: Instant Relief (Under 2 Minutes)

| Technique | How | When to Use |
|-----------|-----|------------|
| Box Breathing | Inhale 4s → Hold 4s → Exhale 4s → Hold 4s | Before meetings, during panic |
| Shoulder Drop | Inhale and lift shoulders to ears → exhale and drop them | Desk tension |
| Cold Water | Splash cold water on face and wrists | Emotional flooding |
| Power Pose | Stand tall, hands on hips, 2 minutes | Before stressful events |
| 54321 Grounding | 5 see, 4 touch, 3 hear, 2 smell, 1 taste | When overwhelmed |

### Tier 2: Short-Term Relief (5–15 Minutes)

| Technique | How |
|-----------|-----|
| Journaling | Write freely for 10 minutes — no editing, no judgement |
| Walking | 10-minute walk outside, no phone |
| PMR | Progressive muscle relaxation — tense and release each muscle group |
| Music | Listen to one calming song with full attention |
| Stretching | 5-minute gentle stretch sequence |

### Tier 3: Long-Term Stress Resilience

| Practice | Frequency | Impact |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Regular exercise | 3–5 times/week | Reduces cortisol, improves mood |
| Consistent sleep | Every night | Restores stress resilience |
| Social connection | Weekly | Oxytocin release, emotional support |
| Meditation/mindfulness | Daily, 5–10 min | Shrinks amygdala, grows prefrontal cortex |
| Saying no | As needed | Prevents overcommitment |
| Nature time | Weekly | Lowers cortisol measurably |

## The "Good Enough" Framework

Perfectionism is a major stress driver. Practice "good enough":

| Area | Perfectionist Standard | "Good Enough" Standard |
|------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Work | Flawless, first time | Complete, competent, on time |
| Home | Instagram-perfect | Clean enough to be comfortable |
| Parenting | Never lose patience | Loving, present, human |
| Fitness | Daily intense workouts | Some movement most days |
| Social | Everyone must like me | I have a few genuine connections |
