## Week 4: Body-Based Techniques

### Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)
Tense each muscle group for 5 seconds, then release for 10 seconds:
1. Feet and toes
2. Calves
3. Thighs
4. Stomach
5. Chest
6. Hands and arms
7. Shoulders
8. Face

### 4-7-8 Breathing
- Inhale: 4 seconds
- Hold: 7 seconds
- Exhale: 8 seconds
- Repeat 4 cycles
