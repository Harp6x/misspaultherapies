## Week 3: Thought Patterns in Depression

### Depression's Favourite Lies
- "Nothing will ever get better" (fortune-telling)
- "I'm a burden" (labelling)
- "Everyone else is happy" (comparison)
- "It's all my fault" (personalising)
- "What's the point?" (hopelessness)

## Week 4: Self-Care When You Can Barely Function

### The Bare Minimum Checklist
When everything feels impossible, just do these:
- ☐ Drink water
- ☐ Eat something (anything)
- ☐ Take medication (if prescribed)
- ☐ Brush teeth
- ☐ Open a window or step outside for 1 minute

### Gradually Expanding
As energy returns, add ONE thing per week from this list:
- ☐ 10-minute walk
- ☐ Shower
- ☐ One social interaction
- ☐ 15 minutes of a hobby
- ☐ Cook a simple meal
