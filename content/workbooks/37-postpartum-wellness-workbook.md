## Module 2: Permission Slips
Check all you give yourself permission for:
- ☐ To not enjoy every moment
- ☐ To cry
- ☐ To ask for help
- ☐ To feel overwhelmed
- ☐ To miss my pre-baby life
- ☐ To not be a perfect mother
- ☐ To take medication if needed

## Module 3: Daily Minimum Self-Care
| Need | How I'll meet it |
|------|-----------------|
| Sleep | Partner takes one night feed |
| Food | Pre-made meals, accept food gifts |
| Shower | While baby naps or partner watches |
| Fresh air | 10-min walk with baby |
| Connection | Daily call/text to one adult |

## Module 5: When to Get Help
If any of these persist for 2+ weeks, contact a professional:
- ☐ Unable to stop crying
- ☐ Can't sleep even when baby sleeps
- ☐ No interest in baby
- ☐ Thoughts of harming self or baby
- ☐ Feeling nothing (numbness)
