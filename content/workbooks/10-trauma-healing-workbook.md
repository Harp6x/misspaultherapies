## Module 1: Safety First

### My Safe Space Visualisation
Describe a real or imagined place where you feel completely safe:
- What do you see? ____
- What do you hear? ____
- What do you smell? ____
- What do you feel on your skin? ____
- What emotion fills this place? ____

### Grounding Toolkit
When overwhelmed, use:
- **5-4-3-2-1:** 5 things you see, 4 hear, 3 touch, 2 smell, 1 taste
- **Ice:** Hold an ice cube, splash cold water on face
- **Feet on floor:** Feel the ground beneath you
- **Name it:** "I am in [location]. It is [date]. I am safe."

## Module 3: The Body Remembers

### Body Scan Journal

- Head/face: ____
- Throat/neck: ____
- Chest: ____
- Stomach: ____
- Hands: ____
- Legs/feet: ____

### Body-Based Release (choose one daily)
- ☐ Shaking (literally shake your body for 2 minutes)
- ☐ Yoga (trauma-sensitive, slow)
- ☐ Walking (mindful, noticing each step)
- ☐ Dancing (free movement, no rules)
- ☐ Deep breathing with long exhale
