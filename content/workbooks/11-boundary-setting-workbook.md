## Week 1: Boundary Awareness

### Types of Boundaries
- **Physical:** Personal space, touch, physical needs
- **Emotional:** Protecting your feelings, not absorbing others'
- **Time:** How you spend your time and energy
- **Digital:** Online availability, social media
- **Material:** Money, possessions, lending
- **Conversational:** Topics you will/won't discuss

## Week 2: Why Boundaries Are Hard

### My Boundary Blocks
- ☐ Guilt ("I should be available")
- ☐ Fear of conflict
- ☐ Fear of rejection
- ☐ Cultural expectations ("good daughter/wife/son")
- ☐ Believing my needs don't matter
- ☐ Not knowing HOW to set boundaries

### Reframes
| Old belief | New belief |
|-----------|-----------|
| "Setting boundaries is selfish" | "Boundaries protect relationships" |
| "They'll be angry at me" | "Their reaction is their responsibility" |
| "Good people don't say no" | "I can be good AND have limits" |

## Week 3: Boundary Scripts

### The Formula
"I [boundary statement]. I [consequence if not respected]."

### Practice Scripts
- Family: "I love you and I'm not available to discuss my marriage plans."
- Work: "I can take this on if we deprioritise X. Which matters more?"
- Friend: "I'm not able to lend money right now."
- Partner: "I need 30 minutes of alone time after work before we talk."
