## Module 3: Shame Resilience Steps (Brené Brown)
1. **Recognise:** "I am in shame right now."
2. **Reality-check:** "Is this true? Am I really ____?"
3. **Reach out:** Tell someone you trust.
4. **Speak shame:** Name it. Shame loses power when spoken.
