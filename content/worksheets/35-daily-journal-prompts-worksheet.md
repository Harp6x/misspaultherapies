# Daily Journal Prompts Worksheet (30 Days)
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

---

| Day | Prompt |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | What am I feeling right now? Where do I feel it in my body? |
| 2 | What am I grateful for today? |
| 3 | What would I tell my younger self? |
| 4 | What boundary do I need to set? |
| 5 | What is one thing I accomplished recently that I'm proud of? |
| 6 | What does my ideal day look like? |
| 7 | What am I avoiding? What would happen if I faced it? |
| 8 | Write a letter to someone you need to forgive (including yourself). |
| 9 | What are 5 things that make me uniquely me? |
| 10 | What does self-care look like for me today? |
| 11 | What thought has been on repeat? Is it true? |
| 12 | What do I need more of in my life? Less of? |
| 13 | Describe a moment that changed you. |
| 14 | What am I holding onto that I need to release? |
| 15 | What would I do if I weren't afraid? |
| 16 | Write about someone who makes you feel safe. |
| 17 | What are my values? Am I living by them? |
| 18 | What does success mean to ME (not others)? |
| 19 | What are 3 things my body did for me today? |
| 20 | When do I feel most like myself? |
| 21 | What is one thing I'd like to learn? |
| 22 | Write about a hard season you survived. |
| 23 | What do I wish people understood about me? |
| 24 | What brings me peace? |
| 25 | Write about a relationship that matters to you. |
| 26 | What am I worried about? Is it in my control? |
| 27 | What would I say to a friend going through what I'm going through? |
| 28 | What makes me feel alive? |
| 29 | What have I learned about myself this month? |
| 30 | Write a love letter to yourself. |
