### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

## The Full Thought Record

| Column | Instructions |
|--------|-------------|
| 1. Situation | What happened? (Facts only) |
| 2. Emotion | What did you feel? Rate intensity (0-100) |
| 3. Automatic thought | What went through your mind? |
| 4. Evidence FOR | What supports this thought? |
| 5. Evidence AGAINST | What contradicts it? |
| 6. Balanced thought | A more realistic perspective |
| 7. Outcome | Re-rate emotion (0-100) |
