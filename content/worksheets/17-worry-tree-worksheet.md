## The Worry Tree Process

### Step 1: Notice the worry
"I am worrying about: ____"

### Step 2: Is this a hypothetical or practical worry?

**Hypothetical** = "What if..." (hasn't happened, may never happen)
→ Let it go. Use: distraction, mindfulness, or schedule worry time.

**Practical** = A real problem happening now that needs action
→ Go to Step 3.

### Step 3: Can I do something about it RIGHT NOW?

**No** → Let it go. Schedule it for later. Use worry time.

**Yes** → Go to Step 4.

### Step 4: Make a plan
- What I'll do: ____
- When: ____
- First step: ____

### Step 5: DO IT. Then let the worry go.
