## Why Goal-Setting Matters for Mental Health

When we feel stuck, lost, or depressed, we often lack a sense of direction. Meaningful goals provide:
- **Purpose** — something to move toward
- **Structure** — daily actions that build momentum
- **Self-efficacy** — evidence that you're capable
- **Hope** — a vision for something better

### Common Goal Killers

| Killer | Counter-Strategy |
|--------|-----------------|
| Perfectionism | "Progress, not perfection. Done is better than perfect." |
| All-or-nothing thinking | "A small step still counts. 1% is better than 0%." |
| Comparison | "Their timeline is not mine. My pace is valid." |
| Fear of failure | "Failure is data. It teaches me what to adjust." |
| Lack of motivation | "I don't need motivation. I need a system. Start with 5 minutes." |
| Overwhelm | "I only need to focus on the next small step." |

## Step 6: The Compassion Clause

**When I fall behind (and I will, because I'm human), I will:**
- [ ] Acknowledge the setback without self-criticism
- [ ] Ask "What got in the way?" (not "What's wrong with me?")
- [ ] Adjust the plan if needed
- [ ] Take one small step to get back on track
- [ ] Remember why this goal matters to me
