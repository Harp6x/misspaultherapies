## Why Values Matter

Values are the directions you want your life to move in. They're different from goals:
- **Goal:** "Get promoted" (achievable, then done)
- **Value:** "Growth" (an ongoing direction you can always move toward)

When you know your values, decision-making becomes clearer, anxiety about "the right choice" decreases, and life feels more meaningful.
