## Rules
1. **Start at the bottom** — don't jump ahead
2. **Stay until anxiety drops 50%** (habituation)
3. **Repeat each step 3-5 times** before moving up
4. **No safety behaviours** during exposure
5. **Celebrate each attempt** — courage, not comfort
