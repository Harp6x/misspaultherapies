## What Are Core Beliefs?
Deep, often unconscious beliefs about ourselves, others, and the world formed in childhood.

## Step 1: Downward Arrow Technique
Start with an automatic thought and ask "If that were true, what would it mean about me?"

**Automatic thought:** ____
↓ If true, it means: ____
↓ And that means: ____
↓ **Core belief:** ____
