# Communication Repair Worksheet (for Couples)
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

---

## After a Conflict: Repair Checklist

### Step 1: Cool Down (Minimum 20 min apart)
- What I did to calm myself: ____

### Step 2: Reflect
- What triggered me? ____
- What I felt underneath the anger: ____
- My part in the conflict: ____

### Step 3: Repair Conversation
**Opening:** "I want to repair what happened. Can we talk?"

**Each partner shares:**
- "When ____ happened, I felt ____."
- "My part in this was ____."
- "What I needed was ____."
- "I'm sorry for ____."

**Listener:** "What I hear you saying is ____. That makes sense because ____."

### Step 4: Agreement
- "Next time this comes up, we'll ____."
- "I'll try to ____."
- "I need you to ____."

---

## Repair Attempt Phrases (keep handy)
- "Can we start over?"
- "I'm sorry"
- "I didn't mean to hurt you"
- "You're right about that part"
- "What do you need from me right now?"
- "I love you, even when we fight"
