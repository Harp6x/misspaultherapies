## Flashback Protocol
When a flashback happens:
1. ☐ Open your eyes. Look around.
2. ☐ Say: "I am [name]. I am in [location]. It is [date]. I am safe."
3. ☐ Feel your feet on the floor.
4. ☐ Hold something cold (ice, water).
5. ☐ Name 5 things you can see.
6. ☐ Call: ____ (safe person)
