## Create Your Personalised Coping Card
*Cut this out and keep it in your wallet/phone case.*

---

### ✂️ COPING CARD ✂️

**When I feel anxious, I will remember:**

1. **This feeling is temporary.** It will pass.
2. **I have survived this before.** Evidence: ____
3. **My anxiety is lying.** It says: ____ | The truth is: ____

**I will do ONE of these:**
- ☐ Breathe: 4 in, 7 hold, 8 out (×4)
- ☐ Ground: 5 see, 4 hear, 3 touch, 2 smell, 1 taste
- ☐ Move: Walk, stretch, shake
- ☐ Call: ____ (safe person)
- ☐ My tool: ____

**Affirmation:** "____"
