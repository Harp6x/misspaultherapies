## Instructions

Use this worksheet whenever you notice a strong negative emotion. Fill in each column from left to right. The goal is not to eliminate negative thoughts but to see them more clearly and respond to them more helpfully.

Print multiple copies and keep one in your bag, on your desk, and by your bed.

---

## Thought Record

| Column | Prompt |
|--------|--------|
| **1. Date / Time** | When did this happen? |
| **2. Situation** | What was happening? Where were you? Who was there? |
| **3. Emotions** | What did you feel? Rate each emotion 0–100% |
| **4. Automatic Thought** | What went through your mind? What's the "hot thought" (the one causing the most distress)? |
| **5. Evidence For** | What supports this thought? |
| **6. Evidence Against** | What contradicts this thought? |
| **7. Thinking Trap** | Which cognitive distortion is this? (All-or-nothing, catastrophising, mind reading, etc.) |
| **8. Balanced Thought** | What's a more realistic, balanced way to see this? |
| **9. Outcome** | Re-rate your emotions now (0–100%). What will you do? |

## Quick Reference: Common Thinking Traps

| Trap | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| All-or-Nothing | "If it's not perfect, it's a failure" |
| Catastrophising | "The worst possible thing will happen" |
| Mind Reading | "I know what they're thinking about me" |
| Fortune Telling | "I just know it will go wrong" |
| Should Statements | "I should be better/stronger/smarter" |
| Emotional Reasoning | "I feel it, so it must be true" |
| Magnifying | "This small mistake is a disaster" |
| Minimising | "The good things don't really count" |
| Personalisation | "It's all my fault" |
| Labelling | "I'm a failure" (instead of "I failed at this one thing") |
| Mental Filter | Focusing only on the negative |
| Discounting Positives | "That doesn't count because..." |

## Tips for Effective Thought Records

1. **Do it close to the event** — memories and emotions fade quickly
2. **Be specific** — "I felt bad" → "I felt ashamed (80%) and anxious (60%)"
3. **The balanced thought must be believable** — not fake positivity
4. **Look for patterns** — do the same thinking traps keep appearing?
5. **Practice daily** — this is a skill that gets easier with repetition
6. **Don't judge yourself** — having negative thoughts is human
