## When to Use: Anxiety, panic, flashbacks, dissociation, overwhelm

## 5-4-3-2-1 Technique

| Sense | Count | What I notice |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **SEE** | 5 things | 1. ____ 2. ____ 3. ____ 4. ____ 5. ____ |
| **HEAR** | 4 things | 1. ____ 2. ____ 3. ____ 4. ____ |
| **TOUCH** | 3 things | 1. ____ 2. ____ 3. ____ |
| **SMELL** | 2 things | 1. ____ 2. ____ |
| **TASTE** | 1 thing | 1. ____ |

## Body-Based Grounding

- ☐ Press feet firmly into floor. Feel the ground.
- ☐ Hold ice cube in your hand for 30 seconds.
- ☐ Splash cold water on your face.
- ☐ Clench and release fists 5 times.
- ☐ Name where you are: "I am in ____. It is ____. I am safe."

## Cognitive Grounding

- ☐ Count backwards from 100 by 7s
- ☐ Name 5 animals starting with the letter S
- ☐ Describe what you're wearing in detail
- ☐ Recite song lyrics or a poem from memory
- ☐ List every colour you can see
