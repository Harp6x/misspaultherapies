## The 3-Step Self-Compassion Break

### When something difficult happens:

**Step 1: Mindfulness** — "This is a moment of suffering."
(Acknowledge the pain without exaggerating or suppressing it)
What I'm feeling: ____

**Step 2: Common Humanity** — "Suffering is part of being human. I'm not alone in this."
Who else might feel this way? ____

**Step 3: Self-Kindness** — Place hand on heart. "May I be kind to myself."
What kind words do I need to hear? ____
