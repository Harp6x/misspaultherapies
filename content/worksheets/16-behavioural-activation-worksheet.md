## Activity Ideas (When Nothing Feels Worth Doing)
- ☐ 5-minute walk outside
- ☐ Shower and get dressed
- ☐ Text one person
- ☐ Listen to one song
- ☐ Make tea/coffee mindfully
- ☐ Stretch for 3 minutes
- ☐ Look at old photos
- ☐ Cook or order a meal

**Remember:** Small counts. Done is better than perfect. Every step forward matters.
