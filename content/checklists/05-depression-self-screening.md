## Part 2: Understanding Your Score

| Score | Severity | Recommended Action |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| 0–4 | Minimal | Continue self-care practices. Monitor if things change. |
| 5–9 | Mild | Practice the self-help strategies below. Consider therapy if it persists. |
| 10–14 | Moderate | Therapy is recommended. Speak to a mental health professional. |
| 15–19 | Moderately Severe | Professional help is strongly recommended. Therapy and possibly medication. |
| 20–27 | Severe | Please seek professional help urgently. You don't have to face this alone. |

**If you scored 1 or more on question 9, please reach out to a professional regardless of your total score.**

## Part 4: What Depression Is and Isn't

### Depression IS:
- A medical condition involving brain chemistry
- Treatable (therapy, medication, or both)
- Common (1 in 5 people experience it)
- Not your fault
- Not a choice

### Depression is NOT:
- "Just being sad"
- Laziness or weakness
- Something you can "snap out of"
- A character flaw
- Something to be ashamed of

### In the Indian Context
- Depression is often dismissed as "thinking too much" or "just adjust"
- Physical symptoms (headaches, body pain, fatigue) are sometimes the primary presentation
- Family may not recognise it or may attribute it to nazar, karma, or life circumstances
- Seeking help is a sign of strength, not weakness

## Part 5: Immediate Self-Help Strategies

While these are not substitutes for professional help, they can provide some relief:

### Today
- [ ] Get out of bed and shower (even if it takes all your energy)
- [ ] Eat something nutritious
- [ ] Step outside for 10 minutes
- [ ] Text or call one person
- [ ] Do one small task (make your bed, wash a cup)

### This Week
- [ ] Establish a regular wake-up time
- [ ] Move your body for 15 minutes daily
- [ ] Limit alcohol and substance use
- [ ] Reduce social media consumption
- [ ] Write down one thing — even tiny — that went okay each day

### The "Bare Minimum" Rule
On your worst days, your only job is to:
1. Stay safe
2. Drink water
3. Eat something
4. Be gentle with yourself

That's enough. Tomorrow you can try for more.

## Part 6: Next Steps

Based on your score, choose your next action:

- [ ] **Minimal (0–4):** Bookmark this checklist and retake in 4 weeks
- [ ] **Mild (5–9):** Start the self-help strategies. Retake in 2 weeks. Consider therapy.
- [ ] **Moderate (10–14):** Book a session with a therapist this week
- [ ] **Moderately Severe (15–19):** Contact a mental health professional today
- [ ] **Severe (20–27):** Reach out for help now — you deserve support
