## Self-Compassion Assessment — Check all that apply:

### Where Are You Now?
- ☐ I am harder on myself than on anyone else
- ☐ I don't allow myself to make mistakes without harsh self-criticism
- ☐ I feel I don't deserve kindness or rest
- ☐ I dismiss my own pain ("Others have it worse")
- ☐ I struggle to comfort myself when I'm hurting
- ☐ I believe being tough on myself is what keeps me going
- ☐ I feel uncomfortable receiving care or compliments
- ☐ I hold myself to standards I'd never impose on a friend

**If 4+ checked:** You're running on self-criticism, not self-compassion. That fuel eventually runs out.

## Daily Self-Compassion Practices

### Morning (Pick 1)
- ☐ Place hand on heart and say: "May I be kind to myself today"
- ☐ Write 1 thing you appreciate about yourself
- ☐ Set an intention: "Today I will speak to myself like I would to a friend"

### During Difficulty (When Something Goes Wrong)
- ☐ **Mindfulness:** "This is a moment of suffering. This hurts."
- ☐ **Common humanity:** "Others feel this way too. I'm not alone."
- ☐ **Self-kindness:** "May I give myself the compassion I need."

### Evening (Pick 1)
- ☐ Write down one hard thing you did today (acknowledge it)
- ☐ Forgive yourself for one mistake
- ☐ Name one way you showed up for yourself today

## The Self-Compassion Break (Use Anytime)

1. Notice the pain: "This is hard right now"
2. Remember you're human: "Everyone struggles sometimes"
3. Offer kindness: "May I be gentle with myself" / Place hands on heart
