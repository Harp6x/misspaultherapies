## Check all that apply since your move:

### Emotional
- ☐ Homesickness (missing people, places, routines)
- ☐ Grief for the life you left behind
- ☐ Excitement mixed with anxiety
- ☐ Loneliness despite new environment
- ☐ Irritability or mood swings
- ☐ Regret about the decision to move
- ☐ Feeling like an outsider
- ☐ Loss of confidence in unfamiliar surroundings

### Practical
- ☐ Difficulty navigating new city/systems
- ☐ Language or cultural barriers
- ☐ Financial stress from the move
- ☐ Work adjustment challenges
- ☐ Children struggling to settle (if applicable)
- ☐ Partner relationship strained by the transition

### Social
- ☐ No local support system yet
- ☐ Difficulty making new friends as an adult
- ☐ Missing your community (religious, cultural, social)
- ☐ Spouse/partner adjusting differently than you
- ☐ Old friendships fading due to distance

## Timeline of Adjustment (Normal)

| Phase | Duration | What to Expect |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| Honeymoon | 0–3 months | Excitement, novelty, exploration |
| Culture shock | 3–6 months | Frustration, homesickness, comparison |
| Adjustment | 6–12 months | Gradual comfort, new routines forming |
| Acceptance | 12+ months | Feeling at home, hybrid identity |

## If You're Stuck in Culture Shock Phase Beyond 6 Months

Therapy can help you process the loss, build resilience, and create belonging in your new environment.
