# Vicarious Trauma & Compassion Fatigue — Caregiver Checklist
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

> 📱 @mspaultherapies | 🌐 mspaultherapies.com | 📧 mspaultherapies@gmail.com

**For:** Therapists, counsellors, doctors, nurses, social workers, teachers, caregivers, and anyone who absorbs others' pain.

---

## Check all that apply (past month):

### Emotional Signs
- ☐ Feeling emotionally drained or depleted after helping others
- ☐ Cynicism or negativity about your work or the people you help
- ☐ Feeling helpless or hopeless about making a difference
- ☐ Emotional numbness or detachment
- ☐ Increased irritability or frustration
- ☐ Feeling personally responsible for others' outcomes
- ☐ Guilt about taking time off or doing things for yourself
- ☐ Crying more easily than usual

### Cognitive Signs
- ☐ Intrusive thoughts about clients'/patients'/loved ones' trauma
- ☐ Difficulty separating your feelings from those you care for
- ☐ Hypervigilance about safety (yours or others')
- ☐ Nightmares or disturbing dreams
- ☐ Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
- ☐ Changed worldview ("People are awful," "The world is dangerous")

### Physical Signs
- ☐ Chronic fatigue even with adequate rest
- ☐ Headaches, stomach problems, or muscle tension
- ☐ Weakened immune system (frequent colds)
- ☐ Changes in appetite or sleep patterns
- ☐ Reliance on substances to cope (alcohol, caffeine, medication)

### Behavioural Signs
- ☐ Withdrawing from social activities
- ☐ Dreading going to work
- ☐ Boundary violations (overworking, taking on too much)
- ☐ Neglecting your own self-care
- ☐ Reducing quality of care you provide

## Scoring

| Checked | Level | Action |
|---------|-------|--------|
| 0–5 | Mild wear | Increase self-care, maintain boundaries |
| 6–12 | Moderate compassion fatigue | Supervision + personal therapy recommended |
| 13–18 | Significant vicarious trauma | Professional support urgently needed |
| 19+ | Severe | Take leave if possible; seek immediate help |

## Recovery Strategies for Caregivers

1. **Professional supervision or therapy** (you need a space to process too)
2. **Clear boundaries** between work and personal life
3. **Regular breaks** — microbreaks during the day, proper time off
4. **Body-based practices** — yoga, exercise, dance, massage
5. **Community** — connect with peers who understand
6. **Meaning-making** — reconnect with why you do this work
