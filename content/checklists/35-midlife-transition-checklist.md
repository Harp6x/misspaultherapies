## Check all that resonate:

### Identity & Purpose
- ☐ Questioning your life choices ("Did I choose the right path?")
- ☐ Feeling like your identity is tied only to your roles (parent, spouse, employee)
- ☐ Desire to rediscover who you are beyond responsibilities
- ☐ Mourning opportunities you didn't take
- ☐ Feeling invisible or undervalued

### Career
- ☐ Boredom or disillusionment with your career
- ☐ Desire to make a dramatic career change
- ☐ Fear of being replaced by younger colleagues
- ☐ Peak responsibility with peak exhaustion
- ☐ Wondering "Is this worth it?"

### Relationships
- ☐ Feeling disconnected from your partner
- ☐ Children becoming independent — not sure who you are without parenting
- ☐ Navigating ageing parents' care
- ☐ Friendships have thinned — feeling lonely
- ☐ Reassessing whether your relationship still serves you

### Body & Health
- ☐ Physical changes that affect self-image (weight, menopause, grey hair)
- ☐ Health anxiety increasing with age
- ☐ Lower energy than before
- ☐ Sleep quality declining
- ☐ Confronting mortality for the first time

### Emotions
- ☐ Unexpected sadness or grief about time passing
- ☐ Irritability or restlessness
- ☐ Nostalgia and romanticising the past
- ☐ Feeling trapped by obligations
- ☐ Desire for more authenticity and less performance

## This Isn't a "Crisis" — It's a Reckoning

Midlife transitions are an invitation to realign your life with your current values — not the values you inherited at 20. It's uncomfortable but deeply meaningful.

| Checked | Interpretation |
|---------|---------------|
| 0–6 | Normal midlife reflection |
| 7–14 | Active transition — journaling, therapy, or coaching recommended |
| 15+ | Significant distress — therapy strongly recommended |
