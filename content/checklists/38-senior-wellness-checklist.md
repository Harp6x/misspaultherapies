## Check all that apply:

### Emotional Wellbeing
- ☐ Feeling lonely or isolated
- ☐ Loss of interest in activities once enjoyed
- ☐ Persistent sadness or tearfulness
- ☐ Feeling like a burden to family
- ☐ Grief over friends or spouse who have passed
- ☐ Anxiety about health, finances, or dependence
- ☐ Fear of death or dying
- ☐ Feeling invisible or irrelevant in family/society

### Physical-Mental Connection
- ☐ Chronic pain affecting mood
- ☐ Sleep disturbances (insomnia, early waking)
- ☐ Memory concerns or cognitive fog
- ☐ Reduced mobility affecting independence
- ☐ Medication side effects impacting mood
- ☐ Loss of appetite

### Social & Role Changes
- ☐ Retirement adjustment difficulties
- ☐ Loss of daily structure and purpose
- ☐ Reduced social circle
- ☐ Dependence on children for daily needs
- ☐ Feeling of losing control over life decisions
- ☐ Technology gap creating isolation

### Indian Context
- ☐ Feeling neglected in a nuclear family setup
- ☐ Children abroad with limited contact
- ☐ Loss of respect or authority in the family
- ☐ Widowhood and its social implications
- ☐ Stigma about mental health being "just old age"

## Scoring

| Checked | Level | Action |
|---------|-------|--------|
| 0–5 | Normal ageing adjustments | Social engagement + self-care |
| 6–12 | Moderate distress | Family support + consider counselling |
| 13+ | Significant | Professional support recommended |

## For Family Members: How to Support

1. **Visit regularly** (or call daily if distant)
2. **Include them in decisions** about their own life
3. **Ask about their feelings** — don't assume they're "fine"
4. **Encourage activities** — hobbies, spirituality, social clubs
5. **Watch for depression signs** — often masked as physical complaints
6. **Consider therapy** — it's never too late to benefit from talking to someone
