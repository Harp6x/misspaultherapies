## "I'm Fine" — But Are You Really?

High-functioning depression means you keep going — work, relationships, responsibilities — but something feels consistently off. Check all that apply:

- ☐ You feel low most days but "push through"
- ☐ You're productive but find no joy in achievements
- ☐ You're always tired, no matter how much you sleep
- ☐ You go through the motions without feeling engaged
- ☐ You're the "strong one" everyone relies on but you're crumbling inside
- ☐ You use busyness to avoid sitting with how you feel
- ☐ You feel guilty for being depressed when your life "looks good"
- ☐ You've lost interest in hobbies but haven't replaced them with anything
- ☐ You isolate subtly — declining plans, shorter conversations
- ☐ You overwork, over-exercise, or over-give to feel "useful"
- ☐ Your inner critic is relentless
- ☐ You struggle to make decisions, even small ones
- ☐ You feel empty rather than sad
- ☐ You wonder "Is this all there is?"
- ☐ You can't remember the last time you felt genuinely happy
- ☐ You've felt this way for months or years (not just a bad week)
- ☐ Others would be surprised to know how you really feel
- ☐ You've thought about therapy but feel you're "not bad enough" to go

## Why It's Missed

High-functioning depression flies under the radar because:
- You still perform well at work/school
- You maintain relationships (even if superficially)
- You don't "look" depressed
- Indian culture rewards stoicism and self-sacrifice
- You may have been this way so long it feels "normal"

## Scoring

| Checked | Interpretation |
|---------|---------------|
| 0–4 | Normal fluctuations in mood |
| 5–9 | Subclinical — self-care and monitoring recommended |
| 10–14 | Likely persistent depressive symptoms — therapy recommended |
| 15–18 | Significant depression masked by functioning — professional help needed |

## The Truth

You don't need to be in crisis to deserve help. If 10+ items resonate, therapy can help you move from surviving to thriving.
