# OCD (Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder) — Awareness Checklist

**Note:** OCD is NOT about being "neat" or "organised." It involves distressing, intrusive thoughts and repetitive behaviours aimed at reducing anxiety.

---

## Common Obsessions (Unwanted Intrusive Thoughts)

- ☐ Fear of contamination (germs, dirt, chemicals, illness)
- ☐ Fear of harming yourself or others (even though you don't want to)
- ☐ Disturbing sexual or violent intrusive thoughts
- ☐ Fear of saying something offensive or inappropriate
- ☐ Excessive concern with symmetry, order, or "rightness"
- ☐ Religious/moral obsessions (scrupulosity) — fear of sinning or being punished
- ☐ Fear of losing important things
- ☐ Obsessive doubt ("Did I lock the door? Did I turn off the stove?")
- ☐ Fear of being responsible for something terrible happening
- ☐ Need to know or remember things perfectly

## Common Compulsions (Repetitive Behaviours or Mental Acts)

- ☐ Excessive hand-washing, cleaning, or sanitising
- ☐ Checking (locks, stoves, switches) repeatedly
- ☐ Counting, tapping, or repeating actions a specific number of times
- ☐ Arranging items until they feel "just right"
- ☐ Seeking reassurance from others ("Are you sure it's okay?")
- ☐ Mental rituals (praying, reviewing, counting in your head)
- ☐ Avoiding triggers (places, people, objects, numbers)
- ☐ Confessing thoughts to relieve guilt
- ☐ Hoarding items "just in case"
- ☐ Re-reading, re-writing, or re-doing tasks

## Impact Assessment

- ☐ Obsessions/compulsions take up 1+ hours per day
- ☐ They cause significant distress or anxiety
- ☐ They interfere with work, school, or relationships
- ☐ You know the thoughts are irrational but can't stop
- ☐ Avoiding rituals causes extreme discomfort
- ☐ You hide your rituals from others

## OCD in the Indian Context

- Religious rituals may mask OCD (excessive pooja, fasting, ritual purity)
- "Superstitious" behaviours may actually be OCD compulsions
- Perfectionism in academics/career normalised as "dedication"
- Contamination OCD amplified post-COVID
- Family may participate in rituals unknowingly (accommodation)

## Scoring Guide

| Checked | Interpretation |
|---------|---------------|
| 0–4 | Some intrusive thoughts (normal for most people) |
| 5–10 | Possible OCD tendencies — self-help + monitoring |
| 11–16 | Likely OCD — therapy (ERP) strongly recommended |
| 17+ | Significant OCD — professional treatment essential |

## Gold Standard Treatment

**ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention)** is the most effective therapy for OCD. It involves gradually facing feared situations without performing the compulsion.
