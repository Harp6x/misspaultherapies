**Note:** PTSD can develop after experiencing or witnessing a traumatic event — accident, assault, abuse, natural disaster, war, medical trauma, sudden loss.

---

## Have you experienced a traumatic event and now notice any of the following? (Past month)

### Re-Experiencing
- ☐ Intrusive memories of the event that come uninvited
- ☐ Nightmares about the trauma
- ☐ Flashbacks — feeling like the event is happening again
- ☐ Intense distress when reminded of the event
- ☐ Physical reactions to reminders (racing heart, sweating, shaking)

### Avoidance
- ☐ Avoiding thoughts, feelings, or conversations about the event
- ☐ Avoiding places, people, or activities that remind you
- ☐ Feeling emotionally numb or detached
- ☐ Unable to remember important parts of the event
- ☐ Avoiding driving, travelling, or specific locations

### Negative Changes in Thinking & Mood
- ☐ Persistent negative beliefs ("The world is dangerous," "I'm broken")
- ☐ Blaming yourself for what happened
- ☐ Persistent shame, guilt, fear, or anger
- ☐ Loss of interest in activities you used to enjoy
- ☐ Feeling detached or estranged from others
- ☐ Inability to experience positive emotions (love, joy, satisfaction)
- ☐ Feeling like your future is shortened

### Hyperarousal
- ☐ Being easily startled or jumpy
- ☐ Feeling constantly on guard (hypervigilance)
- ☐ Difficulty sleeping or staying asleep
- ☐ Irritability or angry outbursts
- ☐ Difficulty concentrating
- ☐ Reckless or self-destructive behaviour

## Scoring

| Checked | Interpretation |
|---------|---------------|
| 0–5 | Normal stress response — may resolve naturally |
| 6–12 | Subthreshold PTSD — monitoring + self-care recommended |
| 13–18 | Possible PTSD — therapy strongly recommended |
| 19+ | Likely PTSD — professional trauma therapy needed |

## Indian Context

Trauma in India is often:
- Normalised ("It happened, move on")
- Hidden due to family honour (especially sexual trauma)
- Reframed spiritually ("It's your karma")
- Dismissed as "being too sensitive"

**None of these responses are helpful. Trauma is real and treatable.**

## Evidence-Based Treatments for PTSD

- **EMDR** (Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing)
- **Trauma-Focused CBT**
- **Prolonged Exposure Therapy**
- **Somatic Experiencing**
