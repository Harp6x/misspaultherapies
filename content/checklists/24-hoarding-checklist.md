# Hoarding Behaviour — Self-Assessment Checklist

> 📱 @mspaultherapies | 🌐 mspaultherapies.com | 📧 mspaultherapies@gmail.com

---

## Check all that apply:

- ☐ Difficulty discarding possessions regardless of their value
- ☐ Strong distress at the thought of getting rid of items
- ☐ Accumulation of items that clutter living areas
- ☐ Living spaces can't be used for their intended purpose
- ☐ Keeping items "just in case" they're needed someday
- ☐ Feeling emotionally attached to objects (guilt about discarding)
- ☐ Difficulty organising possessions
- ☐ Acquiring more items than you have space for
- ☐ Avoiding having people visit due to clutter
- ☐ Feeling overwhelmed by the volume of possessions
- ☐ Conflict with family members about clutter
- ☐ Clutter affecting hygiene, safety, or health
- ☐ Financial problems due to excessive acquiring
- ☐ Emotional distress when others touch or move your things

## Impact Level

| Checked | Level | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|---------------|
| 0–3 | Clutter tendency | Organisational strategies sufficient |
| 4–7 | Moderate hoarding | Self-help + gradual decluttering plan |
| 8–11 | Significant hoarding | Therapy recommended (CBT for hoarding) |
| 12+ | Severe | Professional support + family involvement needed |
