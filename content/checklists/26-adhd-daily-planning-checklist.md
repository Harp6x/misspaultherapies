## Morning Launch Pad (Before Starting Your Day)

- ☐ Wake up at consistent time (set 2 alarms if needed)
- ☐ Take medication (if applicable)
- ☐ Review today's 3 non-negotiable tasks (decide the night before)
- ☐ Set phone to Do Not Disturb for first focus block
- ☐ Eat breakfast (even something small — brain needs fuel)
- ☐ Check calendar for appointments/deadlines
- ☐ Place keys, wallet, phone, bag in their designated spot

## During the Day

### Focus Management
- ☐ Use Pomodoro: 25 min work → 5 min break → repeat
- ☐ Work on hardest task during your peak energy window
- ☐ Keep a "capture pad" nearby for random thoughts (write, don't act)
- ☐ One tab, one task at a time (close everything else)
- ☐ Use body doubling (work alongside someone, virtual or in person)

### Time Awareness
- ☐ Set timers for transitions ("Leave in 15 minutes")
- ☐ Build in buffer time between tasks (10-15 min)
- ☐ Use visual timers (Time Timer app or physical timer)
- ☐ Check clock every 30 minutes (set recurring alarm)

### Emotional Regulation
- ☐ Take movement breaks every 60-90 minutes
- ☐ Notice frustration early — pause before reacting
- ☐ Use "5-second rule" — count down 5-4-3-2-1 then start
- ☐ Drink water throughout the day (dehydration worsens focus)

## Evening Wind-Down

- ☐ Review what you accomplished today (celebrate, don't criticise)
- ☐ Identify tomorrow's 3 non-negotiable tasks
- ☐ Set out clothes, bag, essentials for tomorrow
- ☐ 30-minute screen-free wind-down before bed
- ☐ Consistent bedtime (sleep is non-negotiable for ADHD)

## Weekly Reset (Sunday Evening — 15 min)

- ☐ Review upcoming week's calendar
- ☐ Identify week's top 3 priorities
- ☐ Meal plan or prep (reduces daily decision fatigue)
- ☐ Tidy workspace for Monday
- ☐ Celebrate one win from last week
