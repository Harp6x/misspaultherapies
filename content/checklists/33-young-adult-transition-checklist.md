## Check all that resonate with you right now:

### Identity & Direction
- ☐ Feeling lost about what you want to do with your life
- ☐ Comparing your timeline to friends' milestones
- ☐ Pressure to have it "figured out" by a certain age
- ☐ Values conflict with family expectations
- ☐ Questioning beliefs you were raised with
- ☐ Struggling to find meaning or purpose

### Career & Finances
- ☐ Job search anxiety or rejection fatigue
- ☐ First job overwhelm (imposter syndrome, workplace dynamics)
- ☐ Financial stress (student loans, low starting salary, family expectations)
- ☐ Career path doesn't match your passion but pays the bills
- ☐ Burnout already — and your career just started

### Relationships
- ☐ Navigating first serious romantic relationships
- ☐ Family pressure about marriage/settling down
- ☐ Friendship dynamics changing as everyone's lives diverge
- ☐ Setting boundaries with parents for the first time
- ☐ Loneliness in a new city or after college ends

### Independence
- ☐ Living alone for the first time — mixed feelings
- ☐ Adulting overwhelm (cooking, cleaning, bills, health)
- ☐ Missing the structure of school/college
- ☐ Decision fatigue — too many choices, no clear answers
- ☐ Feeling like everyone else is handling it better

### Mental Health
- ☐ Anxiety has increased since leaving the structure of education
- ☐ Low mood or depression emerging or worsening
- ☐ Using alcohol, substances, or social media excessively to cope
- ☐ Difficulty sleeping due to racing thoughts about the future
- ☐ Loss of confidence or self-esteem

## This Is Normal — And You Deserve Support

The 18–25 transition is one of the most psychologically demanding periods. Your brain is still developing. Everything is changing simultaneously. It's okay to not have it figured out.

| Checked | Suggestion |
|---------|-----------|
| 0–5 | Normal growing pains — self-care + community |
| 6–12 | Elevated stress — consider therapy or mentorship |
| 13+ | Significant distress — professional support recommended |
