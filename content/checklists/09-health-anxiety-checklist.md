## Do You Recognise These Patterns?

Check all that apply over the past 6 months:

### Thoughts
- ☐ Frequently worry that you have a serious illness
- ☐ Interpret normal body sensations as signs of disease
- ☐ Believe that a headache could be a brain tumour
- ☐ Think stomach discomfort means cancer
- ☐ Worry that minor symptoms will get worse
- ☐ Can't stop thinking about your health even when reassured
- ☐ Believe doctors are missing something

### Behaviours
- ☐ Repeatedly check your body for lumps, marks, or changes
- ☐ Google symptoms frequently (3+ times per week)
- ☐ Seek reassurance from family, friends, or doctors
- ☐ Visit multiple doctors for the same concern
- ☐ Request unnecessary tests or scans
- ☐ Avoid health information (or compulsively consume it)
- ☐ Avoid exercise or activities fearing they'll worsen symptoms
- ☐ Take your temperature, pulse, or blood pressure repeatedly

### Impact
- ☐ Spend 1+ hours per day thinking about your health
- ☐ Health worries interfere with work or daily activities
- ☐ Reassurance from doctors only helps temporarily
- ☐ You feel anxious even when test results are normal
- ☐ Relationships are strained because of health concerns
- ☐ You've reduced activities due to health fears

## Scoring

| Checked | Level | Action |
|---------|-------|--------|
| 0–5 | Normal health awareness | Maintain regular check-ups |
| 6–12 | Elevated health anxiety | Self-help + mindfulness recommended |
| 13–18 | Moderate health anxiety | Therapy (CBT) recommended |
| 19+ | Significant health anxiety | Professional support strongly advised |

## The Health Anxiety Cycle

```
Trigger (body sensation or health news)
    ↓
Catastrophic interpretation ("This must be serious")
    ↓
Anxiety increases → more body scanning
    ↓
Checking/Googling/doctor visits for reassurance
    ↓
Brief relief → anxiety returns → cycle repeats
```

## Breaking the Cycle — 3 Key Strategies

1. **Delay checking:** When you feel the urge to Google or body-check, wait 30 minutes. The urge often passes.
2. **Label the thought:** "This is my health anxiety talking, not a medical fact."
3. **Limit reassurance:** Agree with yourself to check with a doctor only once per concern. Trust the result.
