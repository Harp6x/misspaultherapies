## Rate Each Friendship (Think of 3-5 Close Friends)

For each friend, check what applies:

### Healthy Signs
- ☐ I feel safe being myself around them
- ☐ They celebrate my wins genuinely
- ☐ They show up during hard times
- ☐ Communication is balanced (both share and listen)
- ☐ They respect my boundaries
- ☐ I feel energised after spending time with them
- ☐ They accept me even when we disagree

### Unhealthy Signs
- ☐ I feel drained after interacting with them
- ☐ The friendship feels one-sided (I give, they take)
- ☐ They gossip about others to me (probably about me too)
- ☐ They're competitive or envious of my achievements
- ☐ Passive-aggressive behaviour or backhanded compliments
- ☐ They dismiss my feelings or struggles
- ☐ I walk on eggshells around them
- ☐ They only contact me when they need something
- ☐ Guilt-tripping when I'm unavailable

## Friendship Self-Check

- ☐ I have at least one person I can call at 2 AM
- ☐ I feel I have enough social connection in my life
- ☐ I invest time in maintaining friendships
- ☐ I have friends outside my romantic relationship
- ☐ I can be vulnerable with at least one friend
- ☐ I set boundaries in friendships without excessive guilt

## If You're Lonely

Adult friendship is hard — especially after college, marriage, or relocation. You're not alone in feeling alone. Therapy can help you understand patterns, build social skills, and address loneliness.
