## Do You Notice Mood Changes With the Seasons?

Check symptoms you experience during specific seasons (typically monsoon/winter in India):

### Energy & Sleep
- ☐ Sleeping much more than usual (hypersomnia)
- ☐ Difficulty getting out of bed despite adequate sleep
- ☐ Feeling sluggish, heavy, or "weighed down"
- ☐ Low energy throughout the day
- ☐ Craving carbohydrates, sweets, or comfort food
- ☐ Weight gain during specific months

### Mood & Motivation
- ☐ Persistent low mood for weeks during a particular season
- ☐ Loss of interest in activities you normally enjoy
- ☐ Withdrawing from social plans
- ☐ Difficulty concentrating at work or studies
- ☐ Feeling hopeless or pessimistic
- ☐ Irritability or frustration more easily triggered

### Pattern Recognition
- ☐ These symptoms happen around the same time every year
- ☐ Symptoms last 2+ weeks each episode
- ☐ You feel significantly better when the season changes
- ☐ This pattern has occurred for 2+ consecutive years
- ☐ Symptoms are not explained by other life events

## Indian Context: Monsoon SAD

In India, SAD often manifests during:
- **Monsoon season** (July–September): Reduced sunlight, humidity, indoor confinement
- **Winter months** (November–January): Shorter days in northern India
- **Post-festival lull** (November–December): After Diwali excitement fades

## Self-Help Strategies

1. **Light exposure:** Spend 20–30 minutes outdoors in morning sunlight daily
2. **Exercise:** Even 20 minutes of movement significantly improves mood
3. **Social connection:** Schedule regular plans to counter withdrawal urge
4. **Routine:** Maintain consistent sleep/wake times regardless of weather
5. **Vitamin D:** Get levels checked — deficiency is common in India

## When to Seek Help

If symptoms significantly impact your daily functioning for 2+ weeks during a specific season, therapy can help. CBT adapted for seasonal depression has strong evidence.
