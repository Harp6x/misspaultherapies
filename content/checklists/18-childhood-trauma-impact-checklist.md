## As a Child, Did You Experience Any of the Following?

- ☐ Physical abuse (hitting, slapping, beating beyond normal discipline)
- ☐ Emotional abuse (constant criticism, humiliation, name-calling)
- ☐ Sexual abuse or inappropriate touching
- ☐ Neglect (basic needs not met — food, safety, emotional attention)
- ☐ Witnessing domestic violence between parents/caregivers
- ☐ Parent with addiction (alcohol, drugs, gambling)
- ☐ Parent with untreated mental illness
- ☐ Parental separation, divorce, or abandonment
- ☐ Loss of a parent or sibling
- ☐ Bullying (at school, within family, community)
- ☐ Growing up in an environment of constant fear or unpredictability
- ☐ Being parentified (taking care of parents/siblings as a child)
- ☐ Excessive academic pressure with punishment for failure

## As an Adult, Do You Now Experience?

### Relationships
- ☐ Difficulty trusting others
- ☐ Fear of abandonment or clinginess
- ☐ Choosing partners who are emotionally unavailable or abusive
- ☐ People-pleasing or inability to say no
- ☐ Difficulty maintaining close relationships
- ☐ Fear of intimacy (emotional or physical)

### Self-Perception
- ☐ Low self-worth or feeling fundamentally "broken"
- ☐ Persistent shame (not about what you did, but who you are)
- ☐ Perfectionism driven by fear of punishment or rejection
- ☐ Harsh inner critic
- ☐ Feeling like you don't deserve good things

### Emotional Patterns
- ☐ Difficulty identifying or expressing emotions
- ☐ Emotional flashbacks (sudden waves of childhood feelings without clear trigger)
- ☐ Dissociation (zoning out, feeling disconnected from your body)
- ☐ Intense reactions to minor triggers
- ☐ Chronic anxiety or hypervigilance
- ☐ Depression that doesn't respond to typical treatment alone

### Coping Behaviours
- ☐ Self-harm or self-destructive patterns
- ☐ Substance use to numb feelings
- ☐ Overworking or over-achieving to prove worth
- ☐ Disordered eating
- ☐ Difficulty with sleep (nightmares, insomnia)

## This Is Not Your Fault

Childhood trauma shapes your nervous system, attachment style, and beliefs about yourself and the world. These are adaptations that helped you survive — but they may no longer serve you.

Healing is possible. Trauma-informed therapy can help you:
- Understand the connection between past and present
- Process painful memories safely
- Develop secure attachment patterns
- Build self-compassion and self-worth
