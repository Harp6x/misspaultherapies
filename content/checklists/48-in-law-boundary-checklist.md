## Are Your In-Law Relationships Causing Stress?

- ☐ Your in-laws make decisions about your life without consulting you
- ☐ Constant unsolicited advice about parenting, cooking, career, or lifestyle
- ☐ Criticism disguised as "concern" or "tradition"
- ☐ Your spouse doesn't stand up for you with their parents
- ☐ Feeling like a guest (or servant) in your own home
- ☐ Privacy violations (entering your room, reading your messages)
- ☐ Financial interference or control
- ☐ Comparison with other bahus/damads in the family
- ☐ Emotional manipulation through guilt trips
- ☐ Cultural expectations overriding your personal comfort
- ☐ Arguments with your spouse triggered by in-law dynamics
- ☐ Feeling like your spouse chose their parents over you

## Boundary-Setting Scripts (Indian Context)

### With Your Spouse (First Priority)
- "I need us to be a team. Can we agree on boundaries together before talking to your parents?"
- "I respect your parents. I also need you to respect our marriage. Both can coexist."

### With In-Laws (Gentle but Firm)
- "Thank you for your concern. We've decided to handle this ourselves."
- "I appreciate the advice. We'll think about it and let you know."
- "I'm not comfortable with that. I hope you understand."

### For Yourself
- "I am allowed to have boundaries without being a 'bad' bahu/damad"
- "Setting limits is not disrespect — it's self-preservation"
- "My marriage is the primary unit, not an extension of my in-laws' family"

## When to Seek Help

If in-law dynamics are causing: persistent anxiety, marital conflict, depression, or feeling trapped — couples therapy can help you and your spouse navigate this together.
