## Check all that apply to your workplace:

### Culture
- ☐ Constant gossip, backstabbing, or blame culture
- ☐ Unrealistic expectations with no support
- ☐ Micromanagement or lack of autonomy
- ☐ Credit stolen for your work
- ☐ Fear-based management ("Be grateful you have a job")
- ☐ Discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, or background
- ☐ Normalised overwork (leaving on time is frowned upon)
- ☐ High turnover that's never addressed

### Your Boss/Manager
- ☐ Publicly humiliates or criticises you
- ☐ Sets impossible deadlines then blames you
- ☐ Plays favourites
- ☐ Takes credit for your ideas
- ☐ Gaslights ("I never said that")
- ☐ Makes you feel replaceable or worthless

### Your Experience
- ☐ Sunday evening dread every week
- ☐ Physical symptoms before/during work (headaches, nausea, chest tightness)
- ☐ Dreading opening your inbox or work chat
- ☐ Crying because of work regularly
- ☐ Your confidence has declined since joining
- ☐ Work stress bleeding into relationships and sleep
- ☐ Using alcohol or food to decompress after work
- ☐ Fantasising about quitting daily

## Scoring

| Checked | Level | Action |
|---------|-------|--------|
| 0–5 | Normal workplace stress | Coping strategies + boundaries |
| 6–12 | Toxic environment | Therapy + active boundary-setting or exit planning |
| 13+ | Severely toxic | Prioritise your mental health — consider leaving |

## What You Can Control

1. Document everything (emails, messages, incidents)
2. Set boundaries on your time and energy
3. Build a network outside your current workplace
4. Therapy to process the impact and plan next steps
5. Know your rights (labour laws, HR policies, POSH Act)
