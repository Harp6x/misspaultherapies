## How often do you feel... (Never / Rarely / Sometimes / Often / Always)

- ☐ That you lack companionship
- ☐ Left out of social activities
- ☐ Isolated from others
- ☐ That no one really knows you
- ☐ That you have no one to turn to
- ☐ That people around you are "there" but not really connected
- ☐ Lonely even when surrounded by people
- ☐ That your relationships are superficial
- ☐ Misunderstood by the people in your life
- ☐ That you've outgrown your current social circle
- ☐ Exhausted by the effort of maintaining social connections
- ☐ That social media makes you feel more alone, not less

## Types of Loneliness — Which Resonates?

- ☐ **Emotional loneliness:** Missing a deep, intimate connection (partner, best friend)
- ☐ **Social loneliness:** Missing a group or community (belonging)
- ☐ **Existential loneliness:** Feeling fundamentally alone in your experience of life
- ☐ **Transitional loneliness:** Loneliness from life change (move, breakup, job change)

## Contributing Factors
- ☐ Recent relocation
- ☐ End of a relationship
- ☐ Working from home / remote work
- ☐ Social anxiety making connection difficult
- ☐ Busy schedule leaving no time for social life
- ☐ Difficulty trusting people
- ☐ Feeling "different" from peers

## Small Steps Forward

1. **One genuine connection per day** (even a text counts)
2. **Join something** — class, group, volunteer org, community
3. **Reduce social media** — it amplifies loneliness
4. **Be vulnerable first** — depth requires someone going first
5. **Therapy** — address underlying patterns that block connection
