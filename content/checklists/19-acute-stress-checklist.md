## After a Stressful or Traumatic Event (Within 3 Days to 1 Month)

Check all that apply:

### Intrusion Symptoms
- ☐ Recurring, involuntary memories of the event
- ☐ Distressing dreams related to the event
- ☐ Feeling as if the event is recurring (flashbacks)
- ☐ Intense distress at reminders of the event

### Negative Mood
- ☐ Inability to feel positive emotions (happiness, satisfaction, love)
- ☐ Feeling detached from the world or emotions

### Dissociative Symptoms
- ☐ Altered sense of reality (things feel dreamlike or unreal)
- ☐ Unable to remember important aspects of the event
- ☐ Feeling detached from your body
- ☐ Time distortion (hours feel like minutes or vice versa)

### Avoidance
- ☐ Avoiding memories, thoughts, or feelings about the event
- ☐ Avoiding external reminders (people, places, situations)

### Arousal
- ☐ Sleep disturbance
- ☐ Irritability or anger outbursts
- ☐ Hypervigilance (constantly on alert)
- ☐ Difficulty concentrating
- ☐ Exaggerated startle response

## What's Normal vs. What Needs Attention

| Timeframe | What to Expect |
|-----------|---------------|
| 0–3 days | Shock, disbelief, emotional numbness — NORMAL |
| 3–30 days | Symptoms above are common — monitor closely |
| 30+ days | If symptoms persist or worsen → seek professional help |

## Immediate Self-Care After Acute Stress

1. **Safety first:** Ensure you are physically safe
2. **Connect:** Be with trusted people — don't isolate
3. **Basic needs:** Eat, drink water, sleep (even if difficult)
4. **Limit media:** Reduce exposure to news/social media about the event
5. **Gentle movement:** Walk, stretch — discharge nervous system activation
6. **Allow emotions:** Crying, anger, confusion are all normal responses
7. **Don't make major decisions:** Give yourself at least a week
