## Check all that apply since your child left home:

- ☐ Overwhelming sadness or grief
- ☐ Feeling purposeless or lost
- ☐ Not knowing what to do with the time and space
- ☐ Constantly checking on your child (calls, texts, tracking)
- ☐ Difficulty letting go of the parenting role
- ☐ Relationship with spouse feels unfamiliar or strained
- ☐ Realising you've neglected your own interests for years
- ☐ Loneliness even when your partner is present
- ☐ Anxiety about your child's safety and independence
- ☐ Feeling unneeded or irrelevant
- ☐ Crying unexpectedly at reminders (their room, old photos)
- ☐ Identity confusion ("Who am I if not a parent?")
- ☐ Envy of parents with younger children
- ☐ Physical symptoms (fatigue, aches, poor sleep)
- ☐ Marital tension now that the "buffer" of children is gone

## Indian Context

- Parenting in India is deeply identity-defining, especially for mothers
- Joint family to nuclear transition amplifies the loss
- Children moving abroad for studies/work adds geographical grief
- Cultural expectation to "be happy" for your child's success
- Limited conversation about empty nest as a real emotional experience

## Scoring

| Checked | Interpretation |
|---------|---------------|
| 0–4 | Normal adjustment — give yourself time |
| 5–9 | Moderate empty nest difficulty — intentional rebuilding recommended |
| 10+ | Significant distress — therapy can help you navigate this transition |

## Rebuilding: What Now?

1. Grieve the transition (it IS a loss — honour it)
2. Rediscover interests you shelved during parenting years
3. Invest in your partnership (date nights, shared goals)
4. Build or deepen friendships
5. Consider new purpose: volunteering, learning, mentoring
