## For Teens: How Are You Really Doing?

### Mood & Emotions
- ☐ I feel sad or empty most days
- ☐ I get angry or irritable easily
- ☐ I feel anxious or worried a lot
- ☐ I feel like no one understands me
- ☐ I cry more than I used to
- ☐ I feel numb — like nothing matters

### School & Focus
- ☐ My grades have dropped and I don't know why
- ☐ I can't concentrate in class
- ☐ I dread going to school
- ☐ Exams make me physically sick
- ☐ I procrastinate because I'm scared of failing

### Social Life
- ☐ I've been withdrawing from friends
- ☐ I feel left out or lonely
- ☐ I compare myself to others on social media constantly
- ☐ I've been bullied (in person or online)
- ☐ I don't enjoy things I used to love

### Body & Sleep
- ☐ I'm sleeping too much or too little
- ☐ My appetite has changed significantly
- ☐ I feel tired all the time
- ☐ I've been using substances (alcohol, smoking, drugs) to cope

### Serious Signs (Please Tell Someone)
- ☐ I've thought about hurting myself
- ☐ I've hurt myself on purpose
- ☐ I've thought about not wanting to be alive
- ☐ I feel like a burden to everyone

> ⚠️ If you checked any serious signs: **Please talk to a trusted adult, call 112, or reach Vandrevala Foundation: 1860-2662-345**

## For Parents: What to Watch For

- ☐ Sudden mood changes or irritability
- ☐ Academic decline
- ☐ Social withdrawal or new peer group
- ☐ Changes in sleep, appetite, or energy
- ☐ Secretive behaviour (beyond normal teen privacy)
- ☐ Loss of interest in activities
- ☐ Physical complaints without medical cause
- ☐ Self-harm marks (long sleeves in summer)

## What Parents Can Do

1. Ask: "How are you really doing?" — and listen without fixing
2. Validate: "That sounds really hard" > "You'll be fine"
3. Don't panic: Stay calm even if what they share is scary
4. Seek help: Adolescent therapy is confidential, safe, and effective
