## What Is an Emotional Flashback?

Unlike a visual flashback, you don't see the past event — you FEEL it. Suddenly you're flooded with the emotions of childhood: helplessness, fear, shame, or smallness. You may not know why.

## Am I Having an Emotional Flashback Right Now?

- ☐ I suddenly feel small, helpless, or like a child
- ☐ The intensity of my emotions doesn't match the current situation
- ☐ I feel ashamed, worthless, or "bad" for no clear reason
- ☐ I feel panicky, trapped, or like I need to escape
- ☐ My inner critic is attacking me mercilessly
- ☐ I feel like I'm in danger even though I'm physically safe
- ☐ I want to hide, freeze, or disappear
- ☐ I feel abandoned or alone even if people are around

## 13-Step Flashback Management Protocol

Use this IN THE MOMENT:

1. ☐ **Say to yourself:** "I am having an emotional flashback."
2. ☐ **Remind yourself:** "I feel afraid but I am not in danger right now."
3. ☐ **Own your right to have boundaries:** "I don't have to be perfect."
4. ☐ **Speak to your inner child:** "You are safe now. I am the adult and I will protect you."
5. ☐ **Soothe your body:** Place hand on heart, feel your feet on the floor.
6. ☐ **Slow your breathing:** Exhale longer than you inhale (4 in, 6 out).
7. ☐ **Orient to the present:** Name today's date, your age, where you are.
8. ☐ **Think of a safe person:** Someone who cares about you (real or imagined).
9. ☐ **Name the feeling:** "This is grief/shame/fear from the past, not the present."
10. ☐ **Allow the feeling:** Don't fight it. Feelings pass. Let the wave move through.
11. ☐ **Be patient:** Flashbacks can last minutes to hours. This WILL end.
12. ☐ **Resist the critic:** When your inner voice says "You're pathetic" — say "That's the flashback talking."
13. ☐ **Recover gently:** Water, warmth, rest, nature, a comforting activity.
