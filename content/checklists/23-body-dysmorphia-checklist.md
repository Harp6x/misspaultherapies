## Check all that apply:

### Preoccupation
- ☐ Spending 1+ hours daily thinking about perceived flaws in your appearance
- ☐ Focusing on specific body parts (skin, nose, hair, weight, teeth, jawline)
- ☐ Believing a flaw is obvious to others (even when they can't see it)
- ☐ Feeling "ugly" or "deformed" despite reassurance
- ☐ Comparing your appearance to others constantly

### Behaviours
- ☐ Checking mirrors excessively (or avoiding them entirely)
- ☐ Taking selfies to check/monitor your appearance
- ☐ Excessive grooming, makeup application, or skin-picking
- ☐ Seeking reassurance about your appearance
- ☐ Touching or measuring the perceived flaw
- ☐ Researching cosmetic procedures
- ☐ Changing clothes multiple times before going out
- ☐ Using filters on every photo before posting

### Avoidance & Impact
- ☐ Avoiding social situations due to appearance concerns
- ☐ Difficulty going to work/school because of how you look
- ☐ Avoiding photos or being on camera
- ☐ Covering the perceived flaw (hats, scarves, makeup, positioning)
- ☐ Relationships affected by appearance preoccupation
- ☐ Considering or undergoing cosmetic procedures without satisfaction
- ☐ Feeling depressed or anxious specifically about your appearance

## Indian Context

- Fair skin obsession and colourism amplify BDD in India
- "Log kya kahenge" intensifies appearance anxiety
- Matrimonial culture (being "evaluated" on looks for marriage)
- Social media beauty standards (filters, editing, influencer culture)
- Body shaming normalised in families ("You've gained weight")

## Scoring

| Checked | Level | Action |
|---------|-------|--------|
| 0–4 | Normal appearance awareness | Self-care practices |
| 5–10 | Elevated body image concerns | Self-help + monitoring |
| 11–16 | Possible BDD | Therapy recommended |
| 17+ | Likely BDD | Professional treatment strongly advised |
