## Am I Overwhelmed Right Now? Check what applies:

- ☐ Everything feels like too much
- ☐ I can't think clearly or make decisions
- ☐ I want to cry, scream, or shut down
- ☐ My body feels heavy, tense, or shaky
- ☐ I feel like running away from everything
- ☐ Small tasks feel impossible
- ☐ I'm snapping at people I care about
- ☐ I can't identify what I'm feeling — it's just "a lot"

## Immediate Relief Protocol (Do These NOW)

1. ☐ **STOP** — Drop what you're doing
2. ☐ **Breathe** — 4 counts in, 6 counts out × 5 breaths
3. ☐ **Ground** — Feel your feet on the floor. Notice 3 things you can see.
4. ☐ **Name it** — "I am overwhelmed. This is temporary."
5. ☐ **Reduce input** — Silence phone, close laptop, dim lights
6. ☐ **Cold water** — Splash face or hold ice cubes (activates dive reflex, calms nervous system)
7. ☐ **Move** — Walk, stretch, shake your body for 30 seconds
8. ☐ **Choose ONE thing** — Pick the smallest possible next step. Just one.

## After the Wave Passes

- ☐ Drink water and eat something
- ☐ Journal what happened (trigger, feeling, body sensation)
- ☐ Identify what's within your control vs. what isn't
- ☐ Cancel or postpone what you can
- ☐ Tell someone how you're feeling
- ☐ Go to bed early if possible

## Overwhelm Prevention Checklist (Daily)

- ☐ Am I sleeping enough?
- ☐ Am I eating regularly?
- ☐ Am I saying yes to too much?
- ☐ Am I taking breaks?
- ☐ Am I asking for help when I need it?
- ☐ Am I processing emotions or stuffing them?

**If overwhelm is frequent (3+ times per week) → therapy is recommended.**
