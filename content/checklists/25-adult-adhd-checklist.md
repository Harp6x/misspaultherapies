# Adult ADHD — Screening Checklist
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

> 📱 @mspaultherapies | 🌐 mspaultherapies.com | 📧 mspaultherapies@gmail.com

**Note:** ADHD in adults is widely underdiagnosed, especially in India and among women. This is a screening tool — not a diagnosis.

---

## Part A: Inattention Symptoms

Check if you FREQUENTLY experience:

- ☐ Difficulty sustaining attention in tasks (reading, meetings, conversations)
- ☐ Making careless mistakes in work despite knowing better
- ☐ Not listening when spoken to directly ("zoning out")
- ☐ Starting tasks but not finishing them
- ☐ Difficulty organising tasks and managing time
- ☐ Avoiding or dreading tasks that require sustained mental effort
- ☐ Losing things necessary for daily life (keys, phone, wallet, documents)
- ☐ Being easily distracted by unrelated thoughts or stimuli
- ☐ Forgetfulness in daily activities (appointments, bills, chores)

## Part B: Hyperactivity/Impulsivity Symptoms

- ☐ Fidgeting, tapping, or feeling internally restless
- ☐ Difficulty staying seated in situations where expected (meetings, flights)
- ☐ Feeling driven by a motor — always needing to be doing something
- ☐ Difficulty engaging in leisure activities quietly
- ☐ Talking excessively or interrupting others
- ☐ Blurting out answers before questions are finished
- ☐ Difficulty waiting your turn (queues, conversations)
- ☐ Impulsive decisions (purchases, job changes, relationships)
- ☐ Emotional impulsivity — quick to anger, frustration, or tears

## Part C: Impact & History

- ☐ These patterns have been present since childhood (not just recent)
- ☐ They occur in multiple settings (work, home, social)
- ☐ They significantly impair your functioning
- ☐ You've been called "lazy," "careless," or "not living up to potential"
- ☐ You compensate through extreme effort, lists, or anxiety
- ☐ You struggle with time perception (always late, misjudging durations)
- ☐ You experience "hyperfocus" — losing hours on interesting tasks but can't focus on boring ones

## Interpretation

| Part A + B Total | Suggestion |
|-----------------|------------|
| 0–5 | Unlikely ADHD |
| 6–10 | Some ADHD traits — worth monitoring |
| 11–15 | Possible ADHD — psychological assessment recommended |
| 16+ (especially with Part C) | Likely ADHD — comprehensive evaluation needed |

## ADHD in India: Why It's Missed

- Confused with laziness, lack of discipline, or "not trying hard enough"
- High-achieving individuals may mask symptoms through compensatory anxiety
- Women often present as inattentive (quiet) rather than hyperactive — missed entirely
- Cultural emphasis on academic performance leads to punishment rather than evaluation
- Many adults discover ADHD only when their child is diagnosed
