## Emotional Impact — Check all that apply:

- ☐ Grief for your pre-illness self and life
- ☐ Anger ("Why me?")
- ☐ Anxiety about disease progression
- ☐ Depression or persistent low mood
- ☐ Guilt about being a "burden"
- ☐ Shame about limitations or dependence
- ☐ Fear of being abandoned by partner/friends
- ☐ Loss of identity beyond being "the sick person"
- ☐ Frustration with medical system or treatments
- ☐ Jealousy of healthy peers
- ☐ Loneliness and social isolation
- ☐ Financial stress from medical expenses

## Daily Coping Checklist

- ☐ Pace yourself — energy is a finite resource today
- ☐ Prioritise tasks (essential → important → nice-to-have)
- ☐ Accept help without guilt (today)
- ☐ Move your body in whatever way is available (even gentle stretching)
- ☐ Nourish yourself (food, water, medication on time)
- ☐ Connect with one person (call, text, in person)
- ☐ Do one small thing that brings you joy
- ☐ Practise 5 minutes of mindfulness or relaxation
- ☐ Express a feeling (journal, talk, art)
- ☐ Set one boundary (say no to something draining)

## When to Seek Therapy

- Your mental health is significantly affecting your physical health management
- You've stopped doing things you enjoy
- Relationships are deteriorating
- You feel hopeless about the future
- Anxiety about health consumes your daily life

Therapy helps you build psychological resilience alongside medical treatment.
