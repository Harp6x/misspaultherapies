## Check all that apply to your relationship:

### Control & Power
- ☐ Partner tells you what to wear, who to see, or where to go
- ☐ Monitors your phone, social media, or location
- ☐ Makes all financial decisions or controls your money
- ☐ Isolates you from friends and family
- ☐ Makes you feel like you need "permission" to do things

### Communication
- ☐ Frequent yelling, name-calling, or insults
- ☐ Silent treatment lasting days
- ☐ Gaslighting ("That never happened," "You're overreacting")
- ☐ Blaming you for their behaviour ("You made me angry")
- ☐ Dismissing your feelings as "drama" or "too sensitive"
- ☐ Weaponising your vulnerabilities against you in arguments

### Emotional
- ☐ Walking on eggshells around your partner
- ☐ Feeling worse about yourself since the relationship started
- ☐ Constant anxiety about their mood or reactions
- ☐ Apologising for things that aren't your fault
- ☐ Feeling responsible for their emotions
- ☐ Love-bombing followed by coldness (hot/cold cycle)

### Physical
- ☐ Any physical aggression (pushing, grabbing, hitting, throwing things)
- ☐ Threatening physical harm
- ☐ Sexual coercion or pressure
- ☐ Destroying property during arguments

## Scoring

| Checked | Level | Action |
|---------|-------|--------|
| 0–3 | Normal relationship friction | Communication tools can help |
| 4–8 | Concerning patterns | Couples therapy or individual therapy recommended |
| 9–15 | Significant red flags | Individual therapy essential; assess safety |
| 16+ or ANY physical items | Dangerous | Safety planning urgently needed |

## If You're in Danger

- **Emergency:** 112
- **Women's Helpline:** 181 (India)
- **Vandrevala Foundation:** 1860-2662-345

You deserve to be safe. You deserve to be treated with respect.
