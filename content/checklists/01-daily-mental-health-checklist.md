## How to Use

Print this page and check off items each day. You don't need to do all of them — aim for **at least 5 per day**. Over time, these small habits compound into significant mental health improvement.

---

## The Daily Checklist

### Body
- [ ] Drank at least 6 glasses of water
- [ ] Ate 3 meals (even small ones)
- [ ] Moved my body for at least 15 minutes
- [ ] Got some sunlight or fresh air
- [ ] Slept 7–8 hours last night

### Mind
- [ ] Took 3 slow, deep breaths at some point today
- [ ] Noticed one negative thought and gently challenged it
- [ ] Spent 5 minutes without screens (just being present)
- [ ] Did something that required focus (reading, puzzles, cooking)
- [ ] Practised gratitude (named 3 good things)

### Emotions
- [ ] Checked in with myself: "How am I really feeling?"
- [ ] Let myself feel an emotion without judging it
- [ ] Did one thing that brought me a moment of joy
- [ ] Talked to someone about how I'm doing
- [ ] Said something kind to myself

### Connection
- [ ] Had a real conversation with someone (not just texts)
- [ ] Asked someone how they're doing and really listened
- [ ] Hugged someone or showed physical affection
- [ ] Reached out to a friend or family member
- [ ] Spent quality time with someone I care about

### Boundaries
- [ ] Said no to something I didn't want to do
- [ ] Took a break when I needed one
- [ ] Set a limit on screen time / social media
- [ ] Left work on time (or stepped away from study)
- [ ] Gave myself permission to rest without guilt
