## Check all that apply:

- ☐ Loss of identity ("I was a [job title]. Who am I now?")
- ☐ Loss of daily structure and routine
- ☐ Feeling purposeless or useless
- ☐ Missing the social connections from work
- ☐ Relationship tension with spouse (too much time together)
- ☐ Financial anxiety despite planning
- ☐ Boredom that hobbies can't fill
- ☐ Feeling irrelevant or invisible
- ☐ Health anxiety increasing with age
- ☐ Grief about ageing and mortality
- ☐ Depression or low mood that started post-retirement
- ☐ Difficulty accepting help or dependence
- ☐ Children too busy for regular connection
- ☐ Spouse still working, creating imbalance

## Building a Meaningful Retirement

### Daily Structure Template
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| Morning | Physical activity (walk, yoga, garden) |
| Mid-morning | Purpose activity (volunteering, mentoring, learning) |
| Afternoon | Social connection (friend, community, family) |
| Evening | Leisure (reading, hobby, entertainment) |

### Purpose Rebuilding Ideas
- Volunteer with an organisation aligned to your values
- Mentor young professionals in your former field
- Learn something new (language, instrument, craft, technology)
- Write (memoir, blog, letters to grandchildren)
- Join a community group (spiritual, hobby, neighbourhood)
- Travel with intention (not just tourism)
- Start a small passion project or business

## When to Seek Help

If low mood, isolation, or purposelessness persists beyond 3 months post-retirement, therapy can help you navigate this significant life transition.
