## Is Your Anger a Problem? Check all that apply:

- ☐ You get angry more often than others around you
- ☐ Your anger is disproportionate to the trigger
- ☐ You say or do things you regret when angry
- ☐ Anger lasts for hours or days (holding grudges)
- ☐ Physical aggression (hitting, throwing, breaking things)
- ☐ Verbal aggression (yelling, name-calling, threats)
- ☐ Passive aggression (silent treatment, sarcasm, sabotage)
- ☐ Road rage or public outbursts
- ☐ Anger is affecting your relationships
- ☐ People are afraid of your anger
- ☐ You use anger to control others
- ☐ Physical symptoms (headaches, chest pain, stomach issues)
- ☐ Difficulty calming down once angry
- ☐ Using substances when angry

## When Anger Arrives — 10-Step Protocol

1. ☐ STOP — Don't act on the impulse
2. ☐ Take 3 deep breaths (slow exhale)
3. ☐ Rate your anger 1-10
4. ☐ If above 7 — leave the situation ("I need a break")
5. ☐ Physical release (walk, press palms together, cold water on face)
6. ☐ Ask: "What am I REALLY feeling underneath?" (often: hurt, fear, shame)
7. ☐ Ask: "What do I need right now?"
8. ☐ Wait until anger is below 5 before communicating
9. ☐ Use "I feel..." language, not "You always..."
10. ☐ If you acted poorly — repair as soon as possible
