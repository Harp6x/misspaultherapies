## Red Flags — Check Any That Apply

### Academic
- ☐ Missing classes regularly without reason
- ☐ Can't focus despite trying
- ☐ Exam anxiety so severe it affects performance
- ☐ Lost interest in your field of study
- ☐ Imposter syndrome ("I don't belong here")

### Social & Emotional
- ☐ Homesickness that hasn't improved after 3+ months
- ☐ Feeling isolated despite being surrounded by people
- ☐ FOMO from social media affecting your mood
- ☐ Relationship problems (roommate, romantic, family)
- ☐ Peer pressure around alcohol, substances, or lifestyle

### Identity & Future
- ☐ Questioning your career choice
- ☐ Parental pressure to pursue a specific path
- ☐ Struggling with identity (sexuality, values, beliefs)
- ☐ Comparison with peers' achievements
- ☐ Fear of the future or "What am I doing with my life?"

### Physical
- ☐ Irregular sleep schedule (sleeping at 3 AM, waking at noon)
- ☐ Skipping meals or eating poorly consistently
- ☐ No physical activity for weeks
- ☐ Using substances to cope (alcohol, weed, prescription stimulants)

## If 5+ Red Flags Are Checked

College is a massive transition. Struggling doesn't mean you're weak — it means you're human navigating enormous change. Therapy can help you build skills for this phase and beyond.
