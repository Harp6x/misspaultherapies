## Immediate (First 2 Weeks)
- ☐ Allow yourself to feel (shock, anger, grief, relief — all valid)
- ☐ Tell a trusted person — don't isolate
- ☐ Resist making major life decisions while in shock
- ☐ Assess finances — create a survival budget
- ☐ Update health insurance and emergency contacts
- ☐ File for any benefits you're entitled to
- ☐ Write down what happened (journaling helps process)

## Emotional Processing (Weeks 2–8)
- ☐ Separate your identity from your job title
- ☐ Grieve what you lost (routine, purpose, identity, income, colleagues)
- ☐ Manage the shame narrative ("I failed" → "This happened TO me")
- ☐ Limit social media — comparison is toxic during this time
- ☐ Maintain a daily routine (wake time, meals, movement)
- ☐ Stay connected socially (don't retreat)
- ☐ Be honest about how you're feeling (skip the "I'm fine" script)

## Rebuilding (Month 2+)
- ☐ Update resume and LinkedIn without desperation
- ☐ Reach out to network authentically (not transactionally)
- ☐ Consider if this is an opportunity to pivot
- ☐ Set a daily job search limit (2 hours max — prevent burnout)
- ☐ Celebrate small wins (an interview, a connection, a good day)
- ☐ Invest time in learning or upskilling
- ☐ Volunteer — it provides purpose and community

## Red Flags to Watch For
- ☐ Persistent hopelessness beyond 4 weeks
- ☐ Substance use increasing
- ☐ Complete social withdrawal
- ☐ Sleep or appetite severely disrupted
- ☐ Self-worth at rock bottom
- ☐ Relationship breakdown due to stress
- ☐ Thoughts of self-harm

**If any red flags are present → seek professional support.**
