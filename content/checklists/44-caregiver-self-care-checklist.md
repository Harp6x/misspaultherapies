> 📱 @mspaultherapies | 🌐 mspaultherapies.com | 📧 mspaultherapies@gmail.com

**For:** Anyone caring for an ageing parent, ill spouse, child with special needs, or family member with mental illness.

---

## Daily Non-Negotiables

- ☐ Eat at least 2 proper meals today
- ☐ Drink adequate water
- ☐ Take 15 minutes completely for yourself
- ☐ Step outside (even briefly — sunlight matters)
- ☐ Move your body (walk, stretch, dance — 10 minutes)
- ☐ Connect with someone outside your caregiving role
- ☐ Take your own medications/supplements
- ☐ Get at least 6 hours of sleep (ask for help if needed)

## Warning Signs of Caregiver Burnout

- ☐ Exhaustion that rest doesn't fix
- ☐ Resentment toward the person you're caring for
- ☐ Feeling trapped with no way out
- ☐ Withdrawing from friends and activities
- ☐ Getting sick more often
- ☐ Emotional outbursts or crying
- ☐ Neglecting your own health completely
- ☐ Using alcohol, food, or shopping to cope
- ☐ Fantasising about running away
- ☐ Feeling guilty for wanting a break
- ☐ Losing empathy for the person in your care
- ☐ Hopelessness about the situation ever changing

## Permission Slips

- You are allowed to ask for help
- You are allowed to take breaks
- You are allowed to feel frustrated, angry, or sad
- You are allowed to set boundaries with the person you care for
- You are allowed to have a life beyond caregiving
- You are allowed to not be okay
