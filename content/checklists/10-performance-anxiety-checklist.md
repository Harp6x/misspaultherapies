## Do You Experience Anxiety in Performance Situations?

Check all that apply:

### Before Performance (Presentations, Exams, Interviews, Meetings)
- ☐ Dread or panic days/weeks before the event
- ☐ Difficulty sleeping the night before
- ☐ Nausea, stomach churning, or loss of appetite
- ☐ Catastrophic "what if" thinking
- ☐ Over-preparing to the point of exhaustion
- ☐ Procrastinating on preparation due to anxiety
- ☐ Wanting to cancel or call in sick

### During Performance
- ☐ Mind going blank despite preparation
- ☐ Voice trembling or going quiet
- ☐ Hands shaking
- ☐ Excessive sweating
- ☐ Racing heartbeat visible to you
- ☐ Losing track of what you were saying
- ☐ Feeling detached or "watching yourself from outside"
- ☐ Rushing through to "get it over with"

### After Performance
- ☐ Replaying every mistake on loop
- ☐ Fixating on one "bad" moment despite overall success
- ☐ Seeking reassurance ("Was I okay? Did I sound stupid?")
- ☐ Feeling exhausted or emotionally drained
- ☐ Avoiding future similar situations
- ☐ Harsh self-criticism ("I should have done better")

### Impact on Life
- ☐ Turning down career opportunities (promotions, talks, leadership roles)
- ☐ Academic performance doesn't reflect actual knowledge
- ☐ Avoiding social situations that involve "performing" (toasts, introductions)
- ☐ Physical health affected (chronic tension, headaches, GI issues)
- ☐ Self-worth tied to performance outcomes

## Scoring

| Checked | Level | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|---------------|
| 0–6 | Mild nerves | Normal pre-performance jitters. Breathing exercises help. |
| 7–14 | Moderate | Performance anxiety is limiting you. Skills training + therapy recommended. |
| 15+ | Significant | Therapy (CBT + exposure) strongly recommended. |

## Quick Tools for Performance Anxiety

1. **Power posing:** 2 minutes of expansive posture before performing (reduces cortisol)
2. **Reframe anxiety as excitement:** "I'm not nervous, I'm excited" (same physiological state)
3. **Focus on contribution, not evaluation:** Ask "What can I give?" not "How will I be judged?"
4. **Cognitive defusion:** "I notice I'm having the thought that I'll mess up" (observe, don't believe)
