## Check all that apply:

### Normal Pre-Wedding Jitters
- ☐ Excitement mixed with nervousness
- ☐ Stress about logistics and planning
- ☐ Worry about things going wrong on the day
- ☐ Mild anxiety about the commitment

### Signs of Deeper Anxiety
- ☐ Questioning whether this is the right person
- ☐ Fear of losing your identity or independence
- ☐ Dreading the wedding rather than looking forward to it
- ☐ Family drama causing significant distress
- ☐ Cultural/religious pressure driving decisions you're not comfortable with
- ☐ Comparing your relationship to others'
- ☐ Unresolved conflicts with your partner you're avoiding
- ☐ Feeling trapped or like you "can't back out now"
- ☐ Panic attacks or insomnia related to the wedding
- ☐ Body image anxiety amplified by wedding expectations
- ☐ Financial stress causing resentment
- ☐ In-law dynamics creating tension before you've even started

### Indian Wedding Specific
- ☐ Pressure to have an elaborate wedding you don't want
- ☐ Dowry or financial expectations causing distress
- ☐ Feeling like the wedding is for families, not for you
- ☐ Arranged marriage doubts you feel you can't voice
- ☐ Grief about leaving your family (kanyadaan emotions)
- ☐ Anxiety about fitting into a new family

## What's Normal vs. What Needs Attention

**Normal:** Butterflies, logistical stress, excitement, some nervousness
**Needs attention:** Persistent dread, avoidance, questioning the relationship, physical symptoms

## Pre-Marital Therapy Is Not a Bad Sign

It's actually the smartest investment you can make. Discuss expectations, communication styles, finances, family roles, and boundaries BEFORE the wedding.
