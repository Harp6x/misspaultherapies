## Are You Ready to Break Free from Diet Culture?

### Signs You Might Benefit from Intuitive Eating
- ☐ You've been on multiple diets that "worked" temporarily then failed
- ☐ You label foods as "good" or "bad"
- ☐ You eat past fullness regularly or restrict despite hunger
- ☐ You feel guilty after eating certain foods
- ☐ You use food as a primary coping mechanism for emotions
- ☐ You've lost touch with hunger and fullness cues
- ☐ Eating decisions are driven by rules rather than your body
