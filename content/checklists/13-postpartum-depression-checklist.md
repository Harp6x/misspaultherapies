# Postpartum Depression — Awareness Checklist for New Mothers
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

> 📱 @mspaultherapies | 🌐 mspaultherapies.com | 📧 mspaultherapies@gmail.com

**Note:** Baby blues (1–2 weeks after birth) are normal. If symptoms persist beyond 2 weeks or worsen, it may be postpartum depression.

---

## Check all that apply (past 2 weeks):

### Mood
- ☐ Persistent sadness, emptiness, or tearfulness
- ☐ Feeling overwhelmed — like you can't cope
- ☐ Intense irritability or anger (disproportionate to situation)
- ☐ Mood swings — fine one moment, crying the next
- ☐ Feeling numb or disconnected from emotions
- ☐ Guilt about not feeling "happy enough" about the baby
- ☐ Shame about struggling as a mother

### Bonding & Motherhood
- ☐ Difficulty bonding with your baby
- ☐ Feeling like a "bad mother"
- ☐ Doubting your ability to care for your baby
- ☐ Resenting your baby or partner
- ☐ Wishing you could "go back" to life before the baby
- ☐ Comparing yourself to other mothers on social media

### Anxiety & Fear
- ☐ Excessive worry about the baby's health or safety
- ☐ Intrusive scary thoughts about harm coming to the baby
- ☐ Fear of being alone with the baby
- ☐ Panic attacks or constant restlessness
- ☐ Checking on the baby excessively (even when they're safe)

### Physical & Cognitive
- ☐ Exhaustion beyond normal new-parent tiredness
- ☐ Inability to sleep even when the baby is sleeping
- ☐ Loss of appetite or overeating
- ☐ Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
- ☐ Memory problems ("brain fog")
- ☐ Physical aches with no medical cause

### Social & Behavioural
- ☐ Withdrawing from partner, family, or friends
- ☐ Losing interest in things you used to enjoy
- ☐ Not wanting to leave the house
- ☐ Crying frequently
- ☐ Thoughts of harming yourself or the baby

> ⚠️ If you checked the last item, please reach out immediately:
> **Vandrevala Foundation:** 1860-2662-345 (24/7) | **Emergency:** 112

## Indian Context: Why PPD Goes Undetected

- "New mothers should be happy" — cultural pressure to perform joy
- Joint family involvement can help but also mask the mother's struggles
- Confinement practices may increase isolation
- Hormone changes dismissed as "adjustment" or "weakness"
- Guilt about struggling when "everyone else manages"

## Scoring Guide

| Checked | Level | Action |
|---------|-------|--------|
| 0–5 | Baby blues range | Monitor, practice self-care, accept help |
| 6–12 | Possible PPD | Talk to your OB/GYN + consider therapy |
| 13–20 | Likely PPD | Professional support recommended urgently |
| 20+ or harm thoughts | Severe | Seek immediate help — call your doctor or helpline |
