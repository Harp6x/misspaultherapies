## How Do You Feel About Your Body? Check all that apply:

### Negative Body Image Signs
- ☐ You avoid looking at yourself in mirrors
- ☐ You frequently compare your body to others (especially on social media)
- ☐ You feel ashamed or embarrassed about your body
- ☐ You avoid certain activities because of how you look (swimming, gym, photos)
- ☐ You wear oversized clothes to hide your body
- ☐ Your mood is affected by the number on the scale
- ☐ You criticise your appearance daily ("I hate my thighs/stomach/arms")
- ☐ You believe others judge you based on your appearance
- ☐ You've turned down social invitations due to how you feel about your body
- ☐ You spend significant time trying to "fix" perceived flaws

### Positive Body Image Signs
- ☐ You appreciate what your body can do (not just how it looks)
- ☐ You eat based on hunger and nourishment, not punishment or reward
- ☐ You exercise for how it makes you feel, not to change your appearance
- ☐ You can accept compliments about your appearance
- ☐ You don't let a "bad body day" ruin your plans
- ☐ You challenge unrealistic beauty standards when you see them
- ☐ You treat your body with respect regardless of its size
- ☐ You can look at photos of yourself without harsh criticism
- ☐ You recognise that worth is not determined by appearance
- ☐ You limit exposure to content that makes you feel bad about yourself

## Scoring

**Negative items checked:**
| Count | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| 0–2 | Healthy body image with normal fluctuations |
| 3–5 | Some body image concerns — self-help strategies recommended |
| 6–8 | Moderate body image distress — therapy may help |
| 9–10 | Significant body image issues — professional support recommended |

## 5 Steps Toward Body Acceptance

1. **Curate your feed:** Unfollow accounts that make you feel bad; follow body-positive and diverse creators
2. **Challenge the thought:** When "I'm ugly" appears, ask "Would I say this to my best friend?"
3. **Gratitude practice:** Each day, thank your body for one thing it did for you
4. **Wear what feels good:** Choose comfort and expression over "flattering"
5. **Limit body talk:** Reduce conversations about weight, diets, and appearance
