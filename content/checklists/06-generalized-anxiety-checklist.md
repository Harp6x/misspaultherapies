## Scoring

| Score | Level | Interpretation |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| 0–10 | Minimal | Normal levels of worry. Continue self-care practices. |
| 11–20 | Mild | Noticeable anxiety. Self-help tools may be beneficial. |
| 21–30 | Moderate | Anxiety is impacting daily life. Consider professional support. |
| 31–45 | Severe | Significant distress. Professional therapy is strongly recommended. |

## What Your Anxiety Might Look Like (Indian Context)

- Constant worry about family members' health or safety
- Overthinking about career, exams, or "settling down"
- Physical symptoms dismissed as "just stress" or "acidity"
- Avoiding social situations due to fear of judgement
- Checking phone/news repeatedly for reassurance
- Difficulty saying no due to fear of disappointing others
