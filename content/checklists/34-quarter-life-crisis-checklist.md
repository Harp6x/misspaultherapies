## Check all that apply:

- ☐ Feeling stuck between who you are and who you "should" be
- ☐ Constantly questioning your career choices
- ☐ Feeling behind compared to your peers
- ☐ Nostalgia for simpler times (school, college)
- ☐ Fear that you've peaked or that life will only get harder
- ☐ Relationship dissatisfaction ("Is this the right person?")
- ☐ Wanting to make a drastic change (quit your job, move cities, break up)
- ☐ Feeling like an imposter in your professional life
- ☐ Existential questions ("What's the point? Is this all there is?")
- ☐ Financial anxiety despite earning
- ☐ Disconnection from friends as everyone's lives diverge
- ☐ Physical manifestations (insomnia, fatigue, weight changes)
- ☐ Increased reliance on coping mechanisms (drinking, scrolling, shopping)
- ☐ Feeling simultaneously too young and too old
- ☐ Parents are ageing and you feel unprepared for that responsibility

## Scoring

| Checked | Level |
|---------|-------|
| 0–4 | Normal adulting stress |
| 5–9 | Quarter-life transition — intentional reflection recommended |
| 10+ | Significant quarter-life crisis — therapy can provide clarity |

## What a Quarter-Life Crisis Actually Is

It's not being dramatic. Research shows that the mid-to-late 20s is a distinct developmental phase involving identity consolidation, values clarification, and major life decisions made with limited experience. The pressure is real.

## What Helps

1. **Name it:** "I'm in a transition, not a failure"
2. **Stop comparing:** Your timeline is yours
3. **Explore values:** What do YOU want (vs. what you were told to want)?
4. **Invest in quality relationships:** Depth > breadth
5. **Therapy:** A space to process confusion without judgement
