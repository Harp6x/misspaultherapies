## Part A: Do These Situations Make You Anxious?

Check all that apply:

- ☐ Meeting new people or making small talk
- ☐ Speaking up in meetings, classes, or group settings
- ☐ Eating or drinking in front of others
- ☐ Being the centre of attention
- ☐ Making phone calls (especially to strangers)
- ☐ Attending parties or social gatherings
- ☐ Talking to authority figures (boss, teacher, in-laws)
- ☐ Being watched while doing something (writing, working)
- ☐ Ordering food at a restaurant
- ☐ Using public restrooms
- ☐ Entering a room where people are already seated
- ☐ Disagreeing with someone or expressing your opinion
- ☐ Returning items to a shop
- ☐ Job interviews or formal evaluations
- ☐ Dating or romantic interactions

**Count:** ___/15 situations checked

## Part B: What Happens When You're Anxious?

### Physical Symptoms
- ☐ Blushing or face feeling hot
- ☐ Sweating (especially palms, underarms)
- ☐ Trembling or shaky voice
- ☐ Racing heartbeat
- ☐ Nausea or stomach churning
- ☐ Mind going blank
- ☐ Dry mouth or difficulty swallowing
- ☐ Avoiding eye contact

### Thought Patterns
- ☐ "Everyone is judging me"
- ☐ "I'll say something stupid"
- ☐ "They can see how nervous I am"
- ☐ "I don't belong here"
- ☐ Replaying conversations for hours/days after
- ☐ Predicting embarrassment before events
- ☐ Believing others are more confident/capable than you

### Avoidance Behaviours
- ☐ Declining invitations regularly
- ☐ Arriving late/leaving early to avoid mingling
- ☐ Using alcohol or substances to cope socially
- ☐ Staying silent to avoid attention
- ☐ Over-preparing or scripting conversations
- ☐ Hiding behind your phone in social settings
- ☐ Cancelling plans last minute with excuses

## Interpretation

| Checked Items | Level | Suggestion |
|---------------|-------|------------|
| 0–8 total | Mild shyness | Self-help resources may be sufficient |
| 9–18 total | Moderate social anxiety | Therapy + gradual exposure recommended |
| 19+ total | Significant social anxiety | Professional support strongly recommended |

## Quick Coping Tools

1. **Before a social event:** Box breathing (4-4-4-4) for 2 minutes
2. **During:** Focus on asking the other person questions (shifts attention outward)
3. **After:** Write down 3 things that went okay (counters negativity bias)
4. **Daily:** Practice one small social exposure each day
