## Journal Structure

### Front Matter
- Title: "Us: A 60-Day Couples Connection Journal"
- How to use: Both partners journal daily (5 min each), share weekly
- Our relationship baseline (Day 1 fill-in together)
- Our relationship goals for these 60 days

### Daily Page Template (60 pages)

**Day _____ | Date: _____**

**Partner A:**
Connection rating today (1-10): ____
One thing I appreciated about you today: ____
Something on my mind I haven't said: ____
How I felt loved today: ____

**Partner B:**
Connection rating today (1-10): ____
One thing I appreciated about you today: ____
Something on my mind I haven't said: ____

**Together (fill in at the end of the day):**
Did we have quality time today? ☐ Yes ☐ No
Did we argue today? ☐ Yes ☐ No → Resolved? ☐ Yes ☐ No
One word for our relationship today: ____
