## Journal Structure

### Front Matter
- Title: "Still Here: A Grief Processing Journal"
- Note: "There is no right way to grieve. There is no timeline. This journal is a space for whatever you're feeling — the sadness, the anger, the guilt, the love, the nothing. All of it belongs here."
- How to use: No rules. No order. Open to any page. Write or don't.

### Section 1: Naming the Loss (10 pages)

**Page 1: Who/What I Lost**
Name: ____
Relationship to me: ____
Date of loss: ____
How it happened: ____

**Page 2: What They Meant to Me**
In one sentence: ____
In a paragraph: ____

**Page 3: The Last Time**
The last time I saw them: ____
The last thing they said: ____
The last thing I said: ____
What I wish I'd said: ____

**Page 4: What I Miss Most**
(Full page, unlined)

**Page 5: What I Don't Miss (It's Okay)**
(Full page — grief isn't all positive memories)

**Pages 6-10: Letters I'll Never Send**
Letter 1: What I want to tell you
Letter 2: What I'm angry about
Letter 3: What I'm grateful for
Letter 4: What I need you to know
Letter 5: Goodbye (for now)

### Section 2: The Waves (40 pages — undated entries)

**Entry Template:**
Date (optional): ____
Today grief feels like: ____
My body feels: ____
The emotion underneath: ____
What triggered this wave: ____
What I need right now: ____
What I did to cope: ____

(Space for free writing)

"Grief is love with nowhere to go." — Jamie Anderson

### Section 3: The Hard Parts (15 pages)

**Page: Guilt**

**Page: Anger**
I'm angry at: ____

What I need to do with this anger: ____

**Page: The "Firsts" Without Them**
First birthday: ____
First holiday: ____
First anniversary: ____
How I got through it: ____

**Page: Things People Say That Don't Help**
"They're in a better place" — What I wish they'd said instead: ____

"Stay strong" — What I wish they'd said instead: ____

**Page: What Actually Helps**
People who helped: ____
Words that comforted: ____

**Pages: The Bad Days (5 entries)**
Template: Today was a bad day because ____. I survived it by ____.
