## Business Description (750 characters)

Ms Paul Therapies offers professional online therapy and counselling by Aishani Paul, an RCI-licensed clinical psychologist (M.Phil Clinical Psychology). Specialising in anxiety, depression, relationships, self-esteem, trauma, and life transitions. Services include individual therapy, couples counselling, adolescent therapy, family therapy, and psychological assessments. 100% online — available across India and for NRIs abroad. Evidence-based approaches including CBT, DBT, ACT, and Emotion-Focused Therapy. Book a free 15-minute discovery call at mspaultherapies.com/book.
