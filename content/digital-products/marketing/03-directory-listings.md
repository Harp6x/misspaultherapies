## Universal Bio (200 words — adapt per platform)

Aishani Paul is an RCI-licensed clinical psychologist (A118537) with an M.Phil in Clinical Psychology. She is the founder of Ms Paul Therapies, offering professional online therapy and counselling across India and for NRIs abroad.

Aishani specialises in anxiety, depression, relationship difficulties, self-esteem, grief, trauma, and life transitions. She works with individuals, couples, adolescents (13+), and families using evidence-based approaches including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), and Emotion-Focused Therapy (EFT).

Her approach is warm, culturally sensitive, and non-judgmental. She believes therapy should be accessible to everyone and offers a sliding scale for those in financial need.

Sessions are 45–50 minutes, conducted via secure video call. A free 15-minute discovery call is available for new clients.

Languages: English, Hindi, Bengali.

Book online: mspaultherapies.com/book

**Practo Q&A answers to post:**

Q: "What should I expect in my first session?"
A: Your first session is a chance for us to get to know each other. I'll ask about what brought you here, your history, and your goals. You share at your own pace — there's no pressure. By the end, we'll have a plan for moving forward together.

Q: "How do I know if I need therapy?"
A: If something in your life is causing you distress — whether it's anxiety, relationship difficulties, grief, or just a feeling that something is 'off' — therapy can help. You don't need to be in crisis. Early support leads to better outcomes.

Q: "Do you offer online sessions?"
A: Yes, all sessions are conducted online via secure video call. This means you can access therapy from anywhere in India or abroad, from the comfort and privacy of your own space.
