## Community Rules

### 1. Confidentiality
What is shared in this group stays in this group. Do not screenshot, share, or discuss anyone's personal stories outside this space.

### 2. Respect
Everyone's experience is valid. No judgement, no unsolicited advice, no dismissing someone's feelings. "I hear you" is always better than "You should..."

### 3. No Diagnosis or Medical Advice
This is a peer support community, not a clinical space. Do not diagnose others or recommend medication. If someone needs professional help, gently suggest they speak to a therapist.

### 4. Trigger Warnings
If you're sharing about sensitive topics (self-harm, abuse, eating disorders, suicidal thoughts), please add a trigger warning (TW) at the beginning of your message.

### 5. No Promotion
This is not a space for promoting businesses, products, or services. Sharing helpful resources (books, articles, apps) is welcome.

### 6. Crisis Protocol
If you or someone in the group is in immediate danger:
- Call **112** (India emergency)
- Vandrevala Foundation: **1860-2662-345** (24/7)
- Tag the moderator immediately

This group cannot provide crisis intervention. Professional help is critical in emergencies.

### 7. Be Kind to Yourself
You don't have to participate every day. Lurking is welcome. Taking breaks is welcome. This space will be here when you need it.

## Moderation Guidelines (For Aishani)

1. Check the group daily (5 min)
2. Acknowledge vulnerable shares with empathy ("Thank you for sharing this 💛")
3. Redirect clinical questions: "That's a great question for a therapy session. Want to book a call?"
4. Remove members who violate rules after one warning
5. Post weekly prompts to keep engagement alive
6. Share relevant blog posts and resources from mspaultherapies.com
