## Page 2: The Lie You Were Told

Somewhere along the way, you learned that you had to EARN your worth.

Be smart enough. Thin enough. Successful enough. Agreeable enough.

**That's not self-worth. That's a performance.**

Real self-worth is unconditional. It exists because you exist. This handbook will help you start believing that.

---

## Page 3: Where Low Self-Worth Comes From

It's rarely one thing. It's usually a pattern:

- **Critical parents** — Nothing was ever good enough
- **Bullying** — You learned to make yourself smaller
- **Comparison culture** — Instagram made it a daily habit
- **Toxic relationships** — Someone told you your worth was conditional
- **Cultural messaging** — "Don't be too proud/confident/visible"
- **Achievement addiction** — Your worth became your resume

**None of this was your fault. All of it can change.**

## Page 4: The Self-Worth vs Self-Esteem Difference

| Self-Esteem | Self-Worth |
|------------|-----------|
| "I feel good about what I do" | "I feel good about who I am" |
| Based on achievements | Based on existence |
| Goes up and down | Can be constant |
| External validation | Internal knowing |

This handbook focuses on **self-worth** — the deeper layer.

## Page 5: Exercise — The Evidence File

Your brain has a negativity bias. It remembers every failure and forgets every win. Let's fix that.

**Create an Evidence File** (phone note, journal, or jar):

Every day, add ONE piece of evidence that you are:
- ☐ Capable
- ☐ Kind
- ☐ Brave
- ☐ Resilient
- ☐ Loved
- ☐ Growing

Small counts. "I made my bed" counts. "I asked for help" counts. "I survived today" counts.

Read the file when the inner critic is loud.

## Page 6: Meet Your Inner Critic

Your inner critic has a voice. Let's name it and separate it from YOU.

**Name your critic:** ____ (mine is called "The Judge")

**Common critic scripts:**
- "You're not good enough"
- "Everyone else has it figured out"
- "You don't deserve good things"
- "Who do you think you are?"

**Rewrite practice:**

| Critic says | You say back |
|------------|-------------|
| "You're not enough" | "I am a work in progress, and that's okay" |
| "Nobody really likes you" | "I have people who care. My brain is lying." |
| "You always mess up" | "I make mistakes AND I also do many things well" |

## Page 7: The Comparison Cure

Comparison is the thief of self-worth. Here's the antidote:

**1. Limit your inputs**
Unfollow anyone who makes you feel "less than." Yes, even family pages, wedding accounts, hustle culture.

**2. Separate "admire" from "compare"**
"I admire their work" ≠ "I am less than them"

**3. Remember the 3-second rule**
You're comparing your behind-the-scenes to their highlight reel. You see their success in 3 seconds. You didn't see their 3 years of struggle.

**4. Run your own race**
Your timeline is valid. Your pace is valid. Your path is valid.

## Page 8: Boundaries Are Self-Worth in Action

Every time you set a boundary, you're saying: **"My needs matter."**

| Low self-worth | Healthy self-worth |
|---------------|-------------------|
| "I'll stay late again, it's fine" | "I've done my part. I'm going home." |
| "They can talk to me however they want" | "I don't accept disrespect." |
| "My feelings don't matter" | "My feelings are valid data." |

## Page 9: Daily Self-Worth Rituals (Pick 2)

1. **Mirror work:** Look yourself in the eye. Say: "I am worthy of love and good things." (Uncomfortable at first. Do it anyway.)
2. **Morning affirmation:** "Today, I choose to treat myself the way I'd treat someone I love."
3. **Nightly evidence:** Add one thing to your Evidence File.
4. **Body gratitude:** Thank your body for 3 things it did today.
5. **Say no once:** To something that drains you. No guilt.
