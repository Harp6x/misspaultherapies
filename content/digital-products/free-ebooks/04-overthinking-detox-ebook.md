## Page 2: Your Brain Has Too Many Tabs Open

Overthinking isn't a personality trait. It's a habit your brain learned — which means it can unlearn it.

This 7-day detox gives you one technique per day. By day 7, you'll have a complete anti-overthinking toolkit.

**Rules:**
- Practice each day's technique for at least 10 minutes
- Journal at the end of each day (template provided)
- Don't skip days — this builds on itself

---

## Page 3: Day 1 — The Brain Dump

**Time:** 10 minutes | **What you need:** Paper + pen

Write down EVERYTHING in your head. No filtering. No organising. Just dump.

Then sort into 3 columns:
| I can act on this NOW | I can act on this LATER | I have NO control over this |
|-|-|-|
| | | |

Throw away column 3. Schedule column 2. Do one thing from column 1.

**Why it works:** Overthinking is your brain trying to hold 47 things in RAM. Offload to paper.

---

## Page 4: Day 2 — Worry Postponement

Set a "worry time" — 15 minutes at the same time daily.

When a worry appears outside that time:
1. Write it on a sticky note
2. Say: "I'll worry about this at [time]"
3. Return to what you were doing

During worry time: worry intentionally. When the timer ends, stop.

**Why it works:** Research shows 85% of worries don't survive until worry time.

---

## Page 5: Day 3 — The 5-5-5 Rule

When you catch yourself overthinking, ask:
- **Will this matter in 5 days?**
- **Will this matter in 5 months?**
- **Will this matter in 5 years?**

If no → let it go.
If yes → make a plan (one step only).

---

## Page 6: Day 4 — Name the Story

Your brain tells stories. Most of them aren't true.

Instead of believing the thought, NAME it:
- "I'm having the 'I'm not good enough' story again"
- "My brain is running the 'everyone will judge me' tape"
- "There goes the 'worst-case scenario' channel"

**Naming creates distance.** You stop being the thought and start OBSERVING it.

---

## Page 7: Day 5 — The 2-Minute Movement Rule

Overthinking lives in the head. Break the loop with the body.

When you catch yourself spiraling:
- Stand up
- 30 seconds of jumping jacks OR
- 1 minute walk to the window OR
- 30 seconds of cold water on your wrists

Movement disrupts the neural loop. Always.

---

## Page 8: Day 6 — Set a Decision Timer

Overthinkers delay decisions hoping for certainty. Certainty doesn't come.

**The rule:** For any non-life-threatening decision, give yourself a time limit.

| Decision type | Timer |
|-------------|-------|
| What to eat, wear, reply | 2 minutes |
| Plans with friends | 5 minutes |
| Work decisions | 30 minutes |
| Big life choices | 48 hours |

Make the decision. Move on. Course-correct later if needed.

---

## Page 9: Day 7 — The Enough Statement

Write and repeat:

"I have done enough today. I have thought enough. I am enough."

Put it on your phone wallpaper. Say it before bed. Use it whenever your brain starts the "but what if..." loop.

**You don't need to think your way to peace. You need to practice your way there.**
