## Visual Layout

### Title
**FIGHT · FLIGHT · FREEZE · FAWN**
*What's YOUR stress response?*

### 4-Quadrant Layout

---

### 🔴 FIGHT
**Activation:** Anger, confrontation, aggression
**Body:** Jaw clenching, fists tight, heated face
**Behaviour:**
- Arguing, yelling
- Controlling situations
- Being critical or bullying
- "I'll make them stop"
**At its best:** Standing up for yourself, assertiveness
**At its worst:** Rage, aggression, damaged relationships

---

### 🟡 FLIGHT
**Activation:** Escape, avoidance, restlessness
**Body:** Restless legs, fidgeting, can't sit still
**Behaviour:**
- Over-working, over-exercising, staying busy
- Leaving situations abruptly
- Scrolling/numbing
- "If I just keep moving, I won't have to feel"
**At its best:** Taking space, removing yourself from danger
**At its worst:** Burnout, avoidance, never processing

---

### 🔵 FREEZE
**Activation:** Shutdown, numbness, dissociation
**Body:** Feeling heavy, foggy, disconnected
**Behaviour:**
- Zoning out, sleeping excessively
- Unable to make decisions
- Procrastination, paralysis
- "I can't move or think"
**At its best:** Pausing before reacting
**At its worst:** Depression, dissociation, learned helplessness

---

### 🟢 FAWN
**Activation:** People-pleasing, appeasing, merging
**Body:** Tension while smiling, stomach knots
**Behaviour:**
- Agreeing to avoid conflict
- Abandoning your own needs
- Over-apologising
- "Whatever you need — I'm fine"
**At its best:** Empathy, social harmony
**At its worst:** Loss of identity, codependency, resentment

---

### Key Insight
"Your stress response was adaptive once — it protected you. But what protected you then may be limiting you now."

### Centre Text
**Most people have a dominant response. Some switch between them. All are normal. All can be worked with in therapy.**
