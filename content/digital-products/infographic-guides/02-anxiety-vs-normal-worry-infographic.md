## Visual Layout

### Title
**NORMAL WORRY vs ANXIETY DISORDER** — *Know the Difference*

### Two-Column Comparison

| NORMAL WORRY ✓ | ANXIETY DISORDER ⚠️ |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Tied to a specific situation | Free-floating, no clear trigger |
| Goes away when the situation resolves | Persists even when things are fine |
| Doesn't affect daily life | Interferes with work, sleep, relationships |
| You can distract yourself | The worry follows you everywhere |
| Feels proportional to the problem | Feels way bigger than the situation |
| Comes and goes | Constant (most days for 6+ months) |
| You can control it with effort | Feels uncontrollable despite trying |
| No significant physical symptoms | Heart racing, sweating, tension, nausea, insomnia |
| Doesn't stop you from doing things | Avoidance — you stop doing things because of it |

### Key Visual
**The Tipping Point:**
Normal worry ——————|—————— Anxiety disorder
("I'm nervous about this exam") → ("I can't function because of constant dread")

### Bottom Section

**When to seek help:**
- Anxiety lasts most days for 6+ months
- You're avoiding situations because of worry
- Physical symptoms: heart racing, stomach issues, insomnia
- You can't enjoy things you used to
- Worry feels disproportionate and uncontrollable

**Anxiety is treatable. You don't have to white-knuckle through life.**
