### 🟢 SECURE (~50% of people)
**Core belief:** "I am lovable. Others are trustworthy."

**In relationships:**
- Comfortable with intimacy AND independence
- Communicates needs directly
- Handles conflict without shutting down or exploding
- Trusts partner, doesn't need constant reassurance

**Sounds like:** "I miss you AND I'm okay when we're apart."

---

### 🟡 ANXIOUS-PREOCCUPIED (~20%)
**Core belief:** "I need others to feel okay. They might leave."

**In relationships:**
- Craves closeness, fears abandonment
- Over-analyses texts, tone, behaviour
- Needs frequent reassurance
- Protest behaviours when feeling distant (calling repeatedly, jealousy)

**Sounds like:** "Why haven't they replied? Did I do something wrong?"

---

### 🔴 AVOIDANT-DISMISSIVE (~25%)
**Core belief:** "I don't need anyone. Emotions are weakness."

**In relationships:**
- Values independence above connection
- Pulls away when things get close
- Minimises emotions (theirs and others')
- May seem "cold" but is actually protecting themselves

**Sounds like:** "I need space. This is too much."

---

### 🟣 FEARFUL-AVOIDANT / DISORGANISED (~5%)
**Core belief:** "I want love but it's dangerous."

**In relationships:**
- Push-pull pattern (come close, then run)
- Triggered by both intimacy AND distance
- Often linked to childhood trauma
- Confusing to self and partner

**Sounds like:** "I love you — don't leave. Wait, this is too much — I need to go."

---

### Bottom Bar
**Your attachment style is NOT your destiny. It can change with awareness and therapy.**
