### The 20 Red Flags (icon + short text per flag)

1. 🎭 **Love bombing** → Overwhelming affection early on, then withdrawal
2. 🔇 **Silent treatment** → Ignoring you as punishment
3. 🪞 **Gaslighting** → "That never happened" / "You're imagining things"
4. 📱 **Monitoring** → Checking your phone, location, social media
5. 🚫 **Isolation** → Cutting you off from friends and family
6. 😤 **Blame-shifting** → Everything is always YOUR fault
7. 🎢 **Hot/cold cycles** → Incredibly loving, then cruel, then loving again
8. 🤏 **Minimising** → "You're overreacting" / "It was just a joke"
9. 💣 **Walking on eggshells** → You constantly manage THEIR mood
10. 🏆 **Keeping score** → "After everything I've done for you"
11. 🚪 **Threatening to leave** → Using the relationship as leverage
12. 🎯 **Public humiliation** → Putting you down in front of others
13. 🔄 **Moving goalposts** → Nothing you do is ever enough
14. 🧱 **Withholding** → Affection, intimacy, or communication as control
15. 📋 **Excessive jealousy** → Disguised as "caring" or "protective"
16. 💰 **Financial control** → Managing your money, making you dependent
17. 🪤 **Guilt-tripping** → Making you feel bad for having needs
18. 🎭 **Different in public vs private** → Charming outside, cruel at home
19. 🔗 **Making you feel crazy** → You start questioning your own reality
20. 🚨 **You feel afraid** → Of their reaction, mood, or disapproval
