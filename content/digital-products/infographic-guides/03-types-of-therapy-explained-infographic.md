### Card Layout (8 therapy types)

---

**CBT — Cognitive Behavioural Therapy**
🧠 *Changes how you THINK to change how you FEEL*
Best for: Anxiety, depression, OCD, phobias
In a nutshell: "Your thoughts aren't facts. Let's test them."

---

**DBT — Dialectical Behaviour Therapy**
🌊 *Skills for surviving intense emotions*
Best for: Emotional dysregulation, BPD, self-harm, suicidal thoughts
In a nutshell: "I accept myself AND I want to change."

---

**Psychodynamic Therapy**
🪞 *Understanding present patterns through past experiences*
Best for: Relationship patterns, identity, childhood wounds
In a nutshell: "Your past is showing up in your present."

---

**EMDR — Eye Movement Desensitisation & Reprocessing**
👁️ *Reprocessing traumatic memories*
Best for: PTSD, trauma, phobias, disturbing memories
In a nutshell: "Your brain can heal the way your body does."

---

**ACT — Acceptance & Commitment Therapy**
🧭 *Living by values, not feelings*
Best for: Anxiety, chronic pain, life transitions, overthinking
In a nutshell: "Make room for difficult feelings while living meaningfully."

---

**Couples Therapy (Gottman / EFT)**
💑 *Improving communication and connection*
Best for: Relationship conflict, disconnection, trust issues
In a nutshell: "Change the dance, not the dancer."

---

**Somatic Therapy**
🫁 *Healing through the body*
Best for: Trauma, chronic tension, anxiety stored in the body
In a nutshell: "The body keeps the score. Let's release it."

---

**Mindfulness-Based Therapy (MBCT/MBSR)**
🧘 *Present-moment awareness as medicine*
Best for: Recurring depression, stress, chronic pain, rumination
In a nutshell: "You are not your thoughts."
