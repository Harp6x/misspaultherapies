# 7-Day Mental Health Reset Challenge
### One Simple Task Per Day to Shift Your Well-Being

## How It Works

One task per day. Each takes 10–15 minutes. Share your progress on Instagram with **#MsPaulChallenge @mspaultherapies** for community accountability.

---

## Day 1: The Check-In
**Theme:** Awareness
**Task:** Set 3 alarms on your phone — morning, afternoon, evening. At each alarm, pause and write down:
- What am I feeling right now? (Name the emotion)
- Where do I feel it in my body?
- What do I need right now?

**Why:** Most people go through entire days without checking in with themselves. Awareness is step one.

**Share prompt:** "Day 1 of the #MsPaulChallenge @mspaultherapies — I checked in with myself 3 times today. What I noticed: ___"

---

## Day 2: The Digital Detox
**Theme:** Boundaries
**Task:** No social media for 4 hours today (choose a block — morning, afternoon, or evening). Delete the apps temporarily if needed.

During those 4 hours, do something offline:
- Read a book
- Cook a meal from scratch
- Go for a walk
- Have a face-to-face conversation
- Sit in silence for 10 minutes

**Why:** Social media increases comparison, anxiety, and dopamine dependency. Even a short break resets your baseline.

**Share prompt (after the detox):** "Day 2 — I went 4 hours without social media. What I did instead: ___. How I felt: ___"

---

## Day 3: The Gratitude Flood
**Theme:** Positivity
**Task:** Write down 10 specific things you're grateful for. Not generic ("my family") — specific ("the way my mum called to check on me at lunch today").

Then: Send a thank-you message to ONE person on your list. Tell them specifically why you appreciate them.

**Why:** Gratitude rewires your brain's negativity bias. Expressing it to others strengthens relationships and releases oxytocin.

**Share prompt:** "Day 3 — 10 gratitudes written. 1 thank-you sent. Their response: ___"

---

## Day 4: The Movement Day
**Theme:** Body
**Task:** Move your body for 30 minutes in any way that feels good:
- Walk
- Dance in your room
- Yoga (YouTube has free sessions)
- Swim
- Cycle
- Play a sport
- Stretch deeply

**Rule:** No fitness goals. No calorie counting. No "should." Just move because it feels good.

**Why:** Exercise is as effective as antidepressants for mild-moderate depression. It reduces cortisol and increases serotonin, dopamine, and endorphins.

**Share prompt:** "Day 4 — I moved my body for 30 minutes. I chose: ___. How I felt after: ___"

---

## Day 5: The Connection Day
**Theme:** Relationships
**Task:** Have one REAL conversation today. Not texting. Not small talk. A genuine, phone-down, eye-contact conversation with someone.

Conversation starters if you need them:
- "What's been on your mind lately?"
- "When did you last feel really happy?"
- "Is there anything you've been wanting to talk about?"
- "What's something I don't know about you?"

**Why:** Loneliness is as harmful as smoking 15 cigarettes a day. Deep connection is a basic human need.

**Share prompt:** "Day 5 — I had a real conversation with ___. What I learned: ___"

---

## Day 6: The Self-Compassion Day
**Theme:** Inner world
**Task:** Write a letter to yourself — as if you were writing to a friend going through everything you're going through. Be kind. Be honest. Be gentle.

Start with: "Dear [your name], I know things have been hard lately..."

After writing: Read it out loud to yourself. (Yes, it will feel awkward. Do it anyway.)

**Why:** Self-compassion activates the care system in your brain. Writing externalises pain and creates distance, making it easier to process.

**Share prompt:** "Day 6 — I wrote a letter to myself. One line from it: ___"

---

## Day 7: The Intention Day
**Theme:** Future
**Task:** Answer these 3 questions in writing:

1. **What do I want MORE of in my life?** (Be specific — not just "happiness" but what happiness looks like for you)
2. **What do I want LESS of?** (What drains me, triggers me, keeps me stuck?)
3. **What is ONE thing I will do this week to move toward the life I want?**

**Why:** Intention creates direction. Direction creates motivation. Most people never pause long enough to ask what they actually want.

**Share prompt:** "Day 7 — The #MsPaulChallenge @mspaultherapies is done. My biggest takeaway: ___. One thing I'm going to keep doing: ___"
