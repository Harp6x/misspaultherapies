## Why 21 Days?

Research shows it takes 18–66 days to form a habit, with 21 being the most common starting point. This challenge builds progressively — Week 1 is easy, Week 2 is moderate, Week 3 stretches you.

**Track your progress:** Check off each day. Share on Instagram with **#MsPaulSelfCare @mspaultherapies** for accountability.

---

## Week 1: Body Basics

| Day | Task | Time | Done? |
|-----|------|------|-------|
| 1 | Drink 8 glasses of water today (set hourly reminders) | All day | [ ] |
| 2 | Go for a 20-minute walk — no phone, no music. Just observe. | 20 min | [ ] |
| 3 | Sleep by 10:30pm tonight. No screens after 10pm. | Evening | [ ] |
| 4 | Cook one meal from scratch today (nothing fancy — dal chawal counts) | 30 min | [ ] |
| 5 | Do a 10-minute stretch routine (YouTube: "10 min morning stretch") | 10 min | [ ] |
| 6 | Take a long shower/bath. Actually enjoy it — use the nice soap. | 15 min | [ ] |
| 7 | Spend 15 minutes in sunlight (balcony, park, walk) | 15 min | [ ] |

**Week 1 Reflection:** "Taking care of my body this week felt... The hardest day was... The easiest was..."

---

## Week 2: Mind & Emotions

| Day | Task | Time | Done? |
|-----|------|------|-------|
| 8 | Journal for 10 minutes: "How am I really doing?" | 10 min | [ ] |
| 9 | Meditate for 5 minutes (use a free app or just sit and breathe) | 5 min | [ ] |
| 10 | Digital detox: No social media from 6pm to 9am tomorrow | 15 hrs | [ ] |
| 11 | Write 10 things you're grateful for (be specific) | 10 min | [ ] |
| 12 | Say NO to one thing you don't genuinely want to do | 1 min | [ ] |
| 13 | Watch/read/listen to something that makes you FEEL (a movie, poem, song) | 30 min | [ ] |
| 14 | Do the Self-Compassion Break: hand on heart, 3 kind phrases | 5 min | [ ] |

**Week 2 Reflection:** "Focusing on my mind and emotions this week, I noticed... The day that changed something was..."

---

## Week 3: Connection & Growth

| Day | Task | Time | Done? |
|-----|------|------|-------|
| 15 | Call (not text) someone you haven't spoken to in a while | 15 min | [ ] |
| 16 | Write a thank-you message to someone who made a difference in your life | 10 min | [ ] |
| 17 | Try something new — a recipe, a route, a hobby, a class | 30 min | [ ] |
| 18 | Spend 30 minutes on a hobby that has nothing to do with work | 30 min | [ ] |
| 19 | Set one boundary you've been avoiding. Use the script: "I can't take that on right now." | 1 min | [ ] |
| 20 | Spend 1 hour doing absolutely nothing productive. Rest without guilt. | 60 min | [ ] |
| 21 | Write a letter to your future self: "One year from now, I hope..." | 15 min | [ ] |

**Week 3 Reflection:** "Connecting and growing this week showed me... The bravest thing I did was..."
