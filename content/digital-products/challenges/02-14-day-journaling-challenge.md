## Rules
- Write for at least 10 minutes per prompt
- No editing, no crossing out — let it flow
- There is no wrong answer
- You can write by hand or digitally (hand is recommended — it slows you down and engages different brain areas)
- Do it at the same time each day to build the habit

---

## Week 1: Looking Inward

**Day 1 — The Honest Check-In**
"Right now, I am feeling... And if I'm being completely honest with myself, what's really going on is..."

**Day 2 — The Unsent Letter**
Write a letter to someone you need to say something to — but don't send it. Say everything you've been holding back.

**Day 3 — The Childhood Question**
"What did I need to hear as a child that I never heard? What would it mean to tell myself that now?"

**Day 4 — The Fear Inventory**
"My biggest fears right now are... The worst-case scenario is... The most LIKELY scenario is... What I can control is..."

**Day 5 — The Joy List**
List 20 things that bring you joy — big and small. Circle the 3 you haven't done recently. Schedule one this week.

**Day 6 — The Body Letter**
Write a letter to your body. Thank it for what it does. Apologise for how you've treated it. Make one promise.

**Day 7 — Week 1 Reflection**
"This week I learned about myself that... The most surprising entry was Day ___. I'm feeling..."

---

## Week 2: Looking Forward

**Day 8 — The Values Check**
"The 5 things I value most in life are... How well am I living according to these values right now? Where is the biggest gap?"

**Day 9 — The Relationship Audit**
"The people who fill my cup are... The people who drain my cup are... What am I going to do about this?"

**Day 10 — The Forgiveness Entry**
"Something I need to forgive someone for is... Something I need to forgive MYSELF for is... What would it feel like to let this go?"

**Day 11 — The Dream Day**
Describe your perfect ordinary day — from waking up to going to sleep. Not a vacation. A regular Tuesday that makes you feel alive.

**Day 12 — The Younger You**
Write a letter to your 15-year-old self. What would you tell them? What do they need to know about who you become?

**Day 13 — The Future You**
Write a letter FROM your 80-year-old self to you now. What advice would they give? What would they say matters?

**Day 14 — The Closing Entry**
"Over 14 days of journaling, I discovered... I want to remember... Going forward, I will... A message to future me on a bad day is..."

## After the Challenge

**Journaling Maintenance Plan:**
- [ ] Continue daily (10 min)
- [ ] Switch to 3x per week
- [ ] Weekly reflection only
- [ ] Freestyle — journal when I need to

**Prompts for ongoing journaling:**
- "Today I'm feeling... because..."
- "Something I'm proud of this week..."
- "Something I'm struggling with..."
- "What would self-compassion look like right now?"
- "One thing I'm grateful for that I usually overlook..."
