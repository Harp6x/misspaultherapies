### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** Typeform/Tally, Instagram, Kit, fun shareable
**Format:** 10-question quiz → personality result → tailored self-care plan
**Viral mechanism:** Light, fun, highly shareable results

---

## Quiz Landing
**WHAT'S YOUR SELF-CARE PERSONALITY?**
*Find out what kind of self-care actually works for you.*

---

## Questions

### Q1: Your ideal day off looks like:
- A) A long run, gym session, or hike
- B) Journaling in a cosy corner with tea
- C) Brunch with friends, laughing all afternoon
- D) Doing absolutely nothing — phone off, world out

### Q2: When stressed, you naturally reach for:
- A) Movement — walking, cleaning, working out
- B) Processing — thinking, writing, talking it through
- C) People — calling someone, being around others
- D) Escape — sleep, Netflix, comfort food

### Q3: The self-care activity that sounds BEST:
- A) Yoga class or morning run
- B) Therapy session or journaling
- C) Game night or dinner party
- D) Long bath with candles and no obligations

### Q4: Your phone battery at 5% is a metaphor for:
- A) "I need to move my body"
- B) "I need to process my feelings"
- C) "I need connection"
- D) "I need to be alone and recharge"

### Q5: In a group, you're the one who:
- A) Suggests the activity or leads the plan
- B) Checks in on how everyone's doing
- C) Keeps the energy up and everyone laughing
- D) Is quiet but present — recharges by observing

### Q6: What drains you MOST?
- A) Being sedentary for too long
- B) Suppressing emotions or not being heard
- C) Loneliness or disconnection
- D) Overstimulation — too many people/plans/noise

### Q7: Your recharge ritual after a hard day:
- A) Dance party in the kitchen, walk, stretch
- B) Brain dump in my journal, call my therapist
- C) FaceTime my best friend
- D) PJs by 7pm, phone on DND, bed by 9

### Q8: The compliment that means most:
- A) "You're so strong/energetic"
- B) "You're so self-aware/deep"
- C) "You're the best friend/host/partner"
- D) "You're so peaceful/calm to be around"

### Q9: Self-care FOMO — what would you hate to miss?
- A) A fitness class or outdoor adventure
- B) A therapy breakthrough or powerful insight
- C) A birthday party or reunion
- D) A cancelled plan (free evening = heaven)

### Q10: Your self-care motto:
- A) "Sweat it out"
- B) "Feel it to heal it"
- C) "Love heals everything"
- D) "Peace is my priority"

---

## Results

### Mostly A: THE MOVER
**Your self-care is physical.** You process stress through your body. Movement is medicine for you.

**Your ideal self-care plan:**
- Morning walk or run (non-negotiable)
- Yoga 2-3x/week
- Dance breaks during the workday
- Outdoor time every weekend
- Stretch before bed

---

### Mostly B: THE PROCESSOR
**Your self-care is emotional/intellectual.** You need to make sense of things to feel okay. Reflection is your recharge.

**Your ideal self-care plan:**
- Daily journaling (even 5 min)
- Weekly therapy or coaching
- Monthly "processing day" — solo, unstructured, reflective
- Books that make you think
- Meaningful conversations > small talk

---

### Mostly C: THE CONNECTOR
**Your self-care is social.** You refuel through love, laughter, and belonging. Isolation drains you.

**Your ideal self-care plan:**
- Weekly plan with a friend (even a quick coffee)
- Daily check-in text with someone you love
- Monthly gathering you host or attend
- Community involvement (volunteering, groups, classes)
- Date nights if partnered

---

### Mostly D: THE RESTER
**Your self-care is solitude and stillness.** You need space, quiet, and permission to do nothing. The world is loud — your self-care turns the volume down.

**Your ideal self-care plan:**
- Phone-free mornings
- Weekly "nothing" evening (no plans, no screens)
- Cosy rituals: tea, candles, reading, baths
- Saying no to 1 plan per week (no guilt)
- Solo walks in nature
