## Quiz Landing

## Questions (Rate 0 = Never, 1 = Sometimes, 2 = Often, 3 = Always)

1. I feel exhausted even after a full night's sleep.
2. I dread going to work / starting my day.
3. I feel cynical or detached from my work and relationships.
4. I can't remember the last time I felt genuinely excited.
5. Small tasks feel overwhelming.
6. I get sick more often than I used to.
7. I'm using caffeine, alcohol, or food to get through the day.
8. I've stopped doing hobbies or activities I used to enjoy.
9. I feel like nothing I do makes a difference.
10. I feel irritable, short-tempered, or emotionally numb.

---

## Scoring

**0-8: Green Zone — Stressed but Coping**
You're managing, but pay attention to the early signs. Prevention is easier than recovery. Focus on sleep, boundaries, and regular breaks.

**9-17: Yellow Zone — Burnout Warning**
You're beyond normal stress. Burnout is building. This is the intervention point — changes made NOW prevent a full crash. Prioritise rest, set work boundaries, and consider talking to someone.

**18-24: Red Zone — Active Burnout**
You are burned out. This is not laziness or weakness — it's your mind and body demanding change. You need more than a vacation. You need a structural shift in how you work and live.

**25-30: Crisis Zone — Severe Burnout**
You may be experiencing burnout-related depression. Please reach out to a mental health professional. This level of burnout doesn't resolve with self-help alone.

### Red Zone
"This is your body's emergency brake. Listen to it."

"You need support. There's no shame in that."
