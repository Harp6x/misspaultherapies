# Interactive Quiz: Are You a People-Pleaser?
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** Typeform/Tally, Instagram, Kit quiz feature
**Format:** 12-question quiz → scored result → boundary-setting tips

---

## Quiz Landing
**ARE YOU A PEOPLE-PLEASER?**
*Or are you just... nice? Take this quiz to find out.*

---

## Questions (Yes = 1, No = 0)

1. I often say yes when I want to say no.
2. I feel responsible for other people's emotions.
3. I apologise even when something isn't my fault.
4. I avoid conflict at all costs — even at the expense of my needs.
5. I feel guilty when I put myself first.
6. I change my opinions to match whoever I'm with.
7. I over-explain my "no" because a simple "no" feels rude.
8. I feel resentful after doing favours I didn't want to do.
9. I struggle to identify what I actually want or need.
10. I'm terrified of being seen as selfish.
11. I absorb other people's moods and energy.
12. I've been told I'm "too nice" or "too accommodating."

---

## Scoring

**0-3: Healthy Helper**
You're kind and considerate, but you can also advocate for yourself. You say no when you need to and don't carry guilt about it. Keep it up.

**4-7: Recovering People-Pleaser**
You recognise the pattern but still fall into it. Some situations (family, work, close relationships) are harder than others. You're growing — keep practising boundaries.

**8-10: Active People-Pleaser**
People-pleasing is your default mode. You've built your identity around being needed, and the thought of disappointing someone feels physically uncomfortable. This is costing you your energy, identity, and peace.

**11-12: Full-Time People-Pleaser**
You've been prioritising everyone else for so long that you may not even know what YOU want anymore. This isn't kindness — it's self-abandonment. You deserve support to reclaim yourself.

---

## Result Tips

### For Active/Full-Time People-Pleasers:
1. **Practice the Pause:** Before saying yes, say "Let me think about it" (buys time)
2. **The 24-Hour Rule:** Don't commit to anything on the spot
3. **Start small:** Say no to one low-stakes thing this week
4. **Remember:** "No" is a complete sentence. You owe no explanation.
5. **Ask yourself:** "If I say yes to this, what am I saying no to?"
