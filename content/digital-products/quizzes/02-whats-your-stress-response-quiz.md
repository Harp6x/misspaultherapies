## Questions

### Q1: Your boss criticises your work in a meeting. You:
- A) Push back immediately: "I disagree, here's why"
- B) Leave the room as soon as possible, spiral later
- C) Go blank — can't think of anything to say
- D) Apologise and ask what you can do better (even if you didn't do anything wrong)

### Q2: A friend says something hurtful. Your first instinct:
- A) Confront them: "That wasn't okay"
- B) Distance yourself — stop replying, avoid plans
- C) Feel nothing in the moment, process it days later
- D) Laugh it off and change the subject to keep the peace

### Q3: When overwhelmed, your body tends to feel:
- A) Hot, tense, restless — like I could explode
- B) Fidgety, can't sit still, need to move/do something
- C) Heavy, foggy, numb — like I'm underwater
- D) Tight stomach, but smiling on the outside

### Q4: In a heated argument with your partner, you:
- A) Raise your voice, stand your ground, fight to be heard
- B) Walk out, need space, go for a drive
- C) Shut down completely, can't speak
- D) Agree with them to end the fight, even if you don't mean it

### Q5: A deadline is approaching and you're behind. You:
- A) Power through aggressively, maybe snap at people
- B) Work frantically, can't stop, skip meals
- C) Freeze — open the document, stare at it, close it
- D) Ask everyone if they need help before doing your own work

### Q6: Your go-to coping when stressed:
- A) Venting, confrontation, intense exercise
- B) Staying busy, overworking, cleaning, planning
- C) Sleeping, scrolling, zoning out, Netflix
- D) Taking care of everyone else, forgetting my own needs

### Q7: When someone is angry at you, you feel:
- A) Angry back — ready to defend yourself
- B) Panicked — need to escape the situation
- C) Numb — like the sound gets muffled
- D) Desperate to fix it — "What can I do to make you happy?"

### Q8: Your childhood survival strategy was:
- A) Stand your ground, fight back, be tough
- B) Stay alert, be ready to run or avoid conflict
- C) Disappear, be quiet, make yourself invisible
- D) Be the good kid, take care of everyone, don't cause trouble

### Q9: Which statement resonates most?
- A) "I'd rather fight than feel helpless"
- B) "I need to keep moving or I'll fall apart"
- C) "I can't think or feel anything when things get bad"
- D) "If I make everyone happy, maybe I'll be safe"

### Q10: Under stress, your relationships tend to suffer because:
- A) I get aggressive, critical, or controlling
- B) I pull away, get distant, or disappear
- C) I become emotionally unavailable — checked out
- D) I lose myself trying to be what they need

### Q11: Which physical symptom do you relate to most under stress?
- A) Jaw clenching, headaches, tight fists
- B) Racing heart, restless legs, insomnia
- C) Fatigue, brain fog, heaviness
- D) Stomach knots, forcing smiles, tension in shoulders

### Q12: What you need most to heal:
- A) To feel safe enough to lower my guard
- B) To learn I can stay without running
- C) To reconnect with my feelings and body
- D) To learn I matter even when I'm not useful

---

## Scoring

### Mostly A: FIGHT
Your nervous system activates through confrontation and control. Underneath the fight is usually fear or pain you've learned to armour over. **Growth edge:** Vulnerability, softening, expressing hurt instead of anger.

### Mostly B: FLIGHT
Your nervous system activates through escape and busyness. Stillness feels threatening. **Growth edge:** Learning to stay, sit with discomfort, rest without guilt.

### Mostly C: FREEZE
Your nervous system shuts down under threat. Numbness is your protection. **Growth edge:** Reconnecting with your body and emotions, slowly and safely.

### Mostly D: FAWN
Your nervous system copes by appeasing. You've learned that your safety depends on others' approval. **Growth edge:** Finding your own voice, learning your needs matter too.
