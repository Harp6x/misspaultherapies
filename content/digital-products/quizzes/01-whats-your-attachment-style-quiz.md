## Questions (Score: A = Secure, B = Anxious, C = Avoidant, D = Fearful-Avoidant)

### Q1: When your partner doesn't text back for a few hours, you:
- A) Assume they're busy and carry on with your day
- B) Check your phone repeatedly and wonder if you said something wrong
- C) Don't really notice or care

### Q2: During conflict with a partner, you tend to:
- A) Stay calm and try to talk it through
- B) Get emotional, cry, pursue them to resolve it immediately
- C) Shut down, go quiet, or leave the conversation
- D) Swing between pursuing and withdrawing — unpredictable even to yourself

### Q3: How do you feel about emotional vulnerability?
- A) Comfortable sharing feelings with someone I trust
- B) I share a lot — sometimes too quickly

- A) Feel happy and lean into the connection
- B) Feel relieved and want even more closeness

- A) I don't have a dominant fear — I'm generally secure
- B) Being abandoned or not being enough

### Q6: After a breakup, you typically:
- A) Grieve, process, and eventually move forward
- B) Replay everything obsessively, desperate for closure
- C) Move on quickly and don't look back

### Q7: How would friends describe you in relationships?
- A) Balanced and communicative
- B) All-in, giving, sometimes too accommodating

- A) Ask for it directly

### Q9: How do you feel about your partner having close friends of the opposite sex?
- A) Fine, as long as there's transparency

### Q10: Your relationship with your parents growing up was:
- A) Warm, reliable, mostly consistent
- B) Unpredictable — sometimes present, sometimes not

### Q11: "I love you" — when you hear it:

### Q12: How do you feel about sleeping at your partner's place?

### Q13: When you feel disconnected from your partner, you:
- A) Bring it up: "Hey, I've noticed we've been distant"
- B) Become anxious, clingy, or test them

- A) A healthy balance of together and alone time
- B) As much as possible — I feel safest when we're close

- A) Okay, I'd give them space and check in later

## Scoring

Count your most frequent letter:

### Mostly A: SECURE
You're comfortable with intimacy and independence. You communicate needs, handle conflict well, and trust your partner. This is the "earned" style — some people are born into it, others build it through self-awareness and therapy.

### Mostly B: ANXIOUS-PREOCCUPIED
You crave closeness and fear abandonment. You may over-give, over-analyse, and need frequent reassurance. In relationships, you tend to lose yourself. The good news: awareness is the first step to earning security.

### Mostly C: AVOIDANT-DISMISSIVE
You value independence and emotional self-sufficiency. Intimacy feels threatening. You may appear "cold" but are actually protecting a deep vulnerability. Learning to let people in — slowly — is your growth edge.

### Mostly D: FEARFUL-AVOIDANT (DISORGANISED)
You want love but fear it. You push-pull in relationships, confusing yourself and your partner. This style is often linked to early trauma. Therapy can help you build the safety you never had.
