## The Science

- Less sunlight → less serotonin → lower mood
- Less sunlight → more melatonin → more sleepiness
- Humidity → physical fatigue
- Reduced outdoor activity → less exercise → less dopamine
- Gloomy skies → brain priming for sadness

## The Monsoon Self-Care Plan

### Light
- [ ] Sit near a window for 30 min each morning
- [ ] Keep indoor lights bright during the day
- [ ] Consider a SAD lamp if working from home

### Movement
- [ ] Indoor exercise: yoga, dance, bodyweight
- [ ] Stretch every 2 hours
- [ ] Walk during rain breaks (petrichor is mood-boosting)

### Food
- [ ] Warm, nutritious meals (soups, dal, khichdi)
- [ ] Limit comfort eating (acknowledge the craving, choose consciously)
- [ ] Stay hydrated (thirst signals get muffled in cool weather)

### Connection
- [ ] Plan indoor hangouts (chai sessions, games, cooking together)
- [ ] Call someone if you can't meet
- [ ] Don't isolate — rainy days amplify loneliness

### Joy
- [ ] Monsoon playlist
- [ ] Re-read a favourite book
- [ ] Light candles or diyas
- [ ] Journal by the window
- [ ] Petrichor walks
- [ ] Hot chocolate or masala chai ritual

### Routine
- [ ] Wake at the same time daily (resist oversleeping)
- [ ] Get dressed (don't spend all day in pyjamas)
- [ ] Maintain one productive task per day

## When It's More Than the Weather

If low mood persists for 2+ weeks regardless of rain, if you've lost interest in everything, or if you're having dark thoughts — this may be more than seasonal blues. Please reach out.
