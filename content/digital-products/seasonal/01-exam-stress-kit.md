## For Students

### Before Exams
- Break syllabus into daily chunks (visible progress reduces anxiety)
- Study for 45 min → break for 15 min (Pomodoro technique)
- Sleep 7–8 hours — your brain consolidates memories during sleep
- Exercise 20 min daily — improves focus and reduces cortisol
- Avoid comparing your preparation with others

### During Exams
- **Morning of:** 4-7-8 breathing (4 inhale, 7 hold, 8 exhale) × 3
- **If you blank out:** Put your pen down. Ground yourself (feet on floor, hands on desk). Breathe. Start with what you DO know.
- **Between papers:** Don't discuss answers. It only creates anxiety. Rest.

### Emergency Anxiety Reset (60 seconds)
1. Press both feet firmly into the floor
2. Name 5 things you can see
3. Take 3 slow breaths
4. Tell yourself: "I am prepared enough. I will do my best."

### Affirmations
- "My worth is not my marks"
- "I am more than this exam"
- "I have survived every exam before this one"
- "Doing my best is enough"

## For Parents

### Do
- Ask "How are you feeling?" not just "How much have you studied?"
- Ensure they're eating, sleeping, and taking breaks
- Reduce household stress during exam periods
- Remind them you love them regardless of results
- Share your own experiences of academic pressure

### Don't
- Compare them with cousins, neighbours, or classmates
- Use fear as motivation ("You'll ruin your life")
- Take away all leisure time
- Hover while they study
- React with anger to low scores — respond with support
