## Views Included

### 📅 Calendar View
- Colour-coded by mood (green = great → red = bad)
- Click any day to see full entry

### 📊 Weekly Dashboard
- Average mood, anxiety, energy for the week
- Habit completion rate (% of checkboxes)
- Best day / Worst day

### 📈 Monthly Trends
- Board view grouped by mood
- See patterns at a glance

### 🔍 Filter: Low Days
- Filter: Mood Score ≤ 4
- Quick view of what triggers low days

### ✅ Habit Streak
- Gallery view of habit checkboxes
- Visual streak tracker

## Setup Instructions (for user)

1. Duplicate this template to your Notion workspace
2. Star/favourite the Daily Log database
3. Create a daily reminder (phone alarm) to log at the same time
4. Review weekly dashboard every Sunday
5. Share monthly trends with your therapist (optional)
