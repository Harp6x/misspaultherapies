### Pre-Filled Activity Library (50+ ideas)

**Physical (10)**
- 20-minute walk
- Yoga (YouTube follow-along)
- Dance to 3 songs
- Stretch for 10 minutes
- Cook a nourishing meal
- Take a long shower/bath
- Sleep before 11pm
- Drink 8 glasses of water
- Go for a run
- Try a new recipe

**Emotional (10)**
- Journal for 15 minutes
- Cry if you need to
- Call your therapist
- Write an unsent letter
- Practice self-compassion break
- Watch something that makes you laugh
- Say no to one thing
- Set a boundary
- Do a body scan
- Listen to a playlist that matches your mood

**Social (10)**
- Text a friend you miss
- Have a phone/video call
- Cook with someone
- Go for a walk with a friend
- Send a voice note
- Write a thank-you message
- Join a class/group
- Ask someone "how are you really?"
- Plan a low-key hangout
- Help someone with something

**Mental (10)**
- Read 10 pages
- Listen to a podcast
- Do a puzzle
- Learn something new (10 min)
- Digital detox for 2 hours
- Brain dump everything on your mind
- Organise one small space
- Set 3 priorities for the week
- Watch a documentary
- Write a to-do list and do ONE thing

**Spiritual (10)**
- 10-minute meditation
- Spend time in nature
- Practice gratitude (3 things)
- Prayer or mantra
- Watch the sunrise/sunset
- Breathwork (4-7-8)
- Sit in silence for 5 minutes
- Read something inspiring
- Light a candle and be still
- Write about what gives your life meaning

## Bonus: Self-Care Emergency Kit

For when everything feels like too much:
1. Breathe — 3 deep breaths
2. Water — drink a full glass
3. Move — stand up, stretch
4. Connect — text one person
5. Rest — give yourself permission to do nothing
