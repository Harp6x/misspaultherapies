## Pages Included

### 🎯 Dashboard Home
- Life Areas wheel visualization (instructions to create in Notion)
- Active goals (filtered: Status = In progress)
- This week's priorities
- Inspirational quote (rotating)

### 📅 Quarterly Planning
Template:
- Quarter: Q__ 20__
- Theme for this quarter: ____
- Top 3 goals: ____
- What I'll say NO to: ____
- Support I need: ____
- Review date: ____

### 🏆 Achievement Wall
Gallery view of completed goals with:
- What I achieved
- How long it took
- What I learned
- How I celebrated

### 💡 Values Page
- My top 5 values: ____
- How each goal connects to my values
- Decision-making filter: "Does this align with my values?"

### 📊 Monthly Review
- Goals achieved vs set
- Life areas change
- Biggest lesson
- Adjustments for next month

---

## Setup Instructions

1. Duplicate template
2. Complete Life Areas Assessment first
3. Set 3-5 goals for the quarter (not more)
4. Do weekly reviews every Sunday (15 min)
5. Celebrate every completed goal (non-negotiable)
