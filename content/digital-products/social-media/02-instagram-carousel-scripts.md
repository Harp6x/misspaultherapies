## Carousel 1: "5 Signs Your Anxiety Is Talking, Not You"

**Slide 1 (Cover):** 5 Signs Your Anxiety Is Talking, Not You | @mspaultherapies
**Slide 2:** 1. You catastrophise — "What if the WORST happens?"
**Slide 3:** 2. You mind-read — "They definitely think I'm stupid"
**Slide 4:** 3. You avoid — cancelling plans, procrastinating, withdrawing
**Slide 5:** 4. You seek constant reassurance — "Are you sure it's okay?"
**Slide 6:** 5. You can't distinguish worry from reality — "I feel it, so it must be true"
**Slide 7:** The truth? These are patterns, not personality. And patterns can be changed.
**Slide 8 (CTA):** Save this. Share with someone who needs it. 💛 Follow @mspaultherapies for more.

**Caption:** Anxiety speaks loudly. But it doesn't speak truth. Next time you catch yourself in one of these patterns, try: "Is this a fact, or is this my anxiety talking?" ## Carousel 3: "The Gottman 4 Horsemen (Relationship Killers)"

**Slide 1:** 4 Relationship Killers (And What to Do Instead) | @mspaultherapies
**Slide 2:** 🐴 1. CRITICISM — "You always..." "You never..." → TRY: "I feel ___ when ___. I need ___."
**Slide 3:** 🐴 2. CONTEMPT — Eye-rolling, sarcasm, mockery → TRY: Express appreciation daily. Build a culture of respect.
**Slide 4:** 🐴 3. DEFENSIVENESS — "It's not MY fault" → TRY: "I can see your point. What can I do differently?"
**Slide 5:** 🐴 4. STONEWALLING — Shutting down, withdrawing → TRY: "I need a break. Let's come back in 20 minutes."
**Slide 6:** Contempt is the #1 predictor of divorce (Gottman research)
**Slide 7:** The antidote to ALL four? Intentional kindness + repair.
**Slide 8 (CTA):** Couples therapy can help. Free discovery call: mspaultherapies.com/book | @mspaultherapies
