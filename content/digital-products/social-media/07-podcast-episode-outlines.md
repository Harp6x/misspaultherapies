# 12 Podcast Episode Outlines
### "Let's Talk About It" — A Mental Health Podcast by @mspaultherapies

**Intro tagline:** "Real conversations about mental health — for Indians who are ready to talk about it."

## Episode 1: "Why I Became a Therapist"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min

- My journey to clinical psychology
- What therapy means to me personally
- The state of mental health in India
- What this podcast will cover
- Invitation to listeners: DM me your topics

---

## Episode 2: "Therapy Is Not What You Think It Is"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 25 min

- Top 10 misconceptions about therapy
- What my clients expected vs what they experienced
- Why therapy is a skill, not magic
- How to know if you're ready (spoiler: you already are)

## Episode 3: "The Indian Family & Mental Health" (Guest: Another Therapist)
**Type:** Interview | **Duration:** 30 min

- Generational trauma in Indian families
- "Log kya kahenge" — the mental health tax
- How to honour your family AND prioritise your wellbeing
- Stories from clinical practice (anonymous)
- Cultural sensitivity in therapy

---

## Episode 4: "Anxiety in the Age of Instagram"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min

- Social media and comparison culture
- The dopamine loop: why you can't stop scrolling
- Digital boundaries that actually work
- The 3 social media rules I give my clients

---

## Episode 5: "What Nobody Tells You About Couples Therapy"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 25 min

- It's not about who's right
- The Gottman 4 Horsemen (deep dive)
- Why couples wait too long
- "My partner won't go" — what to do
- What a couples session looks like

---

## Episode 6: "Burnout Is Not a Badge of Honour"
**Type:** Solo or Guest (HR professional) | **Duration:** 25 min

- The hustle culture lie
- 3 stages of burnout and where you might be
- Recovery is not just a holiday
- What companies should be doing (but aren't)
- Listener exercise: the burnout audit

---

## Episode 7: "Parenting Without Breaking Your Kid"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 25 min

- The 4 parenting styles and why it matters
- Things we say without thinking and the impact
- Emotional coaching: validating before fixing
- The repair conversation: what to do after you've yelled
- Raising emotionally intelligent children in an Indian context

---

## Episode 8: "Money & Mental Health"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min

- Financial stress and anxiety
- The shame around money in India
- EMIs, loans, and lifestyle inflation
- How money trauma from childhood shows up in adulthood
- 3 steps to a healthier money mindset

---

## Episode 9: "Grief Doesn't Follow a Calendar"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 25 min

- The wave model of grief (vs stages)
- Disenfranchised grief — losses nobody validates
- Grieving during festivals and celebrations
- How to support someone who's grieving
- When grief needs professional support

---

## Episode 10: "The People-Pleaser Recovery Episode"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min

- Where people-pleasing comes from
- The cost: resentment, burnout, loss of identity
- 5 signs you're a people-pleaser
- How to say no (with scripts)
- Recovery is a practice, not a switch

---

## Episode 11: "Men and Mental Health in India"
**Type:** Guest episode (male therapist or advocate) | **Duration:** 30 min

- "Men don't cry" — the cost of toxic masculinity
- Men's mental health statistics in India
- Anger as the only acceptable emotion
- How therapy helps men (not despite masculinity, through it)
- Reaching men who won't ask for help

---

## Episode 12: "Year-End Reflection Special"
**Type:** Solo | **Duration:** 25 min

- A guided reflection exercise (listeners follow along)
- What I learned this year as a therapist
- Listener highlights/DMs (with permission)
- Setting intentions (not resolutions) for next year
- Thank you + what's coming in Season 2
