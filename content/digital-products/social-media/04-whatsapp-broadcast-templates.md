## April

Hi! 💛 April is Stress Awareness Month.

Quick stress check: Rate your stress 1-10 right now.

If it's above 6, try this RIGHT NOW:
- Double inhale through nose
- Long exhale through mouth
- Repeat 3x

Felt something shift? That's the physiological sigh. 10 seconds. Science-backed.

More tools: mspaultherapies.com/guide
