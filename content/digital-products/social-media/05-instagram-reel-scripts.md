## Reel 7: "Your morning anxiety is normal"
**Duration:** 20s | **Format:** Talking head

Cortisol peaks 30–45 minutes after waking — it's called the cortisol awakening response.

Don't check your phone first thing. Instead: stretch, breathe, drink water. Give your nervous system 30 minutes before the world rushes in.

## Reel 18: "How to stop overthinking tonight"
**Duration:** 30s | **Format:** Talking head, bedroom setting

"Can't sleep because your brain won't shut up? Try this:

1. Brain dump — write EVERYTHING in your head on paper. Every thought. Even the weird ones.
2. For each thought, write: 'Can I do anything about this RIGHT NOW?' If no, it waits until tomorrow.
3. Put the paper face down. You've given your brain permission to let go.
4. Physiological sigh × 5.

It won't work perfectly the first time. But your brain learns. @mspaultherapies"
