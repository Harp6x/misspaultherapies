## Detailed Script

### Welcome (10 min)

"Welcome. I'm Aishani Paul, clinical psychologist at Ms Paul Therapies.

Let me ask you something: When was the last time you rested — and I mean truly rested, without guilt?

Drop your answer in chat. Days? Weeks? Can't remember?

If you can't remember, you're exactly where you need to be today.

Burnout isn't about being lazy. It's about being depleted. And today, we're going to understand it and fight back."

### What Is Burnout? (15 min)

"The WHO officially recognised burnout in 2019. It has 3 dimensions:

1. **Exhaustion** — emotional and physical depletion ('I have nothing left')
2. **Cynicism** — detachment from work and people ('I don't care anymore')
3. **Reduced efficacy** — feeling incompetent ('Nothing I do matters')

[SLIDE: The 5 Stages of Burnout]
1. Honeymoon Phase — high energy, optimism
2. Onset of Stress — some days are hard, sleep affected
3. Chronic Stress — consistent physical symptoms, irritability, social withdrawal
4. Burnout — can't function normally, dread, emptiness
5. Habitual Burnout — physical and mental health conditions, crisis

Where are you? Be honest with yourself."

### Interactive: Burnout Audit (15 min)

"Rate each statement 1 (never) to 5 (always):

1. I feel emotionally drained by my work
2. I dread Monday mornings
3. I feel unappreciated
4. I can't switch off from work thoughts
5. I'm more irritable than usual
6. I've lost interest in hobbies
7. I feel guilty when I rest
8. I get sick more often than before
9. I feel like I'm just surviving, not living
10. I can't remember the last time I felt excited about something

**Score:** 10–20 = Healthy range. 21–35 = Stress onset. 36–50 = Burnout zone.

Drop your score range in chat (not the exact number — just Healthy/Stress/Burnout)."

### The Recovery Framework (15 min)

"Recovery has 3 phases:

**1. RESTORE (Immediate)**
- Sleep: non-negotiable 7+ hours. Everything else fails without this.
- Movement: 20 min daily. Walk, stretch, dance. Not punishment, just movement.
- One pleasure activity per day: chai by the window, 10 pages of a book, a song.

**2. REFRAME (Weeks 2–4)**
- Identify your 'should' stories: 'I should be available 24/7' → 'I'm allowed to have off-hours'
- Separate identity from productivity: you are not your output
- Ask: 'Would I let my best friend live like this?'

**3. REBUILD (Ongoing)**
- Redesign your schedule with recovery built in
- Set boundaries (we'll practice these next)
- Regular check-ins with yourself: weekly 10-minute audit"

### Boundary Setting Masterclass (15 min)

"Burnout recovery without boundaries is just a vacation before the same cycle.

**Work boundaries:**
- 'I'll respond to that first thing tomorrow.'
- 'I'm not available after 7pm unless it's an emergency.'
- 'I need to check my capacity before committing.'

**Family boundaries:**
- 'I love you and I need some quiet time right now.'
- 'I can't take on that responsibility. Let's find another solution.'
- 'I need this Sunday for myself.'

**Self-boundaries:**
- No phone in bed
- No emails after dinner
- One commitment-free evening per week

Practice: Pick ONE boundary you'll set this week. Write it down."

### The Non-Negotiable 5 (10 min)

"Your daily burnout prevention checklist:

1. **Sleep 7+ hours** (protect bedtime like a meeting)
2. **Move 20 minutes** (any movement counts)
3. **One thing just for you** (not productive, just pleasant)
4. **Say no to one thing** (or 'not right now')
5. **10 minutes offline** (no screens, no input)

That's it. 5 things. Every day. This is how you prevent burnout, not just recover from it."
