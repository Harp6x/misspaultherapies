## Key Content

### The 4 Horsemen (Gottman)

"Dr. John Gottman studied thousands of couples and found 4 communication patterns that predict relationship failure with 93% accuracy:

1. **Criticism** — attacking character, not behaviour. 'You ALWAYS forget.' 'You NEVER listen.'
   **Antidote:** Soft startup. 'I feel ___ when ___. I need ___.'

2. **Contempt** — eye-rolling, sarcasm, mockery, name-calling. The #1 predictor of divorce.
   **Antidote:** Build a culture of appreciation. Express gratitude daily.

3. **Defensiveness** — deflecting, counter-attacking, playing victim.
   **Antidote:** Take responsibility, even for a small part. 'You're right, I could have done that differently.'

4. **Stonewalling** — shutting down, withdrawing, the silent treatment.
   **Antidote:** Self-soothe, then return. 'I need 20 minutes. I'll come back and we'll continue.'"

### Soft Startup Practice

"Rewrite these complaints:

❌ 'You never help with housework. You're so lazy.'
✅ 'I feel overwhelmed when I'm doing housework alone. Could we split tasks this weekend?'

❌ 'You always choose your friends over me.'
✅ 'I miss spending time together. Can we plan something just us this week?'

Now write YOUR OWN: Think of a recent complaint. Rewrite it as: 'I feel ___ when ___. I need ___.'"

### The Repair Checklist (After a Fight)

1. Cool down first (minimum 20 minutes)
2. One person at a time — speaker/listener structure
3. Each person shares: 'I felt...' 'I heard you say...' 'What I needed was...'
4. Acknowledge your part
5. Apologise specifically ('I'm sorry I raised my voice' not 'I'm sorry you're upset')
6. Discuss what you'd do differently next time
7. Physical reconnection (hug, hold hands) — if both are ready

### Love Maps — Know Your Partner

"How well do you actually KNOW your partner? Quick quiz — answer about YOUR partner:

1. What's their biggest current worry?
2. What's their dream vacation?
3. Who's their closest friend right now?
4. What's stressing them most at work?
5. What makes them feel most loved?
6. What's one thing they wish you understood better?

If you struggled with any of these, your Love Map needs updating. Schedule a 'Love Map date' — 30 minutes, just asking each other questions."

### Rituals of Connection

"**Daily:** 6-second kiss, 'How was your day?' (and actually listen), one appreciation
**Weekly:** Date night (doesn't need to be fancy), one activity together, phone-free evening
**Monthly:** State of the relationship check-in ('What's working? What needs attention?')
**Yearly:** Anniversary reflection, goals as a couple, trip/experience together"
