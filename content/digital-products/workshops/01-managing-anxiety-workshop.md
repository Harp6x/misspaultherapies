## Detailed Script

### Welcome (10 min)

"Hi everyone, I'm Aishani Paul — I'm a clinical psychologist and I run Ms Paul Therapies. I work with people dealing with anxiety, depression, relationship issues, and life transitions.

Today we're going to do something different. Instead of just talking about anxiety, we're going to PRACTICE managing it. You'll leave with actual tools you can use tonight.

Quick ground rules:
- Camera on/off is your choice
- Chat is open for questions anytime
- Everything shared here stays here
- There's no wrong answer

Let's start with a quick poll: On a scale of 1–10, how anxious are you feeling RIGHT NOW? Drop your number in the chat."

[Acknowledge responses. Normalise.]

"If you put anything above a 3 — you're in the right place. If you put a 1, you're either very zen or very good at denial."

[Light laughter. Move on.]

---

### What Is Anxiety? (15 min)

"Anxiety is your body's alarm system. It's designed to protect you from danger. The problem? In modern life, the alarm goes off when you're in a meeting, scrolling Instagram, or trying to sleep. The danger isn't real — but your body doesn't know that.

[SLIDE: The Anxiety Equation]
Anxiety = Overestimating the threat + Underestimating your ability to cope

[SLIDE: Fight-Flight-Freeze]
- **Fight:** Irritability, snapping, clenching
- **Flight:** Avoidance, cancelling plans, procrastination
- **Freeze:** Blanking out, feeling stuck, numbness

Which one do YOU default to? Drop F for fight, FL for flight, FR for freeze in the chat."

[Discuss briefly.]

"Here's the key insight: anxiety is not a character flaw. It's a pattern. And patterns can be changed."

---

### Interactive: My Anxiety Map (10 min)

"Take a piece of paper or open your notes. Draw 4 boxes:

1. **TRIGGER** — what sets off your anxiety? (situations, people, thoughts)
2. **THOUGHTS** — what does your mind say? ("What if...", "I can't...", "They think...")
3. **BODY** — where do you feel it? (chest, stomach, shoulders, throat)
4. **BEHAVIOUR** — what do you DO? (avoid, scroll, overwork, snap)

Take 3 minutes. Fill in YOUR map. This is private — you won't have to share."

[3-minute timer.]

"This map is powerful because it shows you the chain. Once you see the chain, you can intervene at any link."

---

### Tool 1: Body-Based Techniques (15 min)

"Anxiety lives in the body FIRST. So we start there.

**Technique 1: The Physiological Sigh**
This is the fastest way to calm your nervous system. Takes 10 seconds.
- Double inhale through nose (short inhale + second inhale to fill)
- Long exhale through mouth
Let's do 3 together."

[Lead 3 rounds.]

"**Technique 2: 4-7-8 Breathing**
- Inhale for 4
- Hold for 7
- Exhale for 8
The long exhale activates your parasympathetic nervous system. Let's do 2 rounds."

[Lead 2 rounds.]

"Notice anything different? Drop a word in chat — how do you feel now compared to 5 minutes ago?"

---

### Tool 2: Thought-Based Techniques (15 min)

"Now the mind. Anxiety tells you stories. Our job is to check if those stories are true.

The magic question: **Is this a fact, or is this a worry?**

Example:
- Worry: 'My boss hates me because she didn't reply to my email'
- Fact: 'My boss hasn't replied to my email yet'

See the difference? The worry adds interpretation. The fact is just what happened.

**Quick exercise:** Think of a worry you had this week. Write it down. Now rewrite it as just the facts. No interpretation, no prediction."

[2-minute pause.]

"This is called cognitive restructuring. It doesn't make the worry disappear. It right-sizes it."

---

### Tool 3: Behaviour-Based (10 min)

"Anxiety wants you to avoid. And short-term, avoidance works — you feel relief. But long-term, avoidance GROWS the anxiety.

**The 5-Minute Rule:** When you want to avoid something, commit to just 5 minutes. Open the document for 5 minutes. Go to the gym for 5 minutes. Start the conversation for 5 minutes. Most of the time, starting is the hardest part.

**Opposite Action:** When anxiety says 'cancel' — show up. When it says 'stay quiet' — speak. When it says 'don't try' — try once."

---

### Building Your Toolkit (10 min)

"Now create YOUR personal 3-step anxiety response plan:

1. **My body signal** (what I notice first): ___
2. **My go-to body technique:** ___
3. **My fact-check question:** ___

Write this on a sticky note and put it where you'll see it. This is your anxiety first aid."
