# Community Content: 30-Day Daily Mental Health Tips
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** WhatsApp Community, Telegram Channel, Instagram Stories, Newsletter
**Format:** One tip per day, copy-paste ready, with optional discussion prompt

---

## Week 1: Self-Awareness

**Day 1:** Today's check-in: On a scale of 1-10, how's your mental health? No judgment. Just notice. 📊

**Day 2:** Name one emotion you're feeling right now. Not "fine." An actual emotion. Naming it reduces its intensity by up to 50%.

**Day 3:** Your body keeps the score. Where are you holding tension right now? Jaw? Shoulders? Stomach? Take 3 breaths and send air to that spot.

**Day 4:** Write down 3 things that drained your energy this week. Now write 3 things that gave you energy. Do more of column B.

**Day 5:** Notice your inner critic today. What's it saying? Would you say that to a friend? If not, it's not helpful — it's a wound talking.

**Day 6:** Screen time check: How many hours yesterday? No shame. Just awareness. Consider one phone-free hour today.

**Day 7:** Weekly reflection: What went well? What was hard? What do I need more of next week?

## Week 2: Self-Care Basics

**Day 8:** Did you drink enough water today? Your brain is 75% water. Dehydration = brain fog, irritability, fatigue. Drink a glass right now. 💧

**Day 9:** Movement is medicine. You don't need a gym. Walk for 15 minutes today. That's it.

**Day 10:** Sleep challenge: Go to bed 30 minutes earlier tonight. Your brain repairs itself during sleep. Give it the time.

**Day 11:** Cook or eat one meal mindfully today. No phone. No TV. Just taste, smell, texture. Presence is a form of self-care.

**Day 12:** Today's boundary challenge: Say "let me think about it" before saying yes to anything. Just pause. That's all.

**Day 13:** Gratitude rewires your brain. Name 3 specific things you're grateful for today. Not generic. Specific. (E.g., "The chai my colleague made me.")

**Day 14:** Weekly reflection: How did my self-care this week compare to last week? What's one thing I'll protect next week?

## Week 3: Relationships & Connection

**Day 15:** Text someone you haven't spoken to in a while. Say: "Thinking of you." That's it. Connection doesn't need to be complicated.

**Day 16:** Ask someone "How are you, really?" today. And actually listen to the answer.

**Day 17:** Boundaries reminder: "No" is a complete sentence. You owe no one an explanation for protecting your peace.

**Day 18:** Appreciation challenge: Tell one person exactly what you appreciate about them. Be specific. Watch their face light up.

**Day 19:** Conflict tip: Replace "You always ____" with "I feel ____ when ____." It changes everything.

**Day 20:** Social media audit: Unfollow 3 accounts that don't make you feel good. Follow 3 that educate, inspire, or make you laugh.

**Day 21:** Weekly reflection: How were my relationships this week? Did I give too much? Too little? What felt balanced?

## Week 4: Growth & Resilience

**Day 22:** Reminder: You've survived 100% of your worst days. Your track record is perfect.

**Day 23:** Imposter syndrome check: You're not a fraud. You're a human learning as they go. That's not weakness — that's growth.

**Day 24:** What's one fear you can take a tiny step toward today? Not a leap. A step. Courage is built in inches.

**Day 25:** Write a 3-sentence letter to your future self, 1 year from now. What do you hope for them?

**Day 26:** Forgiveness isn't about them. It's about freeing yourself from carrying the weight. Is there something you can begin to release today?

**Day 27:** Celebrate something you did this week. Anything. Finished a task? Set a boundary? Got out of bed? That counts.

**Day 28:** Your values check: What are your top 3 values? Did your actions this week align with them?

**Day 29:** Rest is not earned. It's a right. Today, give yourself permission to do nothing for 30 minutes. No guilt.

**Day 30:** You just spent 30 days showing up for your mental health. That's not nothing. That's everything. Write one sentence about how you feel compared to Day 1.
