Hi,

The year is ending. Before the noise of "New Year New Me" starts, let's do something rare: look back with compassion.

### The Year-End Reflection

Set aside 20 minutes. Grab tea. Answer honestly:

**What happened:**
- The best thing that happened this year: _______________
- The hardest thing I went through: _______________
- Something that surprised me: _______________

**What I did:**
- Something I'm proud of (that nobody else knows about): _______________
- A risk I took: _______________
- A boundary I set: _______________
- Something I let go of: _______________

**What I learned:**
- About myself: _______________
- About relationships: _______________
- About what I actually need (vs. what I thought I needed): _______________

**What I want:**
- One thing I want MORE of next year: _______________
- One thing I want LESS of: _______________
- One word for next year: _______________

### The "Didn't Happen" List

We focus on what we achieved. But what about what DIDN'T happen?

- The breakdown you didn't have (even though you came close)
- The relationship you didn't settle for
- The toxic habit you didn't pick up
- The boundary you held (even when it was hard)
- The morning you chose to get up instead of give up

**Not-doing is also strength.**

### Permission Slip for the Holidays

You have permission to:
- Skip the party
- Not set resolutions
- Feel sad even though "it's the holidays"
- Spend time alone
- Not have your life figured out by January 1st
- Rest without earning it
