Happy Independence Day month.

While we celebrate our nation's freedom, let's talk about personal freedom — the kind that happens when you finally release something that's been weighing you down.

### 5 Things to Declare Independence From

**1. Other people's opinions.**
"Log kya kahenge" has kept generations stuck. This month, make ONE decision based purely on what YOU want.

**2. The need to be perfect.**
Good enough IS good enough. Perfectionism is anxiety wearing a productive mask.

**3. Toxic positivity.**
You don't have to be grateful all the time. Sometimes things are just hard. Let them be hard.

**4. Comparison.**
Their Instagram is a highlight reel. Your life is the unedited version. Stop comparing your behind-the-scenes to someone else's show.

**5. The belief that asking for help is weakness.**
Independence doesn't mean doing everything alone. True strength is knowing when you need support and seeking it.

### Your Freedom Declaration

Complete this: "This month, I am freeing myself from _______________ because _______________."

### The One-Boundary Challenge

Set ONE new boundary this month. Just one. Here are ideas:
- "I will not reply to work messages after 8pm"
- "I will not tolerate comments about my body"
- "I will say no to plans that drain me"
- "I will stop apologising for things that aren't my fault"
