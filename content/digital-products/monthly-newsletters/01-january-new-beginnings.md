Hi,

Every January, the world screams: NEW YOU! GOALS! HUSTLE! TRANSFORM!

And every February, most people feel like failures because the gym membership is unused and the journal is blank after Day 4.

**What if this year was different?**

Not because you achieved more. But because you **expected less of yourself and appreciated more of what's already here.**

### The Anti-Resolution

Instead of a list of things to fix about yourself, try this:

**1. One word for the year** (not a goal — a direction)
Examples: *ease, connection, growth, courage, rest, honest, present*

My word for this year: _______________

**2. Three things from last year you're proud of** (they don't have to be achievements)
1. _______________
2. _______________
3. _______________

**3. One thing you'll let go of** (a habit, a belief, a grudge, an expectation)
_______________

### This Month's Free Resource

**[The 30-Day Gratitude Journal](https://mspaultherapies.com/guide)** — start the year by rewiring your brain for positivity. One prompt per day, takes 5 minutes.

### Mental Health Tip of the Month

The "fresh start effect" is real — your brain IS more motivated at the start of a new period. But instead of using it for extreme goals, use it for **one tiny habit:**

- 5 minutes of breathing daily
- One screen-free meal per day
- Texting one friend per week

**Small > dramatic. Always.**
