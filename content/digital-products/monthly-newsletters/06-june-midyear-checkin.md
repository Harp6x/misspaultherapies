We're halfway through the year. Before the second half begins, let's pause.

### The 5-Question Mid-Year Check-In

Answer honestly. No judgement:

**1. What has taken up most of my emotional energy this year?**
_______________

**2. What's one thing I've done that I'm genuinely proud of?**
_______________

**3. What am I tolerating that I shouldn't be?**
_______________

**4. If the second half of this year could be different in ONE way, what would it be?**
_______________

**5. On a scale of 1–10, how am I really doing?**
_______________

### If Your Answer to #5 Was Below 5

That's not a failure — that's information. And the most important thing you can do with it is ACT on it.

- Below 5 for a few weeks? Start with our [Daily Mental Health Checklist](https://mspaultherapies.com/guide)
- Below 5 for months? Consider therapy — [book a free discovery call](https://mspaultherapies.com/book)

### The "Second Chance January" Mindset

June is your reset button. Those January goals that fizzled? You can restart them. But this time:

- Pick ONE thing (not ten)
- Make it specific and small
- Attach it to an existing habit
- Track it for 30 days
- Celebrate showing up, not just results
