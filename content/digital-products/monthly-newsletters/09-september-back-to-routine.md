Hi,

September has a "back to school" energy — even as adults. The monsoon is fading, festivals are approaching, and there's pressure to finish the year strong.

If you're feeling a mix of motivation and exhaustion — that's completely normal.

### The Gentle Restart Framework

Don't go from 0 to 100. Use the 50% rule:

**Whatever you THINK you should do, start with 50% of that.**

- Want to exercise 6 days? Start with 3.
- Want to wake up at 5am? Start with 6:30am.
- Want to read 2 books a month? Start with 1.
- Want to meal prep every Sunday? Start with prepping lunch only.

**Why:** Your brain resists dramatic change. Small steps create momentum without triggering the "this is too hard, let's quit" response.

### The Burnout Prevention Checklist

As the year gets busy, protect yourself:

- [ ] I have a clear end-time for work each day
- [ ] I'm sleeping 7+ hours
- [ ] I have at least one day per week with NO obligations
- [ ] I'm saying no to things that drain me
- [ ] I have something to look forward to each week
- [ ] I'm moving my body regularly
- [ ] I'm connecting with people who fill my cup

**If you checked fewer than 4:** burnout is knocking. Time to recalibrate.

### Parenting Note

If you have school-going children, September can be stressful for them too. Watch for:
- Sleep changes
- Complaints of stomach aches/headaches
- Reluctance to go to school
- Mood changes

These can be signs of anxiety. Talk to them — and if it persists, consider professional support.
