Hi,

May is Mental Health Awareness Month worldwide. In India, we're still catching up — but things are changing, and you're part of that change just by reading this.

### The State of Mental Health in India

- 150 million+ Indians need mental health support; fewer than 30 million seek it
- India has ~0.3 psychiatrists per 100,000 people (WHO recommends 3)
- 70%+ of people with mental health conditions receive no treatment
- Stigma remains the #1 barrier

### What YOU Can Do This Month

**1. Normalise the conversation.** Share this email. Post about mental health. Say "I see a therapist" if you're comfortable — it gives others permission.

**2. Check on someone.** Not "How are you?" but "How are you, really?" Sit with their answer. Don't rush to fix.

**3. Educate one person.** Share a resource. Correct a myth. Challenge stigma gently.

**4. Check on yourself.** Take the [Mental Health Self-Check](https://mspaultherapies.com/guide). Be honest.

### Myths vs. Facts

| Myth | Fact |
|------|------|
| "Therapy is for crazy people" | Therapy is for anyone who wants to understand themselves better |
| "Just think positive" | Mental health conditions involve brain chemistry, not just mindset |
| "Only weak people need help" | Seeking help requires more courage than suffering in silence |
| "It's all in your head" | Mental health affects your body, sleep, appetite, immunity, and heart |
| "Time heals everything" | Time helps. Skills, support, and sometimes treatment heal. |
