Hi,

April is Stress Awareness Month. And I want to share the most important thing I've learned about stress in my career:

**The goal is never zero stress. The goal is stress you can recover from.**

Some stress is useful (it helps you perform, focus, prepare). The problem is when stress becomes chronic — when your body never returns to baseline.

### The Stress Recovery Test

Ask yourself: After a stressful event, how long does it take me to feel calm again?

- **Minutes:** Healthy recovery. Your nervous system is resilient.
- **Hours:** Early warning. You're depleting faster than you're recovering.
- **Days:** Your stress system is stuck in "on." You need support.
- **Can't remember the last time I felt calm:** Please reach out. This isn't sustainable.

### 2-Minute Stress Reset (Do It Right Now)

1. **Exhale fully.** Push all the air out.
2. **Inhale for 4.** Fill your belly.
3. **Hold for 4.**
4. **Exhale for 8.** Slow and steady.
5. Repeat 3 times.
6. **Drop your shoulders** away from your ears.
7. **Unclench your jaw.**
8. Ask: "What is one thing I can let go of today?"

That just changed your nervous system state. In 2 minutes.

### Quick Stress Facts

- Chronic stress shrinks the prefrontal cortex (decision-making) and enlarges the amygdala (fear centre)
- Exercise reverses these changes
- Social connection is one of the most powerful stress buffers
- Sleep deprivation doubles the stress response to the same trigger
- Laughter genuinely reduces cortisol
