Hi,

October 10th is **World Mental Health Day.**

This year, instead of just posting about it, let's DO something.

### 10 Things You Can Do on October 10th

1. **Share this email** with one person you're worried about
2. **Post about mental health** on your social media (break the silence)
3. **Take the** [Emotional Wellness Assessment](https://mspaultherapies.com/guide) — know where you stand
4. **Text someone** you haven't checked on in a while: "Hey, just thinking about you"
5. **Book that therapy appointment** you've been putting off
6. **Learn one new coping skill** (try the physiological sigh — double inhale, long exhale)
7. **Set one boundary** you've been avoiding
8. **Move your body** for 30 minutes
9. **Write down** 3 things you're grateful for
10. **Go to bed early.** Rest is radical.

### A Letter to Anyone Struggling Right Now

If you're reading this and things feel dark — please hear me:

- You are not a burden
- What you're feeling is real and valid
- It will not always feel this way
- Help exists and you deserve it
- One phone call can change everything

### The State of Mental Health: Why This Day Matters

- 1 in 5 people will experience a mental health condition this year
- In India, mental health spending is less than 1% of the health budget
- The treatment gap (people who need help but don't get it) is over 80%
- Stigma is the #1 barrier — and YOU can help change that by talking about it
