February is the month of love. But let's talk about the love nobody posts about on Instagram — the kind that requires effort, repair, and showing up even when it's hard.

### 3 Relationship Truths From My Therapy Room

**1. The strongest couples fight.** They just fight differently. They attack the problem, not each other. They repair quickly. They say "I'm sorry" without keeping score.

**2. Love languages aren't just romantic.** Your parents, friends, and children also have love languages. Mismatched languages don't mean mismatched love — they mean you need to translate.

**3. The opposite of love isn't anger — it's indifference.** If you're fighting, you still care. If you've stopped caring enough to fight, that's when to worry.

### This Month's Exercise: The Appreciation Practice

Every day this week, tell your partner (or a loved one) ONE specific thing you appreciate about them:

- Not "you're great" — that's vague
- Try: "I noticed you made tea for me this morning without me asking. That made me feel cared for."

Specificity is intimacy.

### For Singles This Valentine's

Your most important relationship is with yourself. Ask:
- Am I treating myself with the kindness I want from a partner?
- What boundaries am I setting for how I allow others to treat me?
- What would it look like to date myself this month?
