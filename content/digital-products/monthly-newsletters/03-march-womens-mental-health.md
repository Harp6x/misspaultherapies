Hi,

March is Women's History Month, and International Women's Day falls on the 8th. Let's talk about something that rarely makes the empowerment posters:

**Women's mental health is in crisis — and nobody's talking about it.**

- Women are 2x more likely to experience anxiety and depression than men
- They carry the "mental load" (tracking appointments, meals, school events, everyone's emotions)
- Hormonal shifts (periods, pregnancy, perimenopause) directly affect mental health
- Indian women face additional pressure: family expectations, career guilt, marriage timelines, motherhood standards

### The Mental Load Audit

This week, track every "invisible task" you do. Not just work and chores — the thinking, planning, remembering, and worrying:
