Hi,

The monsoons are here. And if you've been feeling more tired, low, or unmotivated lately — you're not imagining it.

### The Science of Monsoon Mood

- **Less sunlight** = less serotonin (your mood-regulating neurotransmitter)
- **Less sunlight** = more melatonin (sleepiness hormone produced earlier)
- **Humidity** = physical discomfort that drains energy
- **Reduced outdoor activity** = less exercise, less social connection
- **Gloomy aesthetics** = your brain associates grey skies with sadness (priming effect)

This isn't weakness. It's biology.

### Your Monsoon Mental Health Kit

**Light:**
- Sit near a window for 30 minutes each morning
- Keep lights bright in your home during the day
- Consider a daylight lamp if you work from home

**Movement:**
- Indoor exercise: yoga, dance, bodyweight workouts
- Walk during breaks in the rain (if you enjoy it — rain can be therapeutic)
- Stretch for 5 minutes every 2 hours

**Connection:**
- Don't isolate. Plan indoor hangouts — chai dates, movie nights, board games
- Call someone if you can't meet in person
- The monsoon is a perfect excuse for cozy conversations

**Routine:**
- Wake up at the same time daily (resist the urge to oversleep)
- Eat warm, nutritious meals (your body craves comfort — give it nutrition too)
- Limit screen time before bed

**Joy:**
- Make a monsoon playlist
- Light candles or diyas
- Re-read a favourite book
- Cook something special
- Petrichor walks (the smell of rain on earth is literally mood-boosting)
