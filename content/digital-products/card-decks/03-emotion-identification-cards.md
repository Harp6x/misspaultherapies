# Therapy Card Deck: 40 Emotion Identification Cards
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** Etsy (₹199), Gumroad, therapy tool, parenting resource
**Format:** 40 cards — emotion name, body sensation, expression, needs
**Ages:** Kids (8+), teens, adults | Therapy sessions, classrooms, homes

---

## Card Structure
**Front:** Emotion name + colour + simple face illustration
**Back:** Body sensation + What triggers it + What you need + Healthy expression

---

## Cards

### PRIMARY EMOTIONS (Cards 1-8)

**Card 1 — JOY** 🟡
Body: Lightness in chest, smiling, energy
Triggers: Achievement, connection, surprise, beauty
Need: To savour, share, celebrate
Express: Laugh, dance, tell someone, write it down

**Card 2 — SADNESS** 🔵
Body: Heaviness, lump in throat, low energy, watery eyes
Triggers: Loss, disappointment, rejection, loneliness
Need: Comfort, connection, time, rest
Express: Cry, talk to someone, journal, be held

**Card 3 — ANGER** 🔴
Body: Heat, clenched jaw/fists, racing heart
Triggers: Injustice, boundary violation, feeling unheard
Need: To be heard, respected, to set a boundary
Express: Name it, walk, breathe, write, speak up (calmly)

**Card 4 — FEAR** 🟣
Body: Tight chest, racing heart, shallow breath, stomach knots
Triggers: Threat (real or perceived), uncertainty, change
Need: Safety, reassurance, grounding
Express: Breathe, ground, tell someone, face it gradually

**Card 5 — DISGUST** 🟢
Body: Nausea, pulling away, wrinkling nose
Triggers: Violation of values, toxic behaviour, boundaries crossed
Need: Distance, boundary, cleansing
Express: Set a boundary, walk away, honour the feeling

**Card 6 — SURPRISE** ⚪
Body: Wide eyes, gasp, freezing momentarily
Triggers: Unexpected events (positive or negative)
Need: Pause, processing time
Express: Take a breath, name what happened, choose response

**Card 7 — SHAME** 🟤
Body: Wanting to hide, heat in face, looking down
Triggers: Feeling exposed, failure, judgment, "not enough"
Need: Self-compassion, belonging, reassurance of worth
Express: Share with a safe person, counter with evidence of worth

**Card 8 — LOVE** 💗
Body: Warmth, openness, softness, calm
Triggers: Connection, trust, being seen, giving/receiving care
Need: To express, to be reciprocated
Express: Say it, show it, write it, be present

### BONUS CARDS (37-40)

**Card 37 — "I Don't Know What I'm Feeling"**
That's okay. Start with: Where do I feel it in my body? What colour would it be? Is it heavy or light? Hot or cold? This is the beginning of knowing.

**Card 38 — "I'm Feeling Everything at Once"**
Multiple emotions can coexist. You can be angry AND sad. Relieved AND guilty. Name each one. They all have a message.

**Card 39 — "I Feel Nothing"**
Numbness is also a feeling. It's often your brain protecting you. Be gentle. Start with physical sensations. Emotions will come when it's safe.

**Card 40 — "My Emotions Feel Too Big"**
Big emotions aren't dangerous. They're signals. Breathe. Ground. Ride the wave. It will pass. You are bigger than any emotion.

## Usage
- **Therapy:** Client picks the card matching their feeling → reads the back
- **Parenting:** Kids learn to name emotions beyond "fine" and "angry"
- **Self-help:** Daily check-in — "Which card am I today?"
- **Schools:** Emotional literacy tool for classrooms
