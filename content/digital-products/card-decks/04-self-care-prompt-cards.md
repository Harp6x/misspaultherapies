### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Format:** 52 cards (one per week for a full year), print-and-cut PDF

## Card Categories (13 cards each × 4 categories)

### BODY (Cards 1-13)
1. Take a 20-minute walk today — no music, no phone. Just you and the world.
2. Cook a meal from scratch. Eat it slowly.
3. Stretch for 10 minutes. Notice where you hold tension.
4. Go to bed 30 minutes earlier tonight.
5. Drink 8 glasses of water today. Track it.
6. Dance to 3 songs in your living room. No one's watching.
7. Take a bath or shower longer than usual. Use it as a reset.
8. Skip caffeine today. See what happens.
9. Do a 5-minute body scan. Where is stress living?
10. Wear something that makes you feel good. For YOU, not others.
11. Try a new fruit or vegetable this week.
12. Give yourself permission to rest without guilt today.
13. Book that health appointment you've been putting off.

### MIND (Cards 14-26)
14. Write 3 pages of stream-of-consciousness thoughts. Don't edit.
15. Learn something new for 15 minutes (YouTube, book, podcast).
16. Do a digital detox for 4 hours. How does it feel?
17. Organise one small space — a drawer, your desk, your bag.
18. Write a list of 10 things you're proud of.
19. Read for 30 minutes. A real book. With pages.
20. Solve a puzzle, play a game, or do something that challenges your brain.
21. Write a letter to your future self, 1 year from now.
22. Unfollow 5 accounts that don't make you feel good.
23. Create something — draw, write, cook, build. Anything.
24. Watch a documentary about something you know nothing about.
25. Brain dump everything on your mind. Sort into: act / schedule / let go.
26. Practice saying "I don't know" when you don't. No faking.

### HEART (Cards 27-39)
27. Text someone you haven't spoken to in a while. Say: "Thinking of you."
28. Write 3 things you're grateful for. Be specific.
29. Say "I love you" to someone. Mean it.
30. Forgive yourself for one thing you've been carrying.
31. Ask someone: "How are you, really?" And listen.
32. Set one boundary this week. Even a small one.
33. Cry if you need to. Don't hold it in.
34. Write an unsent letter to someone who hurt you. Then burn or delete it.
35. Compliment a stranger.
36. Tell someone what you appreciate about them — be specific.
37. Practice saying "no" to one thing. No explanation needed.
38. Hug someone for 20 seconds (it releases oxytocin).
39. Write yourself a love letter. Yes, really.

### SOUL (Cards 40-52)
40. Sit in silence for 5 minutes. No phone. No agenda.
41. Watch a sunrise or sunset. Just watch.
42. Spend 30 minutes in nature.
43. Light a candle and just be.
44. Write about what gives your life meaning.
45. Listen to music that moves you. Really listen.
46. Practice a random act of kindness.
47. Revisit a place from your childhood.
48. Write down your top 5 values. Are you living them?
49. Do something that scared you — even something tiny.
50. Meditate for 10 minutes. Guided or silent.
51. Write about the person you want to become.
52. Celebrate yourself today. You made it through a whole year of self-care.

## How to Use
- **Pick one card per week** (random or in order)
- **Complete the prompt** anytime during the week
- **Journal about it** afterwards: How did it feel?
- **Share on Instagram** with #MsPaulSelfCare (optional)
