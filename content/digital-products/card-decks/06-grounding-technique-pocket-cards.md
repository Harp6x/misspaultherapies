## Cards

### Breathing (Cards 1-5)

**Card 1 — Box Breathing**
IN 4 sec → HOLD 4 sec → OUT 4 sec → HOLD 4 sec
Repeat 4 times. Used by Navy SEALs.

**Card 2 — 4-7-8 Breathing**
IN 4 sec → HOLD 7 sec → OUT 8 sec
Repeat 3 times. Best for sleep + calming.

**Card 3 — Physiological Sigh**
Double inhale through nose (sniff-sniff) → Long exhale through mouth
Repeat 3 times. Fastest nervous system reset.

**Card 4 — Belly Breathing**
Hand on chest (still). Hand on belly (rises).
5 slow breaths into your belly. Chest stays quiet.

**Card 5 — Straw Breathing**
Breathe in through nose. Out through pursed lips (like a straw).
The slow exhale activates your vagus nerve. 5 breaths.

### Sensory (Cards 6-10)

**Card 6 — 5-4-3-2-1**
5 SEE · 4 HEAR · 3 TOUCH · 2 SMELL · 1 TASTE
Say them out loud for best effect.

**Card 7 — Cold Water Reset**
Splash cold water on your face or hold ice in your hands.
Triggers dive reflex → slows heart rate instantly.

**Card 8 — Texture Grounding**
Touch 5 different textures around you.
Describe each one: rough, smooth, warm, cool, soft.

**Card 9 — Scent Anchor**
Carry a scent: essential oil, hand cream, coffee bean.
Inhale slowly. Associate it with safety.

**Card 10 — Sound Anchor**
Name every sound you can hear right now.
Near sounds. Far sounds. Layered sounds. Count them.

### Body-Based (Cards 11-15)

**Card 11 — Feet on Floor**
Press feet firmly into the ground. Feel the pressure.
Say: "I am here. I am safe. I am grounded."

**Card 12 — Butterfly Hug**
Cross arms, hands on shoulders. Tap alternately. Left, right, left, right.
Slow. Rhythmic. For 60 seconds.

**Card 13 — Fist Clench & Release**
Make tight fists for 5 seconds. Release.
Repeat 5 times. Notice the difference between tension and calm.

**Card 14 — Push the Wall**
Stand, push palms against a wall. Hard. 10 seconds. Release.
Channels fight energy into something safe.

**Card 15 — Body Inventory**
Head → Shoulders → Arms → Chest → Stomach → Legs → Feet
Rate tension 0-10 in each. Breathe into the tightest spot.

### Cognitive (Cards 16-20)

**Card 16 — Name the Emotion**
"I notice I'm feeling ____."
Naming it reduces its intensity by up to 50%.

**Card 17 — Count Backwards**
From 100 by 7. (100, 93, 86, 79...)
Forces your thinking brain online, quiets the panic brain.

**Card 18 — Alphabet Game**
Pick a category (animals, cities, foods).
Name one for every letter: A=Apple, B=Banana, C=Carrot...

**Card 19 — Safe Place Visualisation**
Close eyes. Picture your safest place (real or imagined).
What do you see? Hear? Smell? Feel? Stay there for 60 seconds.

**Card 20 — "This Will Pass" Card**
Read this:
"I have survived every panic attack, every bad day, every crisis.
My track record is 100%. This feeling is temporary. I will be okay."
