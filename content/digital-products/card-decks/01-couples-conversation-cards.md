## Card Categories

### Getting to Know You Deeper (Cards 1-10)
1. What's a memory from childhood that still makes you smile?
2. What's something about yourself you've never told me?
3. What did you dream of becoming when you were 10?
4. What's the kindest thing a stranger has ever done for you?
5. What do you think is your greatest strength that I benefit from?
6. If you could relive one day of our relationship, which would it be?
7. What's something you're secretly proud of but never talk about?
8. What's a fear you had as a child that you've now overcome?
9. What's the best piece of advice you've ever been given?
10. What's something you wish you could tell your younger self?

### Emotional Intimacy (Cards 11-20)
11. When do you feel most loved by me?
12. What's the hardest thing you've been through that shaped who you are?
13. When do you feel most vulnerable around me?
14. Is there something you wish I understood better about you?
15. What does "feeling safe" in a relationship mean to you?
16. When was the last time you cried? What triggered it?
17. What's an insecurity you carry that I might not know about?
18. How do you know when you're shutting down emotionally?
19. What does forgiveness look like to you?
20. What's the most meaningful thing I've ever said to you?

### Dreams & Future (Cards 21-30)
21. Where do you see us in 5 years?
22. What's a dream you've put on hold? Can we make space for it?
23. If money weren't an issue, how would we spend our weekends?
24. What does your ideal retirement look like — together?
25. Is there a skill or hobby you'd love us to learn together?
26. What kind of old couple do you want us to be?
27. What legacy do you want to leave behind?
28. If we could live anywhere for one year, where would it be?
29. What's one goal you want to accomplish this year?
30. What does "home" mean to you?

### Conflict & Growth (Cards 31-40)
31. What's something I do that hurts you that I might not realise?
32. How can I better support you during a stressful week?
33. What's a fight we had that taught us something important?
34. Do you feel heard when we argue? What would make it better?
35. Is there something you've been avoiding telling me?
36. What's one thing I could change that would make our relationship stronger?
37. How do you prefer to repair after a fight?
38. What's a boundary you need me to respect more?
39. When you're upset, do you need space or closeness?
40. What's the most important "relationship rule" for you?

### Fun & Playful (Cards 41-50)
41. What's the funniest thing that's happened to us?
42. If our relationship were a movie, what genre would it be?
43. What's a song that reminds you of us?
44. What was your first impression of me? Be honest.
45. What's the weirdest thing you love about me?
46. If we had a couples superpower, what would it be?
47. What's a date we've been on that you'd love to repeat?
48. What's something silly that always makes you laugh about me?
49. If you could plan the perfect surprise for me, what would it be?
50. What's a TV couple that reminds you of us?

### Wild Cards (Cards 51-52)
51. Ask me ANYTHING. I'll answer honestly.
52. Tell me something you've been wanting to say but haven't found the right moment.

---

## How to Use
- **Date night:** Pull 3-5 cards, take turns answering
- **Road trip:** One card per hour
- **Weekly ritual:** One card every Sunday evening
- **Couples therapy companion:** Use during sessions
- **Rule:** No phones. No interrupting. Just listening.
