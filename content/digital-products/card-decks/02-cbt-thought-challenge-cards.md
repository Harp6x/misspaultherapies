# Therapy Card Deck: 30 CBT Thought Challenge Cards
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** Etsy (₹199-299 PDF), Gumroad, therapy homework handout
**Format:** 30 cards, print-and-cut PDF
**Use:** Client homework, self-help, therapy office tool

---

## How It Works
Each card has a **common cognitive distortion** on the front and a **challenge question + reframe** on the back. Client identifies their thought, finds the matching card, and practices the challenge.

---

## Cards

### Card 1 — All-or-Nothing Thinking
**Front:** "If it's not perfect, it's a failure."
**Back:** Challenge: "Is there a middle ground between perfect and failure?" | Reframe: "It's not perfect AND it's still good enough."

### Card 2 — Catastrophising
**Front:** "The worst possible thing is definitely going to happen."
**Back:** Challenge: "What's the MOST LIKELY outcome?" | Reframe: "I'm imagining the worst. The most likely outcome is ____."

### Card 3 — Mind Reading
**Front:** "I know they think I'm stupid/boring/annoying."
**Back:** Challenge: "Do I have actual evidence for this? Or am I guessing?" | Reframe: "I can't read minds. I'll check the facts."

### Card 4 — Fortune Telling
**Front:** "This is going to go badly. I just know it."
**Back:** Challenge: "Have my predictions been accurate before?" | Reframe: "I don't know the future. I'll deal with what actually happens."

### Card 5 — Emotional Reasoning
**Front:** "I feel it, so it must be true."
**Back:** Challenge: "Are feelings facts?" | Reframe: "I FEEL anxious. That doesn't mean danger is real."

### Card 6 — Should Statements
**Front:** "I should be further along by now."
**Back:** Challenge: "Who says? By whose timeline?" | Reframe: "I'd PREFER to be further along. I'm on my own path."

### Card 7 — Labelling
**Front:** "I'm such an idiot / failure / mess."
**Back:** Challenge: "Would I say this to a friend?" | Reframe: "I made a mistake. That doesn't define me."

### Card 8 — Personalisation
**Front:** "It's all my fault."
**Back:** Challenge: "What other factors contributed?" | Reframe: "I played a part, but I'm not 100% responsible."

### Card 9 — Mental Filter
**Front:** "I only see what went wrong."
**Back:** Challenge: "What went RIGHT today?" | Reframe: "One bad moment doesn't erase everything good."

### Card 10 — Discounting Positives
**Front:** "That compliment doesn't count. They were just being nice."
**Back:** Challenge: "Why am I dismissing evidence that I'm doing well?" | Reframe: "I'll accept this compliment at face value."

### Card 11 — Magnification
**Front:** "This mistake is ENORMOUS."
**Back:** Challenge: "Will this matter in 5 years?" | Reframe: "It feels big right now. It's actually manageable."

### Card 12 — Minimisation
**Front:** "My achievement doesn't really count."
**Back:** Challenge: "If a friend did this, would I celebrate them?" | Reframe: "This DOES count. I'll own it."

### Card 13 — Overgeneralisation
**Front:** "This ALWAYS happens to me. NOTHING ever works."
**Back:** Challenge: "Always? Never? Can I find one exception?" | Reframe: "This happened once. It's not a pattern."

### Card 14 — Blame
**Front:** "They ruined everything."
**Back:** Challenge: "What's MY part in this situation?" | Reframe: "I'll focus on what I can control."

### Card 15 — Comparison
**Front:** "Everyone else has it figured out except me."
**Back:** Challenge: "Am I comparing my insides to their outsides?" | Reframe: "I only see their highlight reel, not their struggles."

### Card 16 — Control Fallacy (External)
**Front:** "I can't help how I feel. It's all because of them."
**Back:** Challenge: "Do I have any influence over my response?" | Reframe: "I can't control them. I CAN control my next step."

### Card 17 — Control Fallacy (Internal)
**Front:** "Everything is my responsibility."
**Back:** Challenge: "Is it really ALL on me?" | Reframe: "I'm responsible for my part. Not everything."

### Card 18 — Fairness Fallacy
**Front:** "This isn't fair. It shouldn't be this way."
**Back:** Challenge: "Is life always fair? Can I focus on what IS in my control?" | Reframe: "Fair or not, I'll focus on my next move."

### Card 19 — Heaven's Reward Fallacy
**Front:** "If I sacrifice enough, I'll eventually be rewarded."
**Back:** Challenge: "Am I waiting for external validation instead of meeting my own needs?" | Reframe: "I deserve good things NOW, not just after suffering."

### Card 20 — Double Standard
**Front:** "It's okay when others mess up, but not when I do."
**Back:** Challenge: "Why do I hold myself to impossible standards?" | Reframe: "I deserve the same grace I give others."

### Cards 21-30 — Situation-Specific Challenge Cards

### Card 21 — For Anxiety
**Front:** "What if everything goes wrong?"
**Back:** "What if everything goes RIGHT? What's the evidence for each?"

### Card 22 — For Depression
**Front:** "Nothing will ever change."
**Back:** "Has anything ever changed before? Even something small?"

### Card 23 — For Social Anxiety
**Front:** "Everyone is judging me."
**Back:** "Most people are too busy thinking about themselves to judge me."

### Card 24 — For Perfectionism
**Front:** "If it's not perfect, there's no point."
**Back:** "Done and imperfect > perfect and never finished."

### Card 25 — For Imposter Syndrome
**Front:** "I don't deserve to be here."
**Back:** "I'm here because of my skills and effort. Full stop."

### Card 26 — For People-Pleasing
**Front:** "If I say no, they won't like me."
**Back:** "People who only like me when I say yes don't actually like ME."

### Card 27 — For Relationship Anxiety
**Front:** "They're going to leave me."
**Back:** "Is there evidence they're leaving? Or is my fear talking?"

### Card 28 — For Work Stress
**Front:** "I can't handle this."
**Back:** "I've handled hard things before. I'll handle this too."

### Card 29 — For Body Image
**Front:** "I hate my body."
**Back:** "My body carried me through today. That deserves gratitude, not hate."

### Card 30 — Universal
**Front:** "I am not enough."
**Back:** "I am a complete, worthy human — in progress. That IS enough."

---

## Usage Instructions
- Print on cardstock, cut along lines
- Keep on desk, nightstand, or in wallet
- When a negative thought hits, find the matching card
- Read the challenge. Write or say the reframe.
- Track: Did the thought intensity decrease?
