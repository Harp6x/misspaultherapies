# Therapy Card Deck: 40 Family Dinner Conversation Cards
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** Etsy (₹149-249), Gumroad, family therapy handout
**Format:** 40 cards, print-and-cut, family-friendly (ages 8+)
**Great for:** Indian families learning to talk beyond "How was school?"

---

## Categories

### Easy / Warm-Up (Cards 1-10)
1. What was the best part of your day today?
2. If you could have any superpower, what would it be and why?
3. What's something nice someone did for you this week?
4. If our family was a movie, what genre would it be?
5. What's a food you've never tried but want to?
6. What made you laugh today?
7. If you could visit any place in India, where would you go?
8. What's a skill you wish you were better at?
9. What's a song that makes you happy?
10. If you could have dinner with any person (alive or not), who?

### Feelings & Connection (Cards 11-20)
11. What's something about our family that makes you proud?
12. When do you feel most loved in this family?
13. Is there something on your mind that you haven't said yet?
14. What's a memory with this family you never want to forget?
15. What does "home" feel like to you?
16. When was the last time you felt really happy?
17. What do you wish grown-ups understood better about kids/teens?
18. What do you wish kids/teens understood better about grown-ups?
19. Is there something you'd like our family to do together more?
20. How can we support each other better this week?

### Deep / Meaningful (Cards 21-30)
21. What's something you've learned from a mistake?
22. What are you most grateful for right now?
23. If you could change one thing about the world, what would it be?
24. What's something brave you did recently?
25. What does being a good friend/family member mean to you?
26. What's a tradition you want our family to start?
27. What's your biggest dream right now?
28. How do you like to be comforted when you're sad?
29. What's something you're worried about? Can we help?
30. What's the most important lesson this family has taught you?

### Fun / Silly (Cards 31-40)
31. If you could swap lives with any family member for a day, who?
32. What's the funniest thing that's happened in this family?
33. If our family had a team name, what would it be?
34. What would you do if you won ₹1 crore?
35. If you could only eat one meal for the rest of your life, what?
36. What's a weird talent you have that nobody knows about?
37. If our family was stranded on an island, who does what?
38. What's the most embarrassing thing a family member has done? (Kind version!)
39. Describe each family member in one word. Why?
40. What would you want our family to be remembered for?

---

## Rules for Family Dinner Cards
1. Everyone answers. Including the adults.
2. No phones at the table.
3. No judging anyone's answer.
4. Pass is allowed — but try.
5. The youngest picks the card.
