## Module 1: Boundary Awareness (Week 1, 4 lessons)

### 1.1: What Boundaries Actually Are (10 min)
- Doors with locks, not walls
- 6 types: physical, emotional, time, digital, material, intellectual
- Boundaries vs control

### 1.2: Your Boundary Audit (10 min)
- Where are your boundaries weak?
- With whom? (Family, work, partner, friends, self)
- Worksheet: Boundary Audit Matrix

### 1.3: Why You Struggle (10 min)
- Childhood conditioning
- Cultural programming (Indian family dynamics)
- Fear of conflict, rejection, abandonment
- The fawn response connection

### 1.4: Your Boundary Rights (8 min)
- You have the right to say no
- You have the right to change your mind
- You have the right to not explain yourself
- You have the right to prioritise your peace
- Worksheet: My Boundary Rights Declaration

---

## Module 2: The Pause (Week 2, 4 lessons)

### 2.1: Stop Auto-Yes (10 min)
- The people-pleasing autopilot
- Installing a pause: "Let me think about it"
- The 24-hour rule

### 2.2: Checking In With Yourself (8 min)
- Body check: tension, stomach, jaw = your body saying no
- Feeling check: resentment = a boundary needed
- Energy check: depleted = over-giving

### 2.3: The Cost of No Boundaries (10 min)
- Burnout, resentment, loss of identity
- Enabling unhealthy dynamics
- Teaching people how to treat you

### 2.4: Whose Problem Is This? (8 min)
- Separating your responsibilities from others'
- The circle of control
- "Not my circus, not my monkeys" (with compassion)
- Worksheet: Responsibility Sorting Exercise

---

## Module 3: Saying No (Week 3, 4 lessons)

### 3.1: The Boundary Formula (10 min)
- "I [boundary]. [Consequence]."
- No apology. No explanation. No negotiation.
- Tone: firm, calm, kind

### 3.2: Scripts for Every Situation (12 min)
- 5 family scripts
- 5 work scripts
- 5 partner scripts
- 5 friend scripts
- Worksheet: My Top 10 Boundary Scripts

### 3.3: Handling Pushback (10 min)
- "But you've changed"
- "You're being selfish"
- "After everything I've done for you"
- Response: "I understand you're upset. My boundary stands."
- The broken record technique

### 3.4: Handling Guilt (10 min)
- Guilt is a habit, not a moral compass
- Sitting with discomfort (it passes)
- Journaling: "I set this boundary because ____"
- The guilt vs regret test
- Worksheet: Post-Boundary Guilt Journal

---

## Module 4: Boundary Maintenance (Week 4, 4 lessons)

### 4.1: Boundaries With Yourself (10 min)
- Screen time, sleep, self-talk, commitments
- The internal boundary: "I will not abandon myself"
- Self-boundary audit

### 4.2: When to Be Flexible (8 min)
- Rigid vs healthy vs porous boundaries
- Context matters: emergencies, compromise, growth
- Flexibility ≠ weakness

### 4.3: Boundaries & Relationships That Survive (10 min)
- Healthy relationships get BETTER with boundaries
- Relationships that end were built on you having none
- Boundary-respecting vs boundary-violating people

### 4.4: Your Boundary Maintenance Plan (10 min)
- Weekly check-in: Where did I hold / fold this week?
- Monthly audit: Which boundaries need reinforcing?
- Annual review: How have my boundaries evolved?
- Worksheet: My Boundary Maintenance Plan
- Course completion certificate
