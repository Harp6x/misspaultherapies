## Module 1: The CBT Model (3 lessons)

### 1.1: How Thoughts Create Feelings (10 min)
- The CBT triangle: thoughts → feelings → behaviours
- Same event, different interpretation, different emotion
- You are not your thoughts

### 1.2: Automatic Negative Thoughts (ANTs) (10 min)
- What ANTs are and why they appear
- The brain's negativity bias
- Catching ANTs in real-time
- Worksheet: ANT Catcher (3-day diary)

### 1.3: Your Personal CBT Map (10 min)
- Mapping YOUR cycle: situation → thought → emotion → behaviour
- Identifying your top 3 thinking patterns
- Where to intervene (thoughts are the leverage point)
- Worksheet: My CBT Map

## Module 2: Cognitive Distortions (3 lessons)

### 2.1: The Top 10 Thinking Traps (12 min)
- All-or-nothing, catastrophising, mind-reading, fortune-telling
- Emotional reasoning, should statements, labelling
- Personalisation, mental filter, discounting positives
- Real examples for each

### 2.2: Identifying YOUR Distortions (10 min)
- Pattern recognition exercise
- Your "greatest hits" — distortions you default to
- Worksheet: My Distortion Profile

### 2.3: The Distortion Challenge Process (12 min)
- Step-by-step: catch → name → challenge → replace
- Live worked examples
- Practice: 5 distortions from your diary
- Worksheet: Distortion Challenge Cards (printable)

## Module 3: The Thought Record (3 lessons)

### 3.1: The 5-Column Thought Record (12 min)
- Situation | Automatic thought | Emotion (0-100) | Evidence for/against | Balanced thought
- Live demo with real scenarios
- Worksheet: Blank 5-column records (20 copies)

### 3.2: The 7-Column Record (Advanced) (10 min)
- Adding: Cognitive distortion identified | New emotion rating
- When to use 5 vs 7 columns
- Worksheet: 7-column records

### 3.3: Making It a Habit (10 min)
- When to complete thought records
- Digital vs paper
- The "hottest thought" shortcut
- Goal: 3 records per week for 4 weeks

## Module 4: Behavioural Experiments (3 lessons)

### 4.1: Testing Your Thoughts (10 min)
- Thoughts are hypotheses, not facts
- Designing experiments to test beliefs
- Example: "If I say no, they'll hate me" → Test it

### 4.2: Behavioural Activation (12 min)
- For depression: action before motivation
- Activity scheduling
- Mastery and pleasure ratings
- Worksheet: Weekly Activity Schedule

### 4.3: Facing Avoidance (10 min)
- Avoidance maintains anxiety
- Gradual exposure principle
- Building approach behaviours
- Worksheet: My Avoidance Map + Approach Plan

## Module 5: Core Beliefs & Deeper Work (3 lessons)

### 5.1: What Are Core Beliefs? (10 min)
- Surface thoughts vs rules vs core beliefs
- "I am ____" (unlovable, incompetent, defective)
- Downward arrow technique to find them

### 5.2: Challenging Core Beliefs (12 min)
- The belief log: old belief vs new belief
- Daily evidence collection
- Historical test: Where did this belief come from?
- Worksheet: Core Belief Work

### 5.3: Building New Beliefs (10 min)
- The positive data log
- Acting "as if" the new belief is true
- Flash cards for new beliefs
- This takes time — be patient with yourself
