# Podcast: "Therapy Thoughts with Aishani Paul" — 30 Episode Outlines
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** Spotify (via Anchor), Apple Podcasts, YouTube audio, Google Podcasts
**Format:** Solo episodes (15-25 min) + guest episodes (30-40 min)

## Season 1: Mental Health Basics (Episodes 1-10)

### EP 01: "Do I Even Need Therapy?" (PILOT)
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min
**Outline:**
- Welcome + who I am + why this podcast exists
- Signs you might benefit from therapy
- Therapy myths debunked (pagal, weak, privileged)
- What actually happens in a first session
- When self-help isn't enough
- CTA: Book a discovery call / follow on Instagram

### EP 02: "Anxiety Explained — Your Brain Is Not Broken"
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min
**Outline:**
- What anxiety actually is (amygdala, nervous system, fight/flight)
- Normal worry vs anxiety disorder (the line)
- Physical symptoms people don't realise are anxiety
- 3 techniques to try today (box breathing, grounding, thought challenge)
- When to see a professional
- Resource: Free Anxiety Toolkit ebook

### EP 03: "Depression Is Not Laziness"
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min
**Outline:**
- What depression looks like (beyond sadness)
- High-functioning depression — the silent kind
- The Indian context: "Just pray" / "Think positive" / "Others have it worse"
- What actually helps: behavioural activation, connection, therapy, medication
- The bare minimum checklist for bad days

### EP 04: "Your Attachment Style Is Running Your Relationships"
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 25 min
**Outline:**
- 4 attachment styles explained simply
- How childhood shapes adult relationships
- Quiz: Which style are you? (direct to website quiz)
- Can attachment styles change? Yes — earned security
- Tips for each style
- Resource: Attachment style quiz on website

### EP 05: "Boundaries 101 — For People Who Can't Say No"
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min
**Outline:**
- What boundaries are (and aren't)
- Why boundaries are hard in Indian culture
- 6 types of boundaries
- 10 ready-to-use scripts
- Handling guilt after setting boundaries
- Resource: Free Boundaries ebook

### EP 06: "Toxic Relationships — How to Know & How to Leave"
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 25 min
**Outline:**
- Red flags of emotional abuse
- Trauma bonding explained
- Why people stay (it's not stupidity)
- The stages of leaving
- No-contact survival guide
- Helplines and resources

### EP 07: "Overthinking — A Therapist's Guide to Quieting Your Mind"
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 18 min
**Outline:**
- Why your brain overthinks (evolutionary purpose gone wrong)
- The worry loop explained
- 5 techniques: brain dump, worry postponement, 5-5-5 rule, naming, movement
- Resource: Overthinking Detox ebook

### EP 08: "Self-Worth — You Are Enough (Even When You Don't Believe It)"
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 20 min
**Outline:**
- Where low self-worth comes from
- Self-worth vs self-esteem
- The inner critic
- The evidence file technique
- Comparison culture and social media
- Resource: Self-Worth Handbook ebook

### EP 09: "Therapy in India — Breaking the Stigma" (GUEST EPISODE)
**Format:** Guest (another therapist/mental health advocate) | **Duration:** 35 min
**Outline:**
- State of mental health in India (stats)
- Cultural barriers: shame, family, cost, language
- What's changing (younger generation, social media, COVID impact)
- How to find affordable therapy
- What schools and workplaces can do

### EP 10: "Types of Therapy Explained — CBT, DBT, EMDR & More"
**Format:** Solo | **Duration:** 22 min
**Outline:**
- CBT, DBT, psychodynamic, EMDR, ACT, somatic, couples therapy
- Which one is best for what
- How to ask your therapist about their approach
- Red flags in therapy
- Resource: Types of Therapy infographic
