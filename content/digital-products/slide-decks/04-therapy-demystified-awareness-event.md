## Slide Outline

1. Title: "Therapy Demystified — Everything You Wanted to Ask But Were Afraid To"

8. What happens in Session 1 (step by step)
9. What happens in Sessions 2-10

12. Online vs in-person (pros/cons)
13. How to find a therapist in India (directories, referrals, social media)
14. Questions to ask a therapist before starting
15. Red flags in therapy (boundary violations, judgment, no progress in months)
16. How much does it cost? (₹500-5,000/session range, sliding scale, free options)
17. Free/affordable options: iCall, Vandrevala, NIMHANS, college counselling
18. "But my family will judge me" — handling stigma
19. "But I should be able to handle this alone" — the myth of self-sufficiency
20. "Therapy is for weak people" — actually, it takes enormous courage
21. Can therapy work online? (Yes — research says equally effective for most conditions)
22. Medication: When, why, and it's okay
23. Therapy + Indian culture: Making it work
24. Your first step: Book a discovery call / visit a directory / tell one person
25. Resources, helplines, QR code to free downloads, @mspaultherapies
