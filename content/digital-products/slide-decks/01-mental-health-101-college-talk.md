# Slide Deck: Mental Health 101 — College/University Talk
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Use:** Guest lecture, college workshop, awareness event
**Format:** 40 slides, Canva/Google Slides, 45-60 min talk
**Audience:** College students (18-25)

---

## Slide Outline

### Opening (Slides 1-5)
1. **Title:** Mental Health 101 — What Your Textbook Didn't Teach You
2. **About me:** Aishani Paul, M.Phil Clinical Psychology, RCI Licensed
3. **Ice-breaker poll:** "On a scale of 1-10, how's your mental health today?" (anonymous show of hands or Mentimeter)
4. **Agenda:** What we'll cover today
5. **Disclaimer:** This is education, not diagnosis. If anything resonates, seek professional support.

### Part 1: Mental Health Basics (Slides 6-12)
6. **What is mental health?** Not just "no illness" — a spectrum
7. **The mental health continuum:** Thriving ↔ Surviving ↔ Struggling ↔ In crisis
8. **Stats that matter:** 1 in 5 Indians has a mental health condition; 80% don't seek help
9. **Common conditions in college students:** Anxiety, depression, stress, ADHD, eating disorders
10. **The brain science:** Amygdala, prefrontal cortex, stress response (simplified)
11. **Myth vs fact:** 5 common myths debunked
12. **Your feelings are valid:** Normalising the full range of emotions

### Part 2: What Students Actually Face (Slides 13-20)
13. **Academic pressure:** Perfectionism, comparison, parental expectations
14. **Social media:** Comparison trap, FOMO, dopamine hijacking
15. **Relationship stress:** First relationships, breakups, peer conflict
16. **Identity & self-worth:** "Who am I?" questions, imposter syndrome
17. **Family dynamics:** Enmeshment, expectations, "log kya kahenge"
18. **Substance use:** Alcohol, weed, self-medication patterns
19. **Sleep & lifestyle:** The all-nighter myth, screen time, diet
20. **Interactive:** "Which of these resonates most?" (anonymous poll)

### Part 3: What Actually Helps (Slides 21-30)
21. **Self-awareness:** Name it to tame it — emotion labelling
22. **Breathing techniques:** Box breathing demo (do together)
23. **Grounding:** 5-4-3-2-1 (do together)
24. **Journaling:** Brain dump, gratitude, worry postponement
25. **Connection:** Talk to ONE person. Text them today.
26. **Sleep hygiene:** 5 quick wins
27. **Digital boundaries:** Screen time audit, notification detox
28. **Exercise:** 30 min, 3x/week = antidepressant-level effect
29. **Asking for help:** Not weakness — literally the bravest thing
30. **Campus resources:** Counselling centre, helplines, peer support

### Part 4: Therapy — Demystified (Slides 31-37)
31. **What therapy IS:** A conversation with a trained professional
32. **What therapy ISN'T:** Advice, judgment, lying on a couch
33. **Types of therapy:** CBT, DBT, psychodynamic, couples (simplified)
34. **What happens in a first session:** Intake, goals, no pressure
35. **How to find a therapist in India:** Directories, questions to ask, cost
36. **Affordable options:** iCall, Vandrevala, college counselling, sliding scale
37. **When to go:** You don't need to be "broken" to benefit

### Closing (Slides 38-40)
38. **Key takeaways:** 3 things to remember from today
39. **Resources:** QR code to free downloads, helplines, booking link
40. **Thank you + Q&A:** @mspaultherapies | mspaultherapies.com/book
