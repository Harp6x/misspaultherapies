# Slide Deck: Burnout in the Indian Workplace — Corporate Workshop
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Use:** Corporate wellness, HR training, team offsite, World Mental Health Day
**Format:** 35 slides, 45-60 min, interactive
**Audience:** Employees, managers, HR teams

---

## Slide Outline

### Opening (Slides 1-4)
1. Title: "Burnout Is Not a Badge of Honour" — Understanding & Preventing Workplace Burnout
2. About Aishani Paul + Ms Paul Therapies corporate services
3. Anonymous poll: "Rate your burnout level 1-10"
4. Agenda

### Part 1: What Is Burnout? (Slides 5-10)
5. WHO definition: Burnout is an occupational phenomenon (ICD-11)
6. The 3 components: Exhaustion + Cynicism + Inefficacy
7. Burnout vs stress (comparison table)
8. Indian workplace stats: 62% of Indian employees report burnout (Deloitte)
9. The cost to companies: Productivity loss, attrition, healthcare costs
10. Self-check: 10-item burnout quiz (audience does it live)

### Part 2: Why India Burns Out (Slides 11-16)
11. Always-on culture: "Reply to my WhatsApp at 11pm"
12. Hustle glorification: "Sleep is for the weak"
13. Poor boundaries: No separation between work and life
14. Manager behaviour: Micromanagement, lack of autonomy
15. Cultural factors: "Don't complain" / "Be grateful you have a job"
16. Remote work blur: Kitchen table = office = bedroom

### Part 3: Recognising Burnout (Slides 17-21)
17. Physical signs: Fatigue, headaches, insomnia, frequent illness
18. Emotional signs: Cynicism, apathy, irritability, detachment
19. Cognitive signs: Can't focus, poor decisions, memory issues
20. Behavioural signs: Procrastination, substance use, withdrawal
21. The burnout trajectory: Enthusiasm → Stagnation → Frustration → Apathy → Burnout

### Part 4: What Employees Can Do (Slides 22-27)
22. Energy audit: Where is your energy going?
23. Boundary setting: "I'll respond during work hours"
24. The non-negotiables: Sleep, movement, one fun thing/week
25. Micro-breaks: 5-min reset every 90 minutes
26. Saying no: It's a professional skill, not insubordination
27. Seeking help: EAP, therapy, manager conversation

### Part 5: What Managers/Companies Can Do (Slides 28-32)
28. Model healthy behaviour (leave on time, take leave)
29. Respect after-hours (no 10pm messages expecting replies)
30. Check in on people (not just deliverables)
31. Create psychological safety (it's okay to not be okay)
32. Invest in mental health: EAP, workshops, flexible policies

### Closing (Slides 33-35)
33. Key takeaways: 3 things to do this week
34. Resources: EAP info, helplines, Ms Paul Therapies corporate services
35. Thank you + Q&A + feedback form
