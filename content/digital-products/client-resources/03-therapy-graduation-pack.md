## Your Maintenance Plan

### Daily
- [ ] Check in with myself: "How am I today?"
- [ ] One self-care activity
- [ ] Use coping skills as needed

### Weekly
- [ ] Journal reflection (even 5 minutes)
- [ ] Social connection with someone who fills my cup
- [ ] Physical activity 3–5 times
- [ ] Review my values — am I living aligned?

### Monthly
- [ ] Monthly mood/progress check (1–10)
- [ ] Review this graduation pack
- [ ] Celebrate growth (what's different from when I started?)

### Quarterly
- [ ] Deep reflection: Am I where I want to be?
- [ ] Consider a therapy "tune-up" session if needed

## The Door Is Always Open

Therapy isn't one-and-done for everyone. Many people return for "booster sessions" during major life transitions, crises, or just because they want a tune-up.

Coming back is not failure. It's maintenance — like going to the dentist or getting a car serviced.

You're always welcome here.
