## What to Expect

### Before Your First Session
- You'll fill out a brief intake form (basic info + what brings you to therapy)
- Prepare mentally: you don't need to have a speech ready. Come as you are.
- It's normal to feel nervous. Everyone does.

### Your First Session (45–60 minutes)
- **First 5 min:** Settling in. How you're feeling about being here.
- **Next 30 min:** I'll ask about what brought you here, your background, and what you hope to get from therapy. You share at your own pace.
- **Last 10 min:** We discuss what we noticed, whether this feels like a good fit, and next steps.

### What I WILL Do
- Listen without judgement
- Ask questions to understand you better
- Create a safe, confidential space
- Work at your pace
- Be honest if I think a different approach or therapist would serve you better

### What I WON'T Do
- Judge you
- Tell you what to do
- Share anything with your family (confidentiality is absolute, with legal exceptions for safety)
- Rush the process
- Make you talk about anything you're not ready for

## Practical Information

### Session Details
- **Duration:** 50 minutes (standard) / 75 minutes (couples/family)
- **Frequency:** Weekly is recommended for the first 6–8 sessions, then we reassess
- **Mode:** Online (video) or in-person (based on availability)
- **Cancellation:** 24 hours notice required

### Confidentiality
Everything you share is private. Exceptions (required by law):
- Imminent risk of harm to self or others
- Suspected child abuse
- Court order

### Between Sessions
- Practice any exercises we discuss (this is where the real work happens)
- Journal if something comes up you want to bring to the next session
- You can email or message for clarification (not for crisis support)

## FAQs

**"How long will therapy take?"**
It depends on what you're working on. Some people benefit from 8–12 sessions. Others continue for months. We'll set goals together and review regularly.

**"Will it be awkward?"**
The first session often is — and that's okay. Most clients say it gets comfortable by session 2–3.

**"What if I cry?"**
That's completely welcome. Tissues are always available. Crying is processing, not weakness.

**"What if I don't like you?"**
Therapeutic fit matters. If it doesn't feel right, tell me — I won't be offended. I'll help you find someone who's a better match.

**"Can I bring someone?"**
Individual sessions are one-on-one. For couples/family therapy, relevant people are included.
