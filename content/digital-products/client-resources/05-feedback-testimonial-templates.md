## Template 2: Testimonial Request (After 8+ Sessions)

**Send via:** WhatsApp or email | **Timing:** After noticeable progress or at therapy graduation

### WhatsApp Version

Hi [Name],

I hope you're doing well. I wanted to reach out because working with you has been genuinely meaningful.

If you feel comfortable, I'd love to share a few words from your experience on my website/Instagram — completely anonymously, of course. It can help others who are on the fence about starting therapy.

Here are a few optional prompts:
1. What was therapy like for you?
2. What surprised you most?
3. What would you say to someone thinking about starting?

No pressure at all. Even a few lines would mean a lot. You can change/approve the final version before I use it.

Thank you for trusting me with your journey. 💛

Aishani | @mspaultherapies

### Email Version

**Subject:** Would you be open to sharing your experience?

Hi [Name],

Thank you for the trust and courage you've brought to our work together. I'm reaching out because your perspective could genuinely help someone else take that first step.

If you're comfortable, I'd love to feature a short, anonymous testimonial from you on my website or social media. You'd have full control over the final version.

**Optional prompts:**

1. What made you decide to start therapy?
2. What was your experience like?
3. How has therapy impacted your life?
4. What would you say to someone who's hesitant about starting?
5. Is there anything specific about working with me that stood out?

**Guidelines:**
- Your real name will NOT be used (first name/initial only, or fully anonymous)
- You approve the final version before it goes live
- You can withdraw at any time
- This is entirely voluntary — it won't affect our work together

Simply reply to this email with your thoughts, or I can send a short form if you prefer.

Thank you so much.

Warm regards,
Aishani Paul
@mspaultherapies | mspaultherapies.com
