## Why Between-Sessions Work Matters

Therapy is 1 hour per week. Life is the other 167 hours. The real changes happen BETWEEN sessions — when you practice what we discuss in the real world.

Think of it this way:
- **Therapy session** = learning a new recipe with a chef
- **Between sessions** = cooking it yourself at home
- **Without practice** = reading a cookbook without ever cooking

## Your Weekly Between-Sessions Checklist

### After Each Session
- [ ] Write 3 key takeaways from today's session (within 1 hour)
- [ ] Note any homework or exercises assigned
- [ ] Rate your mood (1–10) so you can track progress over time

### During the Week
- [ ] Practice assigned exercises (even if imperfectly)
- [ ] Notice patterns in your thoughts, feelings, and behaviours
- [ ] Write down anything that comes up you want to discuss next session
- [ ] Use your coping tools when distress arises

### Before Next Session
- [ ] Review your notes from last session
- [ ] Note what went well and what was difficult this week
- [ ] Identify what you want to focus on next

## Your Coping Toolkit (Quick Reference)

### When Anxious
1. Extended exhale breathing (4 in, 8 out × 3)
2. 5-4-3-2-1 grounding (see, touch, hear, smell, taste)
3. Ask: "Is this a fact or a worry?"

### When Sad
1. Opposite action — do something even though you don't feel like it
2. Call or text one person
3. Move your body (even 5 minutes)

### When Angry
1. STOP — physically pause
2. Take a 20-minute timeout
3. Assertive expression: "I feel ___ when ___ because ___. I need ___."

### When Overwhelmed
1. Brain dump — write everything in your head onto paper
2. Pick ONE thing to do (the smallest possible step)
3. Self-compassion break: hand on heart, kind words

### When Numb / Disconnected
1. Cold water on face/wrists
2. Strong sensory input (ice cube, strong scent)
3. Grounding: feel your feet, name your surroundings

### Progress Markers (Not Just "Feeling Better")
- [ ] I notice my patterns earlier
- [ ] I use coping skills more often
- [ ] I'm setting boundaries I couldn't before
- [ ] I recover from setbacks faster
- [ ] I understand my triggers better
- [ ] I'm kinder to myself than when I started
- [ ] I'm sleeping/eating/functioning better
- [ ] Relationships are improving

## When to Reach Out Between Sessions

**Normal (wait for next session):** Processing emotions, mild discomfort, wanting to discuss something

**Reach out via message:** Significant increase in symptoms, difficulty using coping skills, medication concerns

**Call crisis line immediately:** Thoughts of self-harm, feeling unsafe, emergency situation

- **Vandrevala Foundation:** 1860-2662-345
