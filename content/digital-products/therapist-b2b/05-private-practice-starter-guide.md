## Chapter 1: Before You Start

### Qualifications Check
- M.Phil in Clinical Psychology (RCI recognised) — required for "clinical psychologist"
- MA/MSc Psychology — can practice as "counsellor" or "psychologist"
- RCI registration: Apply at rehabcouncil.nic.in
- Professional liability insurance: Consider it

### Legal Basics
- Register as sole proprietor or LLP
- GST registration (if turnover > ₹20 lakh)
- Maintain client records securely (HIPAA doesn't apply in India, but follow ethical standards)
- Informed consent forms (mandatory)
- Confidentiality agreements

## Chapter 2: Setting Up

### Online vs In-Person vs Hybrid
| | Online | In-Person | Hybrid |
|--|--------|-----------|--------|
| Startup cost | ₹5,000-15,000 | ₹50,000-2,00,000 | ₹30,000-1,00,000 |
| Reach | National/global | Local | Both |
| Overhead | Low | High | Medium |
| Client preference | Growing rapidly | Still preferred by some | Best of both |

### Essential Tools
- **Video:** Zoom (HIPAA version) or Google Meet
- **Scheduling:** Calendly, Topmate, or Cal.com
- **Payments:** Razorpay, UPI, bank transfer
- **Notes:** Notion, Google Docs (encrypted), or practice management software
- **Website:** Simple WordPress/Squarespace or Instagram bio link
- **Email:** Professional email (yourname@yourpractice.com)

### Setting Your Fee
- Market rate (India, 2024): ₹1,000-5,000 per session
- Factors: Experience, location, specialisation, online vs offline
- Sliding scale: Offer 2-3 reduced-fee slots
- Don't undervalue yourself: Your training took years

## Chapter 3: Getting Clients

### Month 1-3 Strategy
1. Instagram presence: Post 3-5x/week (educational + relatable)
2. List on Practo, Amaha, MindPeers, YourDOST
3. Google My Business listing
4. Tell everyone: Friends, family, former supervisors
5. Offer 1-2 free discovery calls per week
6. Ask for referrals (doctors, schools, HR departments)

### Month 3-6 Strategy
1. Start a newsletter (Kit/Beehiiv)
2. Create 2-3 free downloads (lead magnets)
3. Guest speak at colleges, corporates, events
4. Collaborate with other therapists
5. Start a podcast or YouTube channel

### Month 6-12 Strategy
1. Launch a group program or workshop
2. Create a paid digital product (course, ebook)
3. Build referral partnerships
4. Consider hiring an assistant
5. Raise your rates (you've earned it)

## Chapter 4: The Business Side

### Pricing Structure
- Individual therapy: ₹____/session
- Couples therapy: ₹____/session (usually 1.5x individual)
- Family therapy: ₹____/session
- Corporate workshops: ₹____/hour or ₹____/day
- Digital products: ₹____

### Financial Basics
- Separate business bank account
- Track income and expenses (Excel or accounting software)
- Quarterly tax payments (advance tax if applicable)
- Save receipts for deductions
- Consider hiring a CA once revenue is consistent

### Cancellation Policy (sample)
"Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice will be charged at 50%. No-shows will be charged in full. Emergencies are handled on a case-by-case basis."

## Chapter 5: Avoiding Burnout

- Maximum 5-6 clients per day
- Take a lunch break (real one)
- Weekly peer supervision
- Monthly personal therapy (yes, therapists need therapists)
- One full day off per week (non-negotiable)
- 2 weeks off per year (minimum)
- Track your energy, not just your income

## Chapter 6: Ethics & Self-Care

- Never treat friends or family
- Maintain clear boundaries (no WhatsApp friendships with clients)
- Seek supervision when stuck
- Document everything
- Know when to refer out
- Your mental health is your most important tool — protect it
