## WEEK 1: Anxiety & Overthinking

### Day 1 — HOOK REEL
**Hook:** "If you Google your symptoms at 3am, this is for you."
**Script:** Anxiety isn't just worrying. It's the racing heart. The tight chest. The 47 tabs open in your brain. The 'what ifs' that won't stop. Here's what your therapist wants you to know — anxiety is treatable. You don't have to live like this.
**CTA:** Save this. Share with someone who needs it.
**Audio:** Trending soft/emotional

### Day 2 — TIPS REEL
**Hook:** "3 things that actually help anxiety (from a psychologist)"
**Script:** 1. Box breathing — 4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold. 2. The 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique. 3. Naming the emotion: "I notice I'm anxious" — reduces intensity by 50%. Try all 3 this week.
**CTA:** Which one are you trying? Comment below.

### Day 3 — RELATABLE REEL
**Hook:** "Things people with anxiety are tired of hearing"
**Script:** "Just relax." "Stop overthinking." "It's all in your head." "Other people have it worse." "Just breathe." Yes, I know. That's literally what my therapist taught me, but said with compassion and a plan.
**CTA:** Tag someone who GETS it.

### Day 4 — EDUCATION REEL
**Hook:** "Anxiety vs normal worry — know the difference"
**Script:** Normal worry: tied to a situation, goes away. Anxiety disorder: free-floating, constant, physical symptoms, interferes with daily life, lasts 6+ months. If it's the second one — it's not weakness. It's clinical. And it's treatable.
**CTA:** Free guide in bio. Link in bio.

### Day 5 — STORY REEL (personal/client)
**Hook:** "She told me she hadn't slept properly in 2 years."
**Script:** Her first session, she said: "I thought this was just who I am — an anxious person." 8 sessions later, she sleeps through the night. She goes to parties without spiraling. She said: "I didn't know life could feel this quiet." Anxiety isn't your personality. It's a pattern. And patterns can change.
**CTA:** If this sounds like you — link in bio.

### Day 6 — MYTH-BUST REEL
**Hook:** "Anxiety is NOT just being nervous."
**Script:** Nervousness is temporary. Anxiety disorder is: chronic, physical, debilitating. It includes: panic attacks, avoidance, insomnia, GI issues, muscle tension, concentration problems. It's not "being dramatic." It's your nervous system stuck in overdrive.
**CTA:** Share this with someone who needs to hear it.

### Day 7 — TRENDING AUDIO REEL
**Hook:** (Use trending audio) + text overlay: "My brain at 2am vs what I should actually do"
**Script:** Brain: What if everything goes wrong? / Me: Name 5 things you see. / Brain: But what if— / Me: Breathe. 4-7-8. / Brain: But— / Me: This thought is not a fact. Goodnight.
**CTA:** Save for your next 2am spiral.

## WEEK 2: Boundaries & People-Pleasing

### Day 8 — HOOK
"You're not 'too nice.' You're a people-pleaser. Here's the difference."
Script: Nice = kind by choice. People-pleasing = kind out of fear. If saying no makes you feel guilty, if you lose yourself in relationships, if you say "I'm fine" when you're not — that's not kindness. That's survival. And you can unlearn it.

### Day 9 — TIPS
"5 boundary scripts you can use TODAY"
Script: 1. "I love you AND I can't do that." 2. "Let me think about it." 3. "That doesn't work for me." 4. "I'm not available for that conversation." 5. "No." (That's it. No explanation needed.)

### Day 10 — RELATABLE
"Indian family boundaries be like:"
Script: "Beta, when are you getting married?" Me: "That's personal." Family: *shocked Pikachu* Setting boundaries in Indian families isn't disrespect. It's self-respect with love.

### Day 11 — EDUCATION
"Why boundaries feel so hard (the science)"
Script: Your brain was wired to equate 'no' with danger — especially if saying no as a child meant punishment, withdrawal, or guilt. Your nervous system learned: compliance = safety. Rewiring this takes practice, self-compassion, and sometimes therapy.

### Day 12 — STORY
"She cried the first time she said no to her mother."
Script: Not because she was sad. Because she was TERRIFIED. Her whole life, saying no meant being the "bad daughter." In therapy, she learned: setting a boundary isn't rejection. It's an act of love — for yourself AND the relationship.

### Day 13 — MYTH-BUST
"Setting boundaries is NOT selfish."
Script: Selfish = taking from others for your gain. Boundaries = protecting your energy so you can show up fully. The people who call your boundaries "selfish" are usually the ones who benefited from you having none.

### Day 14 — TRENDING
Text overlay on trending audio: "Things I stopped doing after therapy" — saying yes to everything / explaining my 'no' / taking responsibility for other people's feelings / apologising for existing

## WEEK 3: Self-Worth & Inner Critic

### Day 15 — HOOK
"Your inner critic is not telling you the truth."
Script: That voice that says "you're not enough"? It's not wisdom. It's a wound. Somewhere you learned your worth was conditional. It's not. You are worthy because you exist. Full stop.

### Day 16 — TIPS
"3 ways to quiet your inner critic"
Script: 1. Name it. ("Oh, The Judge is talking again.") 2. Challenge it. ("Would I say this to a friend?") 3. Replace it. ("I'm doing my best, and that's enough.")

### Day 17 — RELATABLE
"Me vs my inner critic" (split screen or text)
Script: Me: I did a good job today. / Critic: But you could've done better. / Me: People like me. / Critic: They're just being nice. / Me: I'm proud of myself. / Critic: Who do you think you are? / Therapist: That's not your voice. That's your wound talking.

### Day 18 — EDUCATION
"Where low self-worth comes from"
Script: Critical parents. Bullying. Toxic relationships. Comparison culture. Cultural messaging: "Don't be too proud." Achievement addiction. None of this was your fault. All of it can change.

### Day 19 — STORY
"He said he'd never received a compliment he believed."
Script: Every compliment bounced off him. "They're just being nice." In therapy, we built an Evidence File — one piece of proof per day that he was capable, kind, worthy. After 3 months, he said: "I think I might actually be okay." You might be okay too.

### Day 20 — MYTH-BUST
"Confidence is not the absence of self-doubt."
Script: Confident people feel doubt too. The difference? They act anyway. Confidence isn't "I'm the best." It's "I'm scared AND I'm doing it." That's the version worth building.

### Day 21 — TRENDING
Text: "Things my therapist said that broke my brain" — "You don't have to earn rest." / "Guilt is not a moral compass." / "You're allowed to outgrow people." / "Your worth existed before anyone told you otherwise."
