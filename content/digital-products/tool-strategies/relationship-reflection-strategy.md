# Relationship Reflection Pack — Product Strategy

## Product Concept
A free guided relationship exploration tool at `/tools/relationship-reflect` with two modes:
- **Solo mode:** 3 sections (Attachment Style, Unmet Needs, Boundaries & Resentment) — for individual reflection on relationship patterns
- **Partner mode:** 3 sections (Emotional Check-In, Repair & Understanding, Shared Vision) — structured conversation prompts for couples to do together

## Target Emotional Pain Point
"My relationships feel strained and I don't know how to fix them." — People struggling with communication, attachment insecurity, unmet needs, recurring conflict, emotional distance, or post-conflict repair. Also targets couples looking for structured conversations.

## User Journey
1. **Discovery** → SEO ("relationship reflection exercises"), social ("Do this with your partner tonight"), /tools hub
2. **Mode select** → Solo or Partner (clear descriptions of each)
3. **Section flow** → 3 sections per mode, each with intro + 3 prompts + writing area
4. **Complete** → Section reflection → next section or final result
5. **Convert** → "Get the Relationship Reflection Workbook" email CTA
6. **Upsell** → Couples therapy / individual therapy discovery call

## Content Structure
### Solo Mode (3 sections × 3 prompts = 9 prompts)
1. **Attachment Style** — Instinctive responses, messaging anxiety, safety/running patterns
2. **Unmet Needs** — Repeated needs, self-meeting, hardest asks
3. **Boundaries & Resentment** — Resentment audit, body's "no", one-boundary challenge

### Partner Mode (3 sections × 3 prompts = 9 prompts, each with Person A + B version)
1. **Emotional Check-In** — How we feel about us, recent appreciation, unsaid things
2. **Repair & Understanding** — Underneath the conflict, unspoken needs, what I want you to know
3. **Shared Vision** — Ideal ordinary day, one thing to start, one-year statement

## UI/UX Structure
- Mode selection: two large cards (Solo 🪞 / Partner 💬)
- Section progress: 3-segment bar
- Per section: intro text → 3 prompt cards with textarea → complete button
- Partner mode: dual labels (Person A / Person B) per prompt
- Section complete: reflection card → next section button
- Final: email CTA + therapy CTA (tailored for solo vs partner)

## Email Funnel
- **Tag:** guided-reflection (shared tag)
- **Downloadable:** Relationship Reflection Workbook PDF (all prompts + 20 bonus questions)
- **Sequence:** "Understanding your attachment style" → "How to communicate needs" → "When to consider couples therapy" → discovery call

## Monetization
- Free: Full tool both modes
- Email-gated: Printable Workbook PDF
- Paid: "Communication Skills Course for Couples" (₹799)
- Workshop: "Couples Communication Workshop" (₹1,500 per couple)
- Therapy: Couples therapy sessions
