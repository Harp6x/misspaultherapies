# Guided Reflection Generator — Product Strategy

## Product Concept
A free, theme-based guided writing tool at `/tools/reflect`. Users select an emotional theme (anxiety, self-worth, relationships, grief, growth, stress), receive 3 progressive therapeutic prompts, write freely, and get a personalised summary reflection. Follow-up insights appear dynamically after 30+ characters. Everything stays client-side.

## Target Emotional Pain Point
"I want to understand myself better but don't know where to start." — People who know they need to process emotions but feel overwhelmed by a blank page. Also targets therapy-curious users who want a taste of therapeutic self-exploration.

## User Journey
1. **Discovery** → Social post ("Pick a theme. Write 3 honest answers. See what comes up."), SEO, or /tools hub
2. **Theme selection** → 6 emotionally labelled cards with emoji and descriptions
3. **Writing** → 3 progressive prompts with textarea, dynamic follow-up reflections appear after writing
4. **Result** → Summary reflection + all 3 responses displayed + share/restart/email CTA
5. **Convert** → "Get weekly reflection prompts" email capture → Kit tag: guided-reflection
6. **Upsell** → "Want to go deeper with a therapist?" → discovery call

## UI/UX Structure
- Theme selection: 3×2 grid of cards with emoji, title, description
- Writing screen: one prompt at a time, progress bar segments, textarea with character counter
- Dynamic follow-up: appears after 30+ characters as gentle insight
- Result: emoji header, headline, body, 3 collapsible response reviews, actions
- Navigation: back to theme, previous prompt, continue/submit

## Content Structure
- 6 themes × 3 prompts each = 18 unique therapeutic prompts
- Each prompt has a stage-specific follow-up that validates and builds on the writing
- Each theme has a unique summary reflection that ties all 3 prompts together

## Email Funnel
- **Tag:** guided-reflection
- **Sequence:** Weekly therapeutic writing prompt → psychoeducation → therapy positioning

## SEO Opportunities

- **Long-tail:** "anxiety reflection prompts free", "self-worth writing exercises", "grief journaling prompts"
