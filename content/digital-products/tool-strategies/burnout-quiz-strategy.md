# Burnout Assessment Quiz — Product Strategy

## Product Concept
A free, evidence-based 12-question burnout assessment at `/tools/burnout-quiz`. Measures three clinical dimensions — emotional exhaustion, cynicism/detachment, and reduced professional efficacy — inspired by the Maslach Burnout Inventory framework. Returns a tiered result (Low/Moderate/High/Critical) with personalized recovery suggestions.

## Target Emotional Pain Point
"Am I burned out or just tired?" — Working professionals and young adults who feel perpetually exhausted but can't tell if it's normal stress or clinical burnout. High-functioning anxiety sufferers who keep pushing through.

## User Journey
1. **Discovery** → Instagram reel ("3 signs you're not tired, you're burned out") / Google search ("am I burned out quiz") / LinkedIn share
2. **Landing** → `/tools/burnout-quiz` — compelling headline, privacy assurance, time estimate
3. **Engagement** → 12 questions, one at a time, auto-advance on selection (2 min)
4. **Result** → Tier badge, headline, explanation, 3 subscale scores with progress bars, next steps
5. **Convert** → "Get your Burnout Recovery Roadmap (free PDF)" → email capture → Kit tag: burnout-quiz
6. **Escalate** → High/Critical results show therapy CTA with gentle urgency
7. **Share** → Result tier is shareable (not raw scores — privacy-preserving)

## UI/UX Structure
- Single question per screen with subscale label (Emotional Exhaustion / Cynicism & Detachment / Professional Efficacy)
- 5 answer options: Never → Always (Likert scale, styled as cards)
- Auto-advance on selection with 300ms delay
- Progress bar + question dots for navigation
- Result screen: tier badge (color-coded), subscale breakdown (3 cards with scores, labels, progress bars), bullet-point suggestions, share/retake, email capture, therapy CTA for high/critical
- Clinical disclaimer at bottom

## Engagement Hooks
- **Curiosity gap:** "Am I burned out or just tired?" — instantly relatable question
- **Quantification:** People love seeing their score (gamification without trivialising)
- **Subscale breakdown:** More nuanced than a single score — feels clinically credible
- **Social proof:** "13,241 professionals have taken this assessment" (future)
- **Retake mechanic:** Monthly progress tracking drives repeat visits

## Psychological Retention Mechanics
- **Zeigarnik effect:** One question at a time with progress dots — strong completion drive
- **Self-serving bias:** People share results that match their self-narrative (validates their exhaustion)
- **Loss aversion:** "Get your recovery roadmap before you close this tab" (gated download)
- **Authority bias:** Clinical framework (Maslach-inspired) builds trust
- **Commitment consistency:** Taking the quiz commits them to the narrative that they need help → increases therapy booking likelihood
- **Social comparison:** Knowing their tier relative to others normalises seeking help
