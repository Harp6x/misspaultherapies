# 7-Day Self-Awareness Journey — Product Strategy

## Product Concept
A structured 7-day guided self-exploration tool at `/tools/self-awareness`. Each day focuses on a different dimension of self-knowledge: Identity, Values, Patterns, Triggers, Strengths, Shadow, and Integration. Days unlock sequentially. Progress saved locally. 3 writing prompts per day with a closing reflection.

## Target Emotional Pain Point
"I feel lost or stuck — like I don't really know myself." — People in transitions, quarter-life or mid-life reassessment, post-breakup identity work, or therapy-adjacent self-exploration.

## User Journey
1. **Discovery** → SEO ("self-awareness exercises"), social carousel ("7 questions that changed how I see myself"), /tools hub
2. **Start** → Day 1 unlocked, Days 2–7 locked with visual progression
3. **Daily engage** → 10–15 min per day: read intro → write 3 prompts → see reflection → complete
4. **Progress** → Progress bar, completed checkmarks, sequential unlock mechanic
5. **Finish** → Day 7 integration → "Get the 30-day deep dive" email CTA
6. **Convert** → Email sequence → paid product / discovery call

## 7-Day Structure
| Day | Theme | Title |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 1 | Identity | Who Am I — Really? |
| 2 | Values | What Actually Matters to Me? |
| 3 | Patterns | The Loops I Keep Running |
| 4 | Triggers | What Sets Me Off — and Why |
| 5 | Strengths | What I'm Good At (That I Don't Give Myself Credit For) |
| 6 | Shadow | The Parts I Don't Want to Look At |
| 7 | Integration | Bringing It All Together |

## Engagement Mechanics
- **Sequential unlock:** Creates commitment + anticipation (can't skip ahead)
- **Daily cadence:** One day = one theme = one session = habit formation
- **Progress persistence:** localStorage saves completed days
- **Completion reward:** Day 7 reflection + 30-day deep dive upsell

## Email Funnel
- **Tag:** guided-reflection (shared with reflection tool)
- **Trigger:** Complete Day 7 → email capture
- **Sequence:** "What your 7-day journey revealed" → daily integration tips → "30-Day Deep Dive" paid product → discovery call

## Monetization
- Free: Full 7-day journey
- Email-gated: 30-Day Self-Awareness Deep Dive (expanded prompts)
- Paid: "Know Yourself" Printable Workbook (₹199)
