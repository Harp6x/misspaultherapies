# Guided Script: Self-Compassion Meditation (7 Minutes)
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** YouTube, Insight Timer, Gumroad (₹0)
**Format:** Audio script (7 min)

---

## Script

Find a quiet place. Sit comfortably. Close your eyes.

Place one hand on your heart. Feel the warmth of your own hand. Feel your heartbeat underneath.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Think of a difficulty you're going through right now. It doesn't have to be the biggest thing — just something that's been weighing on you.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Now say to yourself, silently:

**"This is a moment of suffering."**

*[Pause]*

You're not exaggerating. You're not being dramatic. You're simply acknowledging that this is hard.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Now say:

**"Suffering is part of being human."**

*[Pause]*

Right now, millions of people around the world are feeling something similar. You are not alone in this. You are part of a shared human experience.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Now say:

**"May I be kind to myself."**

*[Pause]*

Feel your hand on your heart. Imagine speaking to yourself the way you would speak to someone you love. Gently. Without judgment.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**"May I give myself the compassion I need."**

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

If it feels right, add:

**"May I accept myself as I am."**
**"May I be patient with myself."**
**"May I forgive myself for what I couldn't control."**

*[Pause 8 seconds]*

Now, think of yourself as a child. See that small version of you — maybe uncertain, maybe afraid, maybe trying so hard to be good enough.

What would you say to that child?

*[Pause 8 seconds]*

Whatever you'd say to them — say it to yourself now. Because you still deserve to hear it.

*[Pause 10 seconds]*

Take a deep breath in... and out.

You practiced something powerful today. You chose kindness toward yourself. That takes courage.

Carry this with you. You can return to this practice anytime — hand on heart, three phrases.

When you're ready, open your eyes.

*[End]*
