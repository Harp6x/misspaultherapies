## Script (Read slowly. Pause where indicated.)

Find a comfortable position. You can sit or lie down. Close your eyes if that feels safe. If not, soften your gaze.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Take a deep breath in through your nose... 2, 3, 4...
Hold... 2, 3, 4...
And slowly out through your mouth... 2, 3, 4, 5, 6...

Again. In... 2, 3, 4...
Hold... 2, 3, 4...
Out... 2, 3, 4, 5, 6...

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Now, bring your attention to your body. Start at the top of your head. Notice any tension. You don't need to fix it — just notice it.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Move your attention down to your forehead... your jaw. Is it clenched? Let it soften. Let your tongue drop from the roof of your mouth.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Now your shoulders. Are they up by your ears? Drop them. Let them fall. Let them be heavy.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Your chest. Your stomach. Notice what's happening there. Anxiety often lives here. Breathe into that space. You don't need to push it away. Just make room.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Your hands. Unclench them. Let your fingers be soft.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Now I want you to say silently to yourself:

"I am here."

*[Pause]*

"I am safe."

*[Pause]*

"This feeling is temporary."

*[Pause]*

"I am not my anxiety."

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Take one more deep breath. In... 2, 3, 4...
And out... 2, 3, 4, 5, 6...

When you're ready, slowly open your eyes. Wiggle your fingers. Feel your feet on the ground.

You just did something brave. You stopped. You breathed. You chose yourself.

Carry that with you today.

*[End]*
