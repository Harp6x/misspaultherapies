# Guided Script: Body Scan for Sleep (10 Minutes)
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** YouTube, Insight Timer, Gumroad (₹0), podcast
**Format:** Audio script (10 min)

---

## Script

Welcome. This body scan is designed to help you release the day and drift into sleep.

Lie on your back. Arms by your side, palms up. Close your eyes. Let the bed hold your full weight. You don't need to hold anything up right now.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Take three slow breaths. Each exhale, let yourself sink a little deeper into the mattress.

In... and out.
In... and out.
In... and out.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

We'll move through your body, from head to toes. All you need to do is notice, breathe, and release.

**Top of your head.** Imagine warmth spreading across your scalp. Like warm water. Softening.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your forehead.** Smooth it out. Release the day's thinking. You don't need to solve anything right now.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your eyes.** They've worked hard today. Let them rest behind closed lids. Heavy. Still.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your jaw.** Let it drop open slightly. Release the words you held back today. You're done talking.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your neck and throat.** Soften. Let your head be heavy on the pillow.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your shoulders.** Drop them away from your ears. Let them melt into the bed. Every worry you're carrying — set it down. You can pick it up tomorrow if you still want to.

*[Pause 8 seconds]*

**Your arms.** Heavy. Warm. From shoulders to elbows to wrists to fingertips. Let them be completely still.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your chest.** Feel it rise and fall. Let your breathing be natural. Easy. You don't need to breathe deeply — just let your body breathe you.

*[Pause 8 seconds]*

**Your stomach.** Soften it. You don't need to hold it in. Let it be round, warm, relaxed.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your lower back.** Release any ache. Let the bed support you fully.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your hips.** Heavy. Grounded. Relaxed.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your thighs.** Warm and heavy. Like they're sinking into sand.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your knees. Your calves.** Softening.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

**Your ankles. Your feet.** All the way to your toes. Warm. Still. Released.

*[Pause 8 seconds]*

Your whole body is now soft, warm, and held.

There is nothing to do. Nowhere to go. Nothing to fix.

You are safe. You are here. Sleep is coming.

*[Pause 10 seconds]*

If thoughts come, let them pass like clouds. You don't need to follow them. Just notice... and return to the warmth of your body.

*[Pause 10 seconds]*

Rest now.

*[Silence or very soft ambient sound for 60 seconds]*

*[End]*

## Recording Notes
- **Duration:** ~10 minutes
- **Tone:** Whisper-soft, slow, monotone (designed to bore the brain into sleep)
- **Background:** Very quiet rain or ambient drone (optional)
