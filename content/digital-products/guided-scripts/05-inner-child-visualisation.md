# Guided Script: Inner Child Visualisation (8 Minutes)
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** YouTube, Insight Timer, Gumroad (₹0), podcast
**Format:** Audio script (8 min)

---

## Script

Find a comfortable, safe space. Close your eyes. Take three deep breaths.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

I want you to imagine yourself walking down a long, quiet hallway. The walls are warm. The light is golden. You feel safe here.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

At the end of the hallway, there's a door. Behind that door is a room you know — from your childhood. Maybe your bedroom. Maybe a place you used to hide. Maybe a garden or a grandparent's house.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Open the door.

*[Pause]*

Sitting there is a child. It's you. The younger version of you. Maybe 5 years old. Maybe 8. Maybe 12. However they appear is right.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Look at them. Notice what they're wearing. How they're sitting. The expression on their face.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

This child has been waiting for you.

Walk toward them slowly. Get down to their level. Look them in the eyes.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

What do they need to hear?

Maybe it's: "You're safe now."
Maybe: "None of that was your fault."
Maybe: "I'm so proud of you for surviving."
Maybe: "I'm here. I'm not leaving."

Say it to them now. Silently. From your heart.

*[Pause 10 seconds]*

Watch their face change. Watch the tension leave their small shoulders. They believe you.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Now, ask them: "What do you need?"

*[Pause 8 seconds]*

Listen. Whatever they say — honour it.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

If it feels right, hold them. Let that small version of you be held by the strong, grown version of you. The adult who made it. Who survived. Who is here, doing this brave work.

*[Pause 10 seconds]*

Tell them: "I will take care of us now."

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Slowly, gently, bring your awareness back to the present. Feel your body in the chair or the bed. Feel your breath. Feel your hands.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

You can visit this child anytime. They live inside you. And now they know — they're not alone anymore.

Take a deep breath in... and out.

When you're ready, open your eyes.

*[End]*

---

## Recording Notes
- **Duration:** ~8 minutes
- **Tone:** Deeply compassionate, slow, emotionally safe
- **Warning:** This can bring up strong emotions. Include a disclaimer:
  "If childhood memories feel overwhelming, please practice this with a therapist present."
- **Best for:** Deep content — YouTube, podcast, therapy companion
