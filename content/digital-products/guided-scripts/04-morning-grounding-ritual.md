# Guided Script: 3-Minute Morning Grounding Ritual
### Ms Paul Therapies | @mspaultherapies

**Platform:** Instagram Reel, YouTube Short, TikTok, podcast intro
**Format:** Audio script (3 min) — perfect for short-form content
**Best for:** Daily ritual people come back to, algorithm-friendly

---

## Script

Good morning.

Before the world gets loud, take this moment.

Sit at the edge of your bed. Feet flat on the floor. Hands on your thighs. Eyes closed.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Take one deep breath. The first conscious breath of your day.

In through your nose... 2, 3, 4...
Out through your mouth... 2, 3, 4, 5...

*[Pause]*

Now check in.

**Body:** Where do I feel tension? ____. Breathe into that spot.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

**Mind:** What's my first thought today? ____. Notice it. Don't chase it.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

**Heart:** What do I need today? ____. Honour that.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Now set one intention. Not a to-do list. An intention.

How do I want to FEEL today?

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Say it to yourself: "Today, I choose to feel ____."

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

Take one more breath. In... and out.

Open your eyes. You've already done something for yourself today. The rest is a bonus.

Go gently.

*[End]*

---

## Recording Notes
- **Duration:** ~3 minutes
- **Tone:** Warm, steady, morning-soft
- **Perfect for:** Instagram Reel with subtitles, YouTube Shorts, daily podcast drop
- **Engagement hook:** "Save this. Do it every morning for 7 days."
