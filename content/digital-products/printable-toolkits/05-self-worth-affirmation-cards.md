## Card Design Guide (for Canva)
- Size per card: 3.5" × 2" (business card)
- Layout: 5 cards per A4 page, cut marks
- Colours: Rotate between cream, sage green, soft pink, lavender, warm beige
- Font: Serif for affirmation, sans-serif for attribution
- Each card has: Affirmation + "@mspaultherapies" small footer

## 30 Affirmations

### Page 1 (Cards 1-5) — Core Worth
1. I am worthy of love and belonging — exactly as I am.
2. My value does not decrease based on someone's inability to see my worth.
3. I am allowed to take up space.
4. I don't need to prove my worth. It already exists.
5. I am enough. I have always been enough.

### Page 2 (Cards 6-10) — Boundaries & Self-Respect
6. Saying no does not make me selfish. It makes me self-aware.
7. I choose relationships that honour my peace.
8. My boundaries are an act of love — for myself and others.
9. I will not shrink to make someone else comfortable.
10. I deserve the same kindness I give to others.

### Page 3 (Cards 11-15) — Healing & Growth
11. Healing is not linear. I honour my own timeline.
12. I am not my mistakes. I am what I do next.
13. I give myself permission to outgrow who I used to be.
14. My past does not define my future.
15. Every day I am becoming more of who I truly am.

### Page 4 (Cards 16-20) — Courage & Resilience
16. I have survived 100% of my worst days.
17. I am stronger than the stories fear tells me.
18. Courage is not the absence of fear. It's action in spite of it.
19. I trust my ability to navigate hard things.
20. I am allowed to start over as many times as I need.

### Page 5 (Cards 21-25) — Self-Compassion
21. I forgive myself for not knowing what I didn't know before.
22. I will speak to myself the way I speak to someone I love.
23. It's okay to rest. I don't have to earn it.
24. I release the need to be perfect.
25. My feelings are valid, even when they're uncomfortable.

### Page 6 (Cards 26-30) — Joy & Purpose
26. I am allowed to want more for my life.
27. I choose joy, even in small moments.
28. I am worthy of my own dreams.
29. My presence matters. My voice matters.
30. Today, I choose to believe in myself.

## How to Use
- **Print on thick paper** (cardstock if possible)
- **Cut along dotted lines**
- **Pick one card each morning** (random or intentional)
- **Place it where you'll see it** — mirror, desk, wallet, phone case
- **Say it out loud** at least once
- **Keep used cards** in a jar — read the full jar when you need a boost
