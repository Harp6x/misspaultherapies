### Am I Burned Out? (Quick Check)
☐ Exhausted even after sleeping
☐ Cynical about work/life
☐ Feeling ineffective — "what's the point?"
☐ Dreading Mondays (and Tuesdays... and every day)
☐ Emotionally numb or irritable
☐ Physical symptoms: headaches, stomach issues, muscle tension
☐ Can't remember the last time I felt excited about something
☐ Using caffeine/alcohol/food to cope

**4+ checks = burnout likely. Keep reading.**

## Page 3: The 5-Day Emergency Burnout Reset

### Day 1: CANCEL
Cancel one non-essential commitment this week. No guilt.

### Day 2: REST
Take a real break. Not "productive rest." Actual doing-nothing rest.
Ideas: Nap. Stare at the ceiling. Watch comfort TV. Sit in the sun.

### Day 3: MOVE
30 minutes of movement. Not a workout — just movement.
Walk. Stretch. Dance. Yoga. Whatever feels good, not punishing.

### Day 4: CONNECT
Reach out to one person who makes you feel like yourself.
No venting about work. Just be human together.

### Day 5: PROTECT
Set one boundary:
- Leave work on time
- Say no to weekend plans
- Turn off notifications after 8pm
- Skip the family WhatsApp group for a day
