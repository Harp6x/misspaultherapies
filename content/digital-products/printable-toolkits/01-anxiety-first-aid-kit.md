## Page 2: Breathing Exercises (Illustrated)

### Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
IN 4 sec → HOLD 4 sec → OUT 4 sec → HOLD 4 sec → Repeat ×4

### 4-7-8 Breathing (for sleep/calm)
IN 4 sec → HOLD 7 sec → OUT 8 sec → Repeat ×4

### Belly Breathing
Hand on chest (still). Hand on belly (rises).
Breathe into belly for 5 slow breaths.

### Physiological Sigh (fastest reset)
Double inhale through nose (sniff-sniff) → Long exhale through mouth
Repeat ×3. Calms in under 30 seconds.

### Body-Based Grounding
☐ Press feet into floor — feel the ground
☐ Hold ice or cold water on wrists
☐ Splash cold water on face
☐ Clench fists for 5 sec → release
☐ Say: "I am [name]. I am in [place]. I am safe."

### Cognitive Grounding
☐ Count backwards from 100 by 7
☐ Name every colour you can see
☐ Describe what you're wearing in detail
☐ Recite song lyrics from memory
