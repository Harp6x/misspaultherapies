### The Bare Minimum Checklist ✓
When you can't do "self-care," do these:
☐ Drink water (one glass)
☐ Eat something (anything)
☐ Brush teeth OR wash face
☐ Open a window (light + air)
☐ Send one text to anyone
☐ Change one piece of clothing

**Every checked box is a win. You're doing more than you think.**

## Page 2: Behavioural Activation Menu

Depression says: "Stay in bed. Nothing will help."
Science says: "Action comes before motivation."

### Tiny Actions (when energy = 0)
☐ Sit up in bed
☐ Open curtains
☐ Listen to one song
☐ Scroll through old happy photos
☐ Pet an animal / hold a soft toy

### Small Actions (when energy = 2-3)
☐ Take a 5-minute walk (just outside your door and back)
☐ Make tea/coffee
☐ Text "thinking of you" to someone
☐ Shower (sit-down shower counts)
☐ Watch one episode of something comforting

### Medium Actions (when energy = 4-5)
☐ Cook a simple meal
☐ Call a friend
☐ 15-minute walk
☐ Tidy one small surface
☐ Journal for 5 minutes

**Pick ONE. Do it. That's enough for today.**

## Page 3: Things to Remember When It's Dark

Read these when you feel nothing will change:

1. **Depression lies.** It tells you things that aren't true.
2. **You have survived every bad day so far.** 100% track record.
3. **This feeling is temporary.** It doesn't feel like it, but it is.
4. **You are not a burden.** You are a person going through something hard.
5. **Doing nothing is okay.** Rest is not the same as giving up.
6. **Small steps count.** Getting out of bed IS an accomplishment.
7. **You don't need to be productive to be worthy.**
8. **Asking for help is strength, not weakness.**
9. **Someone out there is glad you exist.**
10. **You matter. Even when you don't feel like it.**

## Page 5: When to Seek Professional Help

**Talk to a professional if:**
- Low mood lasts more than 2 weeks
- You've stopped doing things you used to enjoy
- Sleep or appetite has changed significantly
- You feel hopeless most of the time
- You're thinking about harming yourself
- You're using substances to cope
