### Gottman's 4 Horsemen (Relationship Killers)

| Horseman | What it looks like | Antidote |
|----------|-------------------|----------|
| **Criticism** | "You ALWAYS..." "You NEVER..." | Gentle startup: "I feel ____ about ____. I need ____." |
| **Contempt** | Eye-rolling, sarcasm, mockery | Build culture of appreciation. Express gratitude daily. |
| **Defensiveness** | "It's not my fault!" "What about YOU?" | Take responsibility: "You're right about that part." |
| **Stonewalling** | Shutting down, silent treatment, walking out | Self-soothe. Say: "I need 20 min. I'll come back." |

## Page 2: The I-Statement Formula + Practice

### Instead of "YOU" statements → Use "I" statements

**Formula:** "I feel [emotion] when [specific behaviour] because [reason]. I need [request]."

### Practice These:

| Situation | YOU statement (avoid) | I statement (use) |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Partner on phone during dinner | "You never pay attention to me!" | "I feel disconnected when we're on phones at dinner. I'd love 20 min of phone-free time." |
| Partner forgot plans | "You don't care about me!" | "I felt hurt when our plans were forgotten. I need us to prioritise our time together." |
| Partner criticised you | "You're so mean!" | "I feel small when I'm criticised in front of others. I need us to address things privately." |

## Page 3: Fair Fight Rules (Print & Put on Fridge)

### OUR RULES FOR CONFLICT

1. ☐ **No name-calling.** Ever.
2. ☐ **No bringing up the past.** Stick to THIS issue.
3. ☐ **No yelling.** If voices rise, take a break.
4. ☐ **Take turns.** One speaks, one listens. Then switch.
5. ☐ **Use "I" statements.** No "you always/never."
6. ☐ **Time-outs are allowed.** Say: "I need 20 minutes." Come back.
7. ☐ **No involving others.** Don't bring parents/friends into our fights.
8. ☐ **Repair matters more than winning.**
9. ☐ **Touch base after.** "Are we okay?"
10. ☐ **Remember: we're on the same team.**

## Page 4: Active Listening Exercise

### The Speaker-Listener Technique

**Speaker holds the "floor" (use an actual object — phone, pen, cushion).**

**Speaker rules:**
- Speak in short chunks (2-3 sentences)
- Use I-statements
- Stick to one topic

**Listener rules:**
- Paraphrase what you heard: "What I hear you saying is ____"
- Validate: "That makes sense because ____"
- Ask: "Is there more?"
- Do NOT problem-solve yet

**Switch roles.**

### Practice This Week
Topic 1 (low-stakes): How was your week?
Topic 2 (medium): Something that's been on your mind
Topic 3 (harder): A recurring friction point

## Page 5: Weekly Connection Rituals + Repair Phrases

### 5 Weekly Rituals to Protect Your Relationship
1. **Daily 6-second kiss** (Gottman research: builds connection)
2. **Weekly date** (even if it's chai on the balcony)
3. **Daily appreciation** — "One thing I appreciated about you today: ____"
4. **Phone-free meals** (at least one per day)
5. **Weekly check-in** — "How are WE doing?"

### Repair Phrases (Use During/After Fights)
- "Can we start over?"
- "I hear you. Tell me more."
- "I'm sorry for my part in this."
- "What do you need from me right now?"
- "I love you, even when we disagree."
- "I didn't mean to hurt you."
- "Let's find a compromise."
- "You matter to me. This conversation matters."
