## Professional Bio

### Short Bio (50 words)
Aishani Paul is a clinical psychologist (M.Phil, RCI Licensed) and founder of Ms Paul Therapies. She specialises in anxiety, relationships, trauma, and burnout through evidence-based online therapy. She creates free mental health resources reaching thousands through Instagram, Gumroad, and her website.

### Medium Bio (100 words)
Aishani Paul is a clinical psychologist with an M.Phil in Clinical Psychology and RCI licensure. She founded Ms Paul Therapies to make quality mental health support accessible, culturally sensitive, and stigma-free — particularly for the Indian audience. Her practice specialises in anxiety, depression, toxic relationships, boundaries, and burnout using CBT, DBT, and trauma-informed approaches. Beyond 1:1 therapy, Aishani creates widely-shared free digital resources — ebooks, toolkits, quizzes, and guided meditations — available on Gumroad, Instagram, and mspaultherapies.com. She's passionate about normalising therapy in India and empowering people with practical, evidence-based tools.

### Long Bio (200 words)
Aishani Paul is a licensed clinical psychologist (M.Phil in Clinical Psychology, RCI registered) and the founder of Ms Paul Therapies — a digital-first therapy practice dedicated to making mental health support accessible, affordable, and culturally relevant for the Indian population.

With expertise spanning anxiety disorders, depression, toxic relationships, attachment patterns, boundaries, burnout, and couples communication, Aishani integrates evidence-based approaches including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), and trauma-informed care into her practice.

Beyond her clinical work, Aishani is a prolific creator of free mental health resources — from mini ebooks and printable toolkits to guided meditation scripts, interactive quizzes, and Notion templates. Her resources have been downloaded thousands of times across Gumroad, Kit, Topmate, and Instagram.

She regularly conducts workshops for corporates, colleges, and community organisations on topics including workplace burnout, emotional intelligence, and therapy demystification. Her content is known for being direct, warm, evidence-based, and deeply relatable.

Aishani's mission is simple: no one should suffer in silence because they don't have access to good information or support. Whether through therapy, teaching, or free resources — she shows up for that mission daily.
