## The Problem

- **42% of Indian employees** report experiencing burnout (Deloitte 2023)
- **80% of Indian workers** say mental health affects their productivity (SHRM India)
- Each employee experiencing mental health issues costs companies an estimated **₹1.5–3 lakh/year** in lost productivity
- Poor mental health leads to absenteeism, presenteeism, high attrition, and low engagement
- Post-pandemic, employee expectations around mental health support have permanently shifted

## The Solution: Ms Paul Therapies Corporate Program

A customisable employee mental health program designed for Indian workplaces — culturally sensitive, evidence-based, and delivered 100% online.
