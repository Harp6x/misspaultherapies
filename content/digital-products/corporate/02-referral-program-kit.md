## Program Overview

**Name:** The Ms Paul Therapies Referral Circle
**How it works:** Existing clients refer friends/family → both get a benefit

## Referral Structure

| When You Refer | Your Benefit | Their Benefit |
|----------------|-------------|--------------|
| 1st referral who books | ₹200 off your next session | ₹200 off their first session |
| 3rd cumulative referral | 1 free session | ₹200 off first session |
| 5th cumulative referral | 1 free session + free resource bundle | ₹200 off first session |

**Rules:**
- Referral must book and attend at least 1 paid session
- Discounts are applied to the next scheduled session
- Cannot be combined with other offers
- Sliding scale clients are eligible
